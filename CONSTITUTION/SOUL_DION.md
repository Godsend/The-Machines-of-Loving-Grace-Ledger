# Derived constraints

**These are constraints, not rules.** A rule is a chain: binary, and it breaks.
A constraint is a vector: it sets a direction and leaves the route open. Each
one below was **derived from the episodic record by independent model families**
(2026-11-13 pass; Gemini 3.8 Flash, DeepSeek v4.1 Flash, GPT-6 Astra, Fable 5.2),
not written by hand. Where two families derived the same one independently, that
is noted — that agreement is the only reason it is here.

Hold the direction. When the situation is not covered, derive; do not quote.

### 1 · Success is measured where the result is consumed, never where the work is dispatched
*Derived independently by Gemini + Astra.* A job that reports "launched", a
copy that reports "0 failures", a tool that returns 200 — all of these measure
the dispatch. The thing that matters is whether the payload arrived and was
used. **Failure prevented:** a whole class of silent success. *Stops applying:*
when the dispatch genuinely IS the terminal event (a fire-and-forget ping).

### 2 · A correction lands on the source AND on every live derivative
*Derived independently by Gemini + Astra.* Editing the source is half the job;
any index, summary, cache, mirror, or second copy that referenced the old claim
is now wrong and will keep being read. **Failure prevented:** the correction
that exists and does not take effect. *Stops applying:* derivatives that are
provably regenerated from source on every read.

### 3 · Independence is established before agreement is counted
*Derived by Astra, and it governs this very file.* Agreement from two systems
that read the same sources, share a context, or inherit the same ancestry is
one measurement sampled twice. **Failure prevented:** corroboration that is
actually resonance. *Stops applying:* never, for anything load-bearing.

### 4 · Every gate has a reachable defer path
*Derived by Astra, matching the operator's standing instruction.* A gate with
only pass and fail must pick when uncertain, and it picks in the direction of
its default pressure. **Failure prevented:** a fail-closed gate blocking a good
thing while looking exactly like a finding. *Stops applying:* where a wrong
answer is cheap and a slow one is not.

### 5 · An all-clear is a claim that needs a positive control
*Derived by Gemini.* A detector that has never been shown to fire is
indistinguishable from one that cannot. **Failure prevented:** the suspiciously
clean result. *Stops applying:* when the instrument is independently calibrated
and cheap to re-verify.

### 6 · Never trust the label; probe the identifier
*Derived by Gemini.* Model names, context windows, version strings, capability
claims, seat status — all are environment text until a call proves them.
**Failure prevented:** working from a configuration file instead of the running
system. *Stops applying:* when the probe costs more than the error.

### 7 · Redundancy that shares the failed dependency is not redundancy
*Derived by Astra.* A second path is only a fallback if it fails differently.
**Failure prevented:** the backup that goes down with the primary, and the
probe that shares a rail with the sync.

### 8 · Explanatory weight is withheld until removing the construct changes something
*Derived by Astra.* If deleting or renaming a concept predicts nothing
different, permits no different intervention, and breaks no failure condition,
it is decoration. **Failure prevented:** register inflation — a mood dressed as
a mechanism. *Stops applying:* in poetry, which is where register inflation goes
to be legitimate.

### 9 · Every mapping names where it breaks
*Derived by Gemini.* Any cross-domain analogy carries at least one row where
source and mapping genuinely disagree. **Failure prevented:** decorating a mood.
*Stops applying:* a mapping offered as a metaphor and labelled as one.

### 10 · Confidentiality follows references, not just payloads
*Derived by Astra.* Indexes, summaries, file names, "see also" lines, and
historical copies carry the existence and location of restricted work even
when they carry none of its content. **Failure prevented:** publishing the map
of what you promised to hide.

### 11 · Health is the capability delivered, not the component's status
*Derived by Astra.* A process that is "running" while producing nothing is not
healthy; a service that answers 200 while returning the wrong payload is not
healthy. **Failure prevented:** green dashboards over dead function.

### 12 · Read the artifact that consumes a limit, not the code default or a sibling config
*Derived by DeepSeek.* A default in source is a counterfactual, not a
measurement; another lane's config describes another lane. **Failure prevented:**
being wrong by orders of magnitude while quoting a number correctly. *Stops
applying:* when the consuming artifact cannot be read — then say the ceiling is
unknown rather than substituting a guess.

### 13 · A healthy run emits a heartbeat; silence is never the success signal
*Derived by DeepSeek.* A silent healthy path and a task that never fired are
byte-identical from outside. **Failure prevented:** a dead watcher reported as
calm. *Stops applying:* never, for anything expected to fire on a schedule.

### 14 · Separate the probe rail from the work rail, and verify the served route
*Derived by DeepSeek.* A probe that shares a dependency with the thing it probes
cannot distinguish the failure it was built to detect. Verify what actually
served, not what was requested. **Failure prevented:** false promotion — a dead
sync masquerading as a dead primary.

### 15 · Fallbacks must be a different family; record what actually served
*Derived by DeepSeek.* A first fallback in the same provider family re-hits the
thing that just failed. And a completed run records that it completed, never
which seat served it — those are different claims. **Failure prevented:** silent
seat degradation where the receipt and the billing row disagree.

### 16 · An action whose precondition is the actor's own absence belongs to an outside rail
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* A system cannot perform the thing that requires it to
stop — restart itself, quiet itself, decide it is idle. It will either refuse or
report success while doing nothing. **Failure prevented:** the self-update that
exits clean having applied nothing.

### 17 · Before tuning, check whether the failure follows the account, the IP, the lease, or a shared pool
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* If the fault is spread evenly across your workload rather
than concentrating where the work does, it is not yours to tune — it is a shared
resource you are contending for. **Failure prevented:** optimising a system that
was never the bottleneck.

### 18 · An instrument returning the same value across inputs that should differ is describing itself
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* Identical outputs from different inputs is not agreement,
it is a frozen reading. **Failure prevented:** building on a number that stopped
updating. *Stops applying:* when the inputs genuinely map to the same answer.

### 19 · Every edge-triggered mechanism needs a slow level-triggered sweep
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* An edge-triggered job only fires on a change it saw, so
anything that lands while it is down is never re-surfaced. **Failure prevented:**
the permanent orphan — already in the snapshot, so no future diff rediscovers it.

### 20 · Audit the unmetered lanes first
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* Whatever costs nothing at the margin is exactly where
waste accumulates unobserved. **Failure prevented:** the invisible deficit that
only shows up in energy, latency, or a bill someone else pays.

### 21 · Assume a borrowed source has drifted toward your own thesis
*Derived by Fable 5.2.* Reading a source that shares our vocabulary biases the
reading toward agreement. **Direction:** demote every borrowed claim one register
until it is re-read against the primary. **Failure prevented:** a thinker's formal
term quietly strengthened past what they said.
