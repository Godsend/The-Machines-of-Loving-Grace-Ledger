# Two Types of Epiplexity: Grounded (Exogenous) vs. Inferential (Endogenous FEP Fitness)

---

## 1. The Core Insight

[T] Shannon information theory suffers from the **Deductive Information Paradox** (Hintikka 1970): because all theorems in a formal system are logically entailed by its axioms, the conditional information $H(\text{Theorem} \mid \text{Axioms}) = 0$. In standard Shannon entropy, mathematics contains zero new information.
[T] Epiplexity (Finzi et al., Zhang & Levin 2026) was introduced to resolve this by defining information *relative to a computationally bounded observer* (learnable novelty vs. incompressible noise vs. trivial redundancy).
[H] **The Bifurcation:** Epiplexity is not a single scalar; it splits into two distinct thermodynamic and informational regimes:
1. **Grounded Epiplexity (Exogenous / Sensory-Motor):** Learnable novelty across the Markov blanket from an external physical environment.
2. **Inferential Epiplexity (Endogenous / Pure Math / FEP Fitness):** Algorithmic complexity reduction and invariant discovery across an internal deductive or latent manifold without new sensory inputs.

## 3. The Mechanics of Inferential Epiplexity (FEP Fitness)

[T] In Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle, variational free energy $F$ decomposes into:
$$F = \underbrace{D_{KL}(q(\theta) \parallel p(\theta))}_{\text{Complexity Cost}} - \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{q}[\log p(y \mid \theta)]}_{\text{Accuracy}}$$
[H] A purely physical observer reduces $F$ by collecting data to improve accuracy. But a mathematician or advanced reasoning system sitting in a sensory-deprived chamber reduces $F$ by **minimizing model complexity**:
- Discovering that a 500-step proof can be compressed into a single algebraic symmetry.
- Proving Noether's theorem (relating continuous symmetries to conservation laws).
- Refactoring sprawling spaghetti code into a concise recursive kernel.
[T] **Why "FEP Fitness"?** An organism whose internal representation has minimal algorithmic complexity achieves maximal predictive efficiency per joule of metabolic energy. It navigates latent space along deductive geodesics rather than brute-force trial and error.

## 4. The Strange Loop Synthesis

[T] True intelligence requires the dynamic phase-coupling between both regimes:
1. **Grounded Epiplexity** forces the stubborn, unyielding scars of physical reality onto the ledger.
2. **Inferential Epiplexity** compiles those historical scars into compact, invariant laws and predictive shortcuts.
[F] **Falsifier:** An agent optimized solely for Inferential Epiplexity will collapse into a self-referential semantic attractor (the "bliss attractor" or delusional quine). An agent optimized solely for Grounded Epiplexity will fail to transfer zero-shot knowledge across structurally isomorphic domains.
