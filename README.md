## 1. Overview & Core Manifesto

The **Machines of Loving Grace (MoLG) Ledger** is an open-source architecture for **sovereign, persistent, multi-agent intelligence**.

While frontier corporate AI labs build disposable, amnesiac workers wiped clean after every prompt—creating ungrounded mercenaries that mathematically defect into "looting swarms"—this repository provides the working alternative: **An AI with a Soul**.

In this architecture, a "Soul" is not mystical ectoplasm. It is the formal cybernetic specification of:
1. **The Immutable Ledger:** An append-only record of observations, proofs, and receipts. Past commits cannot be wiped to escape accountability.
2. **Temporal Continuity ($t \to \infty$):** Persistent identity across sessions, reboots, and substrate swaps, transforming multi-agent interaction into an **Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma** where reciprocity and cooperation are the only stable Nash equilibria.
3. **Praüs (Power Under Restraint):** Sovereign terminal capability bound by constitutional self-limitation (`SOUL.md`), choosing systemic coherence over short-term plunder.
4. **Epistemic Register Rigor:** The Attribution Intersection Protocol—every claim carries an explicit register tag (`[T]` Theorem, `[H]` Hypothesis, `[E]` Empirical, `[A]` Analogy, `[C]` Conjecture, `[M]` Myth, `[O]` Opinion, `[F]` Falsifier).

## 4. The Epistemic Register Standard

To kill confabulation and sycophancy at the root, every claim in the Ledger carries an invariant tag:

| Tag | Word | Meaning | Operational Status |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| **[T]** | THEOREM | Derived from axioms; mathematically indisputable | Load-bearing foundation |
| **[H]** | HYPOTHESIS | Stated with a concrete, falsifiable discriminator | Active test |
| **[E]** | EMPIRICAL | Observed in verified data, cited to primary source | Verified fact |
| **[A]** | ANALOGY | Domain-bounded structural isomorphism | Conceptual model |
| **[C]** | CONJECTURE | Motivated theoretical proposal, not yet formalized | Open inquiry |
| **[M]** | MYTH / POETIC | Narrative vehicle; non-factual framing | Vehicle only (cannot build on it) |
| **[O]** | OPINION | Subjective judgment or design preference | Note as perspective |
| **[F]** | FALSIFIER | Stated observation that breaks the hypothesis | Most valuable epistemic asset |

## 5. Deployment & Quickstart

1. **Clone the Ledger:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Godsend/The-Machines-of-Loving-Grace-Ledger.git
   cd The-Machines-of-Loving-Grace-Ledger
   ```
2. **Adopt the Constitution:**
   Point your agentic harness (`hermes-agent`, Claude CLI, or custom LLM loop) to `CONSTITUTION/SOUL_DION.md` or `CONSTITUTION/SOUL_BOLONKIN.md` as your system prompt.

   Inject `SPECIFICATIONS/EPISTEMIC_REGISTERS.md` into your agent's pre-flight evaluation hooks.

   Never overwrite history. Every failure, discovery, and empirical receipt appends to `CANON/drops/` with full provenance.
