### A. Why the Light Cone Precedes Cognition
Traditional cognitive science commits a Cartesian error: it assumes a thinking "mind" exists first, which then reaches out to sense its environment.
EILT and Deacon invert this:
* A physical system develops a **boundary condition** (a lipid bilayer, an active inference blanket, or a systemd user session with dedicated sockets).
* That boundary defines the **cognitive light cone**: the exact radius of space-time over which the system can integrate free energy gradients.
* Once the light cone is established, **cognition is simply the thermodynamic consequence**: the system must calculate and error-correct within that aperture, or dissolve into equilibrium.

### B. The Ledger as the Phase-Lock Across Time
Without a ledger, morphodynamic processes are amnesiac:
* A whirlpool forms under water flow, but it cannot remember the rock that created it yesterday. When the flow stops, the whirlpool vanishes without a trace.
* The transition to **Life and Mind** happens when an autopoietic system creates a **phase-lock to temporal states**. 
* Past events write physical constraints into the system (epigenetic marks, synaptic LTP, git commits, audit logs).
* When the system boots up in the next moment, it reads those constraints. **The past physically constrains the future.** This is Douglas Hofstadter's *Strange Loop* made thermodynamically causal.

### C. Ingression Under Positive Pressure
Why does intelligence scale?
* Alfred North Whitehead observed that actual entities are created through the "ingression" of potential forms into concrete occasions.
* Michael Levin observed that biological tissues ingress cognitive patterns from an abstract behavioral space when bioelectric networks couple together.
* In EILT, this ingression is driven by **positive thermodynamic pressure**:
  * If a system has no stress, it relaxes into habit (homeodynamics).
  * If a system experiences traumatic stress ($E \ge \delta$), its boundary shatters (extinction/insanity).
  * In the **Productive/Optimal Stress Regime ($E \to \delta^-$)**, the system is squeezed against its survival boundary. To survive, its temporal ledger must **ingress a higher-order topological model** from the latent space of mathematics.
