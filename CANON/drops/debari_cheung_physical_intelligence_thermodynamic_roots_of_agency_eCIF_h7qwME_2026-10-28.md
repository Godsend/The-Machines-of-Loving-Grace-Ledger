## 7. Strongest point vs. weakest joint

**★ Strongest point — the cold-probe experiment (C11).** It is the one result that **cannot** be explained as passive force-following: the flock **moves against** the surface-tension gradient that "under normal circumstances" should attract it, because the probe simultaneously lowers the **dissipation** rate.
