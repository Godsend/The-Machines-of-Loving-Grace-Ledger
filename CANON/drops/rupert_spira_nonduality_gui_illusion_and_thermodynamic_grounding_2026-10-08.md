### B. Berkeley’s Idealist Trap (Equivocation on Epistemic Access)

> *"Is there anything to your current experience other than the knowing of it? ... If you admit that knowing is all you ever experience, how do we know there is anything other than knowing? We don't."* ([50:17–50:95])

This is George Berkeley's classical *Master Argument*, and it is a pure equivocation:
- **Tautological Epistemic Claim:** An agent can only *experience* that which enters its experiential apparatus. (True, by definition of the word "experience").

By Spira’s logic, the Geiger counter should conclude that uranium does not exist, and that only "clicking" is fundamental to the universe.
