## 2. Delta (new to canon)

- **D1 [C] Continuity as the null hypothesis.** He says that if you take evolution and development seriously, *"the null hypothesis is continuity. If someone wants to argue for emergent phase transitions, that is sharp categories, we're going to need really strong evidence for it"* (L44–48), and that there is *"no magic bright line that turns chemistry into mind"* (L40). Earlier filings had "no bright line" as an opinion. This is the first canon statement of it as a **burden-of-proof rule**. It is a methodological prior, not a finding, and no test is given for what "really strong evidence" of a phase transition would look like (see F-NB1).
- **D2 [O] The parity argument for consciousness in body structures, with a criteria list.** This is the second of his three stated claims (L32–36): *"for the same reasons you associate consciousness with complex brains … for those exact same reasons, you need to consider very seriously the possibility of consciousness in many body structures."* He names the criteria (L57–66): (1) **behaviour**, meaning problem-solving, communication, goals, and the usefulness of taking the system's perspective; (2) **evolutionary kinship**; (3) **conserved mechanisms**; (4) **causal emergence and integration measures**; plus a "fifth one … still investigating" (L57–59). He says he spent a whole talk on this "last year" (L36), but **that talk is not identified or in canon**. Here he only names the criteria and does not argue them. The list itself is new to canon (0 hits for "evolutionary kinship" or "conserved mechanism").
- **D3 [CONTRA, escalation of TaT F1].** The 10-16 drop flagged *"random networks already do this … free gift from math"* as inverting his own paper. This talk goes further: random networks are *"**almost optimal** for this ratchet"* (L99–100). It also adds an **origin-of-life application**: a preprint, *"what this means in an actual model of origin of life"*, set *"prior to Darwinian selection … no replicators"* (L107–110). The preprint is **unresolved** and I will not name it. Pigozzi, Goldstein & Levin 2025 (*Commun Biol* 8:1027) still reports biological networks at **+128%** versus random networks at **+56%**, with the property *"favored by evolution."* "Almost optimal" is **not supported** by the published record. Cite the paper, not the talk.
- **D4 [E] Anthrobot specifics.** He gives *">9,000 differentially expressed genes … half the genome"* (L146–147), *"four specific behaviors"* in a transition-probability ethogram (L147–148), and a reminder that the cells are 100% normal *Homo sapiens* with no engineering (L157–158). **Checked at abstract level:** Gumuskaya et al. 2025, *Adv Sci* 12(31):e2409330 (doi 10.1002/advs.202409330) reports a *"massive remodeling of gene expression,"* a shift toward evolutionarily ancient genes, and reduced epigenetic age. **The 9,000 count is not in the abstract and was not checked.** Gumuskaya et al. 2024, *Adv Sci* 11(4):e2303575 reports morphotypes *"each related to a distinct movement type"* and repair of neural scratches. **The number four is not in the abstract and was not checked.** COI on both papers: Astonishing Labs, which Levin co-founded. "Half the genome" is loose: 9,000 is roughly 45% of about 20k protein-coding genes, and the denominator was not stated.
- **D5 [O] His consciousness opinions, stated as such** (L303–313), are the clearest version in canon:
  - consciousness *"isn't made"*;
  - it is *not "a late product of evolution"*;
  - it is *not "a property of matter even"*, which he pitches as *"weirder than … consciousness is a property of all matter"* (L29), i.e. explicitly **not panpsychism**;
  - *"consciousness is a property of patterns … the perspective of patterns like us ingressing through physical space"*;
  - he does not know whether patterns experience anything outside ingression (L313–314).
  
  "Property of patterns" has 0 prior canon hits. The unconscious-process scepticism repeats Khemani D1, but here it carries a sharper argument: **"we simply have no negative control"** (L310–311), since all third-person data is calibrated on the verbal "language user within the body" (L308–310).
- **D6 [O] Adequacy conditions for a theory of consciousness** (L314–326), new to canon:
  - **(a)** It should output **protocols**, not predictions of behaviour or physiology (*"then they're theories of behavior and physiology"*). The protocol should *"put you in first person in the same … conscious state as some other system"*, which means **mind-sharing devices**.
  - **(b)** It should **"make the Humean bridge"** from the description of a system to the ethical imperatives toward it, the is→ought step.
  
  The protocols point is not new for Levin outside canon. He says the same thing in a Fields/Solms/Levin discussion transcript (thoughtforms-life.aipodcast.ing) and in a Closer To Truth summary. It is new to **this** canon.
- **D7 [C/E-listed] Programme items named.** These are:
  - *"gene regulatory networks playing Atari games"* as communication interfaces (L276). A candidate paper is arXiv:2605.16321, "Language Game: Talking to Non-Human Systems": GRN dynamics frozen as an RL policy core, with games as a "lingua franca". **The abstract matches, but the authors and Levin's involvement were not verified, so it is linked, not attributed.**
  - a collaboration with **Lauren Ross** (philosopher of causation) on non-energetic, pattern-to-physics causation (L266). This is unpublished, and Ross is new to canon.
  - whether fully synthetic embodiments *"pull down some of the same patterns as biological media … or a completely separate set"* (L290–292). This is a crisp, open empirical question; the phrase "pulling down" is already in the Agüera digest L93.
  - *"what is this fourth thing"* beyond design, selection and training (L288).

## 3. Friction (Friction Rule)

**★ FR-1 [CRITICAL_DIVERGENCE] vs `dual_aspect_monism_bare_commit_consciousness_and_cycle_inheritance_2026-10-08.md` (house [HYP]: consciousness = the bare commit).** The 10-16 drop already pushed on the bare-commit account from one side: a continuum against a sharp event. This talk pushes from a **different side**: the **negative control**. The bare-commit [HYP] is only falsifiable if some processes in the system *are not* commits, i.e. are unconscious, and we can tell which. Levin's D5 argument says there is **no negative control for unconsciousness anywhere**, because every "no experience" datum is a verbal report from one subsystem. If he is right, nothing in the system can ever be shown to be a non-commit, so the bare-commit account has no observable contrast class and drops from [H] to [C]. He also says consciousness *"isn't made"*, while the bare-commit account makes consciousness something produced each commit, a made event. **What the house must answer:** name an observable that distinguishes a commit from a non-commit without relying on verbal or self-report of the agent's own index. If that can't be done, the bare-commit claim inherits Levin's objection. (Logged as F-NB2.)

**FR-2 [CRITICAL_DIVERGENCE] vs `geoffrey_lee_wont_know_ai_conscious_futurology_2026-10-16.md`.** Lee: for intermediate systems there may be **no fact of the matter**, and no empirical lever settles it. Levin's D6(a) says the opposite: a good theory outputs a **protocol** that would let you be in the other system's state, so the question becomes **settleable by technology**. These two positions cannot both be right about any given intermediate system. On top of that, Levin's D1 (continuity as the null hypothesis) plus D5 (no unconscious processes) amounts to a **default-yes** prior, while Lee's deflationary pluralism is **default-indeterminate**. Neither side gives a discriminating test. Levin admits *"I don't know if we can ever know what it's like to be them"* (L297–298), which **concedes Lee's epistemic point while keeping the realist one**. The tension is left open.

**FR-3 [CONTRA, internal to Levin, minor].** He opens with *"I don't claim to have any kind of a new theory of consciousness"* (L32) and closes by setting conditions any such theory must meet, which his own view doesn't meet: he has no protocol and no is→ought output. He also says unconscious processes are probably incoherent (L305–308). The claims are hedged as opinion, which is fair, but **D5 plus D1 together are a substantive metaphysics**, not "no strong claims". Tag them [O]. Do not cite them as [C] or above.

**Carried forward unchanged:** the LK A1 gap (ingression vs our poor modelling) and TaT F2 (agency grades ≠ subjecthood). This talk shows **no discriminating experiment** either. The D2 criteria are an **argument from analogy**. The split in the GFS octopus digest (*nested autonomy without nested subjecthood*) still applies: criteria (1) and (4), behaviour and integration, can be met by sub-organismal agents without settling subjecthood. Criteria (2) and (3), kinship and conserved mechanisms, are inherited *by construction* by any body structure of a conscious organism, so they cannot discriminate the very cases at issue.

## 4. Falsifiers

- [F] **F-NB1 (null-hypothesis continuity).** If a well-characterised developmental or learning system shows a **true discontinuity** in a cognitive measure, meaning a non-smooth jump in causal emergence or learning capacity across a parameter that survives choice of metric (cf. the Schaeffer-2023 metric-artefact control already filed in `ingression_epiplexity_register_review_2026-09-24.md`), then D1's prior is defeated for that system. Conversely, if every apparent jump dissolves under continuous metrics, D1 gains support.
- [F] **F-NB2 (house obligation, from FR-1).** The bare-commit [HYP] must name a non-self-report observable that separates commit from non-commit states. If it can't by the next bare-commit revision, downgrade it [H]→[C] and record Levin's negative-control objection as the reason.
- [F] **F-NB3 (synthetic vs biological ingression, D7).** Levin's own question can be tested. If AI or robot embodiments and biological constructs with matched task interfaces show **statistically distinct** unbidden competencies (side quests), the "same space" picture loses its parsimony. If they converge, it gains. Watch for the lab paper.
- [F] **F-TT2 (inherited, now sharper).** "Almost optimal" (D3) is falsified on the current published record unless a topology-matched random ensemble matches or beats biological networks on training-driven causal-emergence gain.
