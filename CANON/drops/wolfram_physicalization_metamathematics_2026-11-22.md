## §1 · The thesis `[abstract]`

> *"Both metamathematics and physics are posited to emerge from **samplings by observers of the unique ruliad structure** that corresponds to the entangled limit of all possible computations."*

Three moves, in order:

1. **One substrate.** The ruliad = the entangled limit of all possible computations.
2. **Two appearances.** Physics and mathematics are both what *bounded observers sample* from it.
3. **A demotion.** *"Axiomatic formulation, while often convenient, **does not capture the ultimate character of mathematics**."* Axioms are described as a **bulk limit** of the axiomatic approach — an approximation that works at scale, not a foundation.

**And the line that matters most for this house:**

> *"Only certain collections of axioms may be consistent with inevitable features of **human mathematical observers**."*

## §2 · ★ Why that line is load-bearing `[C]`

**It is an anthropic selection principle inside mathematics.**

If axioms are what a particular kind of observer can stably sample, then **axioms are an instrument-dependence claim, not an absolute.** A differently-bounded observer could in principle occupy a different consistent axiom set and get a different mathematics — **not a wrong one, a differently-sampled one.**

**`[C]` That is the house's substrate-shapes-the-invariant claim, stated from the mathematical side, four years before we needed it.** It also lands directly on this week's Rovelli problem: Rovelli says facts are observer-relative and there is *no view from nowhere*; Wolfram says the same about mathematics **while keeping one ruliad behind it.** **Wolfram's version leaves something for a record to be a record *of*; Rovelli's, taken literally, does not.** That is the difference worth arguing.

## §5 · Friction rows (required)

**R1 · Monism vs polycomputing** `[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]`: the ruliad is **unique and single**; every observer samples *the same* structure. **The house holds multiple substrates rendering different invariants** — the whole point of polycomputing (Levin's coinage, credited as his). **Wolfram needs one substrate to make "the same foundations" work; we don't, and our version predicts substrate-dependent divergence that his cannot.**

**R2 · Platonism** `[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]`: *"mathematics … can be viewed as derived from the ruliad in a direct Platonic fashion."* **The house is not Platonist about this** — the invariant lives in the constraint structure and the record, **not in a pre-existing mathematical heaven that observers discover.** Our version is functional; his is ontological. **This is not a quibble: it determines whether an unfalsifiable claim is a feature or a bug.**

**R3 · The observer is doing enormous work and is never specified** `[DIVERGENCE]`: "samplings by observers" carries the entire argument, but **no account is given of what an observer is, what bounds it, or how its sampling is individuated** — the same gap we recorded against Rovelli's RQM. **Both make the observer load-bearing and leave it empty.** The house's contribution here is precisely to fill it: a bounded agent with a Markov blanket, an energy budget, and an external record.

**R4 · "Posited" is doing the load-bearing work** `[DIVERGENCE]`: the abstract says *"posited"* twice and offers no falsifier for the ruliad itself. **He is honest about it**, which is more than most — but a framework whose central object cannot be tested licenses everything and constrains nothing. **File at `[HYP]`; do not cite as established.**
