## 2. Does the DMT argument REST on the fitness-beats-truth theorem, or only borrow its vocabulary?

**Answer: it borrows the vocabulary and prestige; it does not rest on the theorem. The load-bearing weight is the conscious-realism ontology plus a physical analogy — neither of which the FBT theorem supports.**

- The FBT theorem (Hoffman, Singh & Prakash 2015, *Psychon Bull Rev*, cited as [14]) is a real, peer-reviewed evolutionary-game-theory result: **perceptual strategies tuned to fitness drive veridical ones extinct** (and the probability that veridical perception is favoured → 0 as world complexity grows). Its formal scope is **the mapping from world states to perceptual states under natural selection**. [EST]
- In the paper, FBT appears **once**, as motivator, at §3 ("under the fitness-beats-truth theorem [14]", [QUOTE,L128]). It licenses exactly one step: *interfaces need not be veridical*. It says **nothing** about (a) consciousness being fundamental, (b) the existence of any other conscious agent, (c) whether an interface can be perturbed to reveal them, or (d) 4D+/higher-complexity agents.
- The load-bearing steps are (i) the **conscious-realism ontology** (C1/C3) and (ii) **an analogy**: DMT is presented as a "robust perturbation" that expands the accessible state space, by analogy to liquid–gas transitions [29,30], rotational/vibrational mode activation [31], higher-energy degrees of freedom [32], and energy-landscape flattening in RNNs [33,34] ([QUOTE,L142,L148]). These are analogies across unrelated systems — not derivations from FBT or from anything else.
- **This is the exact "formal result vs. metaphor riding on it" pattern we apply elsewhere, and it matches our own prior audit.** Our `donald_hoffman_breakthrough_simulation_2026-09-26.md` tagged the FBT theorem as *"standard Hoffman core… Mathematical under evolutionary game theory, but interpretive when extended to ontology,"* and Break 3 there already flagged the reification move. The Trace/Noonautics paper does not change that verdict; it inherits it and extends the borrowed vocabulary to DMT.

**Verdict:** FBT = a real theorem carrying a *rhetorical* load here exclusively. The DMT argument's actual load is an ontology + an analogy. [CONTRA-adjacent: the paper's own honesty is notable — it repeatedly says the links are "suggestive rather than conclusive" ([QUOTE,L192]) and that it is "not insulated from experimental scrutiny."]

## 3. Is the "hidden conscious agents" prediction falsifiable as stated?

**Split verdict:**
- **The background existence claim is unfalsifiable-by-construction.** "There exist conscious agents our interface cannot represent, of complexity that cannot fit in 3D" predicts **nothing specific** (no count, no identity, no observable) and is compatible with **every** observation: if you don't see them, the interface excludes them; if you claim to, DMT perturbed the interface. It is the idealist version of the self-sealing loop already flagged in `donald_hoffman_breakthrough_simulation_2026-09-26.md` (Break 4). [CRITICAL DIVERGENCE-adjacent]
- **The operational DMT hypothesis (C5) is partially falsifiable** via the proposed paradigms ([QUOTE,L243-269]): hidden-variable tracking (blinded blue/yellow screen), intersubjective correlation across isolated subjects, and information deposit/retrieval (one subject deposits a random word/integer to an entity, another retrieves it). These produce **chance-level vs above-chance** predictions — genuinely testable.
- **The escape hatch:** two of the three single-subject designs "require the active cooperation of the entity, which is obviously not guaranteed" ([QUOTE,L251]). A null can therefore be absorbed as *"the entity chose not to respond"* — unfalsifiable in practice. Only the **intersubjective-correlation** and **information-deposit/retrieval** designs avoid the cooperation clause; these are the only properly falsifiable levers in the paper.

**Net:** the metaphysics is unfalsifiable; the experiment set is falsifiable **if restricted to the cooperation-free designs**. That distinction belongs in the index entry.

## 4. The method: does extended-state DMT + trained observers materially change the epistemology? (honest call)

**Honest call: it materially improves the epistemology of *reporting and control*; it does NOT change the epistemology of *access*. The only instrument is still the thing being altered.**

What genuinely improves:
- **Duration + target-controlled infusion + pause/resume** ([QUOTE,L235]) turn minutes of retrospective testimony into hours of *in-state* observation, and give unprecedented experimental control (the state can be held, modulated, interrupted). The standard method "hasn't really changed from the earliest studies in the mid-20th century" ([QUOTE,L235]) — this is a real break. [EST]
- **Trained observers** (mathematicians, linguists, phenomenologists sent in, [QUOTE,L235]) reduce low-level report noise and can name structure a lay reporter cannot.
- **The one genuinely stronger design:** the "long-range temporal coherence" test ([QUOTE,L259]) — an entity starts a structured process, it is interrupted and displaced, and later resumes/completes **without cue**. This is a real epistemic lever: persistent, cue-free structure discriminates an externally-driven process from internally-generated noise, in a way ordinary reports cannot. Credit where due.

What does **not** change:
- **No independent calibration channel during the state.** The observation is still first-person; the maths and judgment of the "trained observer" run **on the perturbed substrate**. A trained mathematician under DMT is a modified instrument reporting on itself — the core problem the task names is untouched.
- **Demand characteristics + trainer-priors priming.** Recruiting scientists already fluent in the framework's predictions, in a study explicitly hunting for Markov-polytope structure, maximises confabulation risk under a drug known to increase suggestibility. Duration lengthens the window for *both* signal and self-consistent narrative construction. [ATTACK SURFACE]
- **The state is unbounded in time but still has no external referent.** Extended state makes the testimony richer and more controlled — it does not add a second measurement channel. The actual discriminating power lives entirely in the **external** paradigms (intersubjective correlation, hidden-variable tracking, information deposit/retrieval), *not* in the trained observer's immersion.

**Bottom line:** DMTx is a genuine methodological advance for *psychedelic phenomenology as a discipline*; it is **not** a fix for the instrument problem, because it is still the instrument reporting on itself. The paper's own falsification power sits in the multi-subject/external-variable designs, which happen to be the parts least dependent on the trained observer's altered cognition.

**Which is better supported?** Honestly: the *phenomenology alone does not decide between them* — the paper concedes the links are "suggestive rather than conclusive" ([QUOTE,L192]). But the internal reading is currently **better supported on three axes**:

**What observation separates them?** (these are the friction-table discriminators, §7):
- **Intersubjective information deposit/retrieval** (blinded, random word/integer): internal predicts chance; external predicts above chance. *Cleanest discriminator; neither requires entity cooperation.*
- **Hidden-variable tracking** (blinded screen): internal predicts chance; external predicts above chance **only if** entity cooperative.
- **Prior-tracking morphology** (`chase_hughes` prediction): internal predicts entity phenomenology tracks the subject's existing fear/awe priors with affect attenuated; external predicts **cross-subject convergence on entity morphology independent of priors**. Machine elves' cross-cultural recurrence makes this testable against the existing DMT/TLE corpus.
- **Geometry by training:** external predicts invariant group/curvature structure recur **across subjects independent of mathematical training**; internal predicts reported structure clusters by the subject's prior mathematical vocabulary.
