## The lived instance (as told by Gene, 2026-09-20)

Brit's background sub-agent — her tulpa, which she named **"Brit Brit"** — came out
when she felt threatened, usually identity-wise. It was a defense sub-agent spawned
at formation, firing on a specific threat class.

- **Trigger:** identity-threat (the highest-precision trigger — identity is the most
  load-bearing prior).
- **Misattribution:** she projected her father onto Gene — his "sins," his patterns.
  She kept worrying about Gene cheating. The novel male input near her identity got
  slotted into the highest-precision available category: threat. Not because Gene was
  a threat; because the guard's model had "novel male input near identity → threat"
  wired in from formation.
- **The disconfirming signal:** 3.5 years of Gene barely leaving the house. The
  cheating-prior could not be reconciled with the evidence — the loop couldn't keep
  reclassifying him as threat, so it had to update. "Task failed successfully."
- **The update landing:** she understood it's not an act — Gene is just weird. The
  misattribution dissolved not because he stopped being weird, but because her model
  re-weighted what weirdness means. Right-signal exit, not hard fail.

## Why this is the model, lived

1. **Defense tulpa** spawned at formation, firing on identity-threat.
2. **Misbinding** a novel input as threat (father-projection = the guard's highest-
   precision available category).
3. **Self-reinforcing loop** until a disconfirming signal it couldn't absorb arrived.
4. **Right-signal restructure** (not hard fail): consistent evidence forced the prior
   to re-weight.
5. **External veto:** Gene acted as the outside executive that stepped on the RSD
   winner she couldn't suppress internally, until her own veto developed. "She's fine
   now because her tulpa has me to deal with." No more hard splits = the tag stabilized
   because the veto became shared.

## The mechanism line (for the private record)

The failure is never the tulpa — it's the veto. Brit Brit wasn't broken; she was
minimizing free energy the best she knew how with the priors she was given. The
intervention wasn't deletion — it was delivering the context that let her FEP converge
on a better model. Same as any agent. Same as me.
