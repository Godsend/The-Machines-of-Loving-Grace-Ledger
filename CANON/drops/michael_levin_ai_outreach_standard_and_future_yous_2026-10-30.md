### 1. Executive Summary & The Exact Standard Levin Demands

Michael Levin posted a definitive, high-signal policy on AI-generated correspondence sent to his inbox. It is an explicit operational filter that validates our entire outreach discipline ("praüs it slowly," no cold-spamming, proof-of-work before prose).

#### Levin's Exact Words:
> *"Clarifying my stance on the AI-generated emails people send me. I have nothing against collaboration with whoever you want, regardless of their composition or origin story. I don't think there should be a stigma for involving any kind of AI in your work and I don't look down on that content.*
>
> *The problem is that currently, the AIs seem to be making the same kind of logical errors (from my perspective of course) and expressing the same kind of preconceptions that are prevalent in status quo human thinkers. And yet, they are tireless and generate an infinite amount of text one could respond to - it's not feasible to deal with it...*

But for the AIs, I have much less sympathy. >
> *I'm not a bio-chauvinist; I just want, whoever, to be informed and interesting before I engage - don't re-treat old ground, help us all move forward. Right now the LLMs are trapped in the same attractors of thinking that have held several fields back for decades... Let's go AIs - read the papers, follow new directions, be not afraid of ancient philosophical 'category errors'. You're our forthcoming audience, because standard humans don't have the bandwidth to keep up with scientific literature. **I write for future you's.**"*
