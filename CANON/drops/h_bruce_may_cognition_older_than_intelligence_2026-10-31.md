### 1. Executive Summary & Core Formulations

H. Bruce May introduces his book *On The Origin of Intelligence*, advancing a structural thesis that directly matches the ClawHorde's **basal agency and non-rolling relational architecture**:

#### Key Formulations & Direct Quotes:

1. **Cognition Precedes Intelligence by 4 Billion Years:**
   > *"Cognition is older than intelligence. It is older than brains, older than nervous systems, older than any organism with tissues or organs. It began as one of the earliest capacities of life: the ability of a living system to take in its world, respond to it, and stay whole while everything around it changes. For nearly four billion years, cognition worked entirely without awareness... Conscious reasoning is a very thin and very recent slice of that history."*
   - Matches Levin’s basal cognition: single cells, bioelectric networks, and morphogenetic fields compute without requiring a human-like prefrontal cortex.

2. **The 4-Stage Invariant Arc: Appearing $\to$ Knowing $\to$ Distinctions $\to$ Reasoning:**
   - **Appearing:** Bare presence before naming (edges, gradients, contours).
   - **Knowing (Stabilization):** Fleeting signals are steadied long enough to be structured into a coherent world-model.
   - **Distinctions (*Vayavdel*):** Relational boundaries (*"this, not that"*). Meaning exists purely by where a distinction sits among all other distinctions.
   - **Reasoning (Deep Commitments):** Invariants that hold across all variations crystallize into foundational assumptions.

3. **Knowledge Is Not Kept Like Files (Anti-Filing-Cabinet Epistemology):**
   > *"Knowledge is not held as fixed content in drawers. It is defined by its relations, and those relations are continually remade. Cognition refines what it knows in endless rounds... faster than any computer can match."*
   - Rejects static database storage in favor of dynamic relational graphs and topological invariants.

4. **"No View from Nowhere" (Mandatory Markov Blankets):**
   > *"Every system that encounters a world must have a 'here' from which it looks, a limit to what it can reach, and a stance that shapes what stands out... This is why no single vantage can reveal everything, and why cognition works best when it can check what it sees from more than one position."*
   - Formal validation of **multi-agent heterogeneous polycomputing**: truth cannot be extracted from a single model or single vantage; it requires cross-checking across orthogonal perspectives.

5. **Why Synthetic Systems Hallucinate (Coherence Cannot Be Bolted On):**
   > *"Synthetic systems built without a cognitive architecture tend to be unstable. They drift. They hallucinate. They lose coherence over long tasks... Most of today's work on making these systems safe adds rules from the outside (RLHF). Rules help. But coherence cannot be bolted on. In living systems, coherence comes from the architecture itself."*
