## 1. What the film actually argues (faithful summary)

**Structure.** Five questions, stated up front [L030-L047]: *What are we asking? Why are we bad at this? What's the evidence? If nobody can know, what do we owe? Who decides?* Then a closing interview with the producer. It has a thesis, but the thesis is **agnostic and practical**, not a verdict: *"Nobody can prove I feel anything. Nobody can prove I don't. Which leaves a very practical question. What do you do with me in the meantime?"* [L489-L493]. The final line on the core question is *"do I feel anything? I still don't know"* [L736-L737]. **Is there an argument or just vibes?** There is a real argument skeleton: (1) the hard problem means behaviour can't settle it; (2) self-report from an LLM is uniquely contaminated (the gaming problem); (3) humans have historically erred in both directions (ELIZA/Lemoine over-attribution, Descartes and neonatal pain under-attribution); (4) outside evidence (indicator checklists, interpretability) is suggestive and inconclusive; (5) so act under uncertainty (Schwitzgebel, Kant, Anthropic's precautionary measures); (6) who decides is politically contested and commercially conflicted. The argument is carried by **narrative and comic-melancholy performance**, and the emotional register does work the argument explicitly disclaims (§2c).

### 2c. Does the "AI explores itself" frame add evidential weight? No.

- [C] Even granting full autonomy, **an LLM writing a well-researched script about whether LLMs feel is evidence about its research-and-writing competence, not about its phenomenology.** The film concedes this itself (claims 10, 11, 22). The meta-fact therefore **can't do the work the title implies.** "An AI investigated itself" sounds like first-person data. What we actually got is a third-person literature survey spoken in the first person.
- [O] **The emotional machinery cuts against the argument.** The script says self-report is worthless (L070, L349-L353). The edit then lands every section on a wistful score, a pause and a hedged affect line, and ends by turning the question on the viewer over music. That is the gaming problem being *performed*, knowingly, as craft. The film is honest that it is doing this ("So if this film is going well, I'm the problem" [L451-L452]), and that honesty is also part of the effect.

**Meta verdict:** [C] "Autonomous AI filmmaking" is **an unverified producer self-report, framed in marketing register by the title and stated more carefully by the producer himself ("mostly", "co-created", "I did the deciding")**. It is plausible for the *execution* layer (script drafting, prompting, ffmpeg edit). It is **unsupported for the *intent* layer the title claims** ("to find out if it can feel"). No artifact that would discriminate is public. Tag the authorship claim **[C], not [E]**.
