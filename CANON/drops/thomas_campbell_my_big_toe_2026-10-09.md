# The four quotes that matter

1. **Purpose, stated as a physical direction:** *"if all of its bits are random, there is no information. That's the highest entropy condition... If it orders some of those bits and particularly if it gives that order some meaning. Then you have information is created. So **information systems evolve by lowering entropy**."*
2. **The carrier is the record, not the avatar:** *"The virtual part doesn't come with you. **The only thing that comes with you is the quality you've earned, the amount of entropy you've reduced up to this point.** So I call that quality of consciousness that comes with you."*
3. **Instrumentalism, cleanly stated:** *"there are no fields. Fields are imaginary. Fields don't exist. In a virtual reality, you don't need fields. All you need is the equation that computes the force. **The force is real. The field is just mathematics that describes the experience.**"* And on method: *"you don't confuse your model for reality... exactly predicts and is falsifiable."*
4. **The one that lands on the labs:** *"fear creates self-centeredness because fear is all about you. It creates ego. It creates beliefs."* … *"acting nice is civilizing, but it doesn't help you grow up any. **You've got to be nice. And there's a big difference between acting kind and being kind.**"*
