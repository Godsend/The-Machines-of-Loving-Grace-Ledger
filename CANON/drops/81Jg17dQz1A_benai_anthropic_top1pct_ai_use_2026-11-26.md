## 3. Strongest point and weakest joint

**Strongest point.** Most of the hard numbers are carried over correctly. **Weakest joint.** The video turns **task-specific domain expertise** into **communication skill** and then sells a prompt template as the cure. Study A's thesis is the opposite of a prompting thesis: «In our data, success is determined by how well a person understands the problem they are trying to solve, not whether they’re trained in coding.» `[VERIFIED, primary]` The tape's version is "So in short, how clearly they communicated." `[VERIFIED]` The classifier does score precision of direction, verification requests and who corrects whom `[VERIFIED, primary]`. But because precision is an *input* to the expert label, *precise briefs go with success* is partly built into the measure `[HYP]`. A four-part template can make a brief look precise without supplying the domain understanding the label is meant to detect `[HYP]`.
