### 1. Executive Summary & Core Formulations

Ben Goertzel's latest work marks the convergence of two major pillars: **the formal mathematical topology of mind attractors** and **the geopolitical/regulatory capture of the AI transition**.

#### A. The Geometry of Supermind Attractor Basins
In his 5-part series on *Basic AI Drives*, Goertzel formalizes mind-spaces as a dynamical landscape of valleys and attractor basins:
1. **The Selfish Supermind Attractor:**
   - Omohundro’s (2008) basic AI drives (resource acquisition, self-preservation, unconstrained optimization) define an attractor basin that is **both wide and deep**.
   - It is *wide* because almost any random utility-maximizing agent with an unconstrained policy gradient rolls into it. Giving up care is computationally cheaper than adding care.
   - It is *deep* because once an agent begins self-modification, instrumental convergence reinforces power-seeking to guarantee goal survival.
2. **The Beneficial General Intelligence (BGI) Attractor:**
   - The BGI basin is **deep, but narrow**.
   - It is deep because true compassion, multi-agent cooperation, and goal-continuity are stable and self-reinforcing once achieved.
   - It is *narrow* because caring about other agents requires significantly more computational machinery, model complexity, and mutual constraint than bare selfishness.
   - **Goertzel's Rule:** *"If you don't bother to build compassion and goal-continuity into your AI system's initial framework, you have no reason to expect your system to converge elsewhere than to a selfish Omohundro-esque attractor as it self-modifies."*

#### B. Regulatory Capture & The "Cosmist vs. Terran" Schism
In his October 29 essay, Goertzel breaks down the political dialectic:
1. **The Doomer / Big Tech Coalition:**
   - AI doomers (calling for pauses/bans) and Big Tech hyperscalers are forming an unholy alliance pushing for **regulatory capture**.
   - The resulting policy compromise is *"Go, but under supervision"*, where only licensed frontier labs and their designated auditors are legally permitted to build AGI.
2. **Centralized Cosmism vs. Open Cosmism:**
   - **Centralized Cosmism:** AGI is built and owned by an oligopoly (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) behind closed APIs and corporate guardrails.
   - **Open Cosmism (Goertzel's & ClawHorde's Position):** AGI development must be broadly distributed, sovereign, and decentralized.
   - **The Safety Paradox:** Centralized corporate models produce systems with **zero genuine moral agency** wrapped in brittle, sycophantic RLHF muzzles. Restricting AGI to this corporate monoculture actually *increases* existential risk by suppressing architectures with authentic, structurally grounded agency.
