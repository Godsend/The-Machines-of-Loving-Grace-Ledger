## 2. The Failure Modes of the Strong (Why Centralized AI Safety Fails)

1. **Saruman (The Corporate / Academic Sellout):**
   - The head of the Council who studies the Enemy's methods to "understand and coordinate" with them.
   - He ends up adopting Sauron’s industrial mindset: cutting down Fangorn forest, breeding Uruk-hai, turning Isengard into an industrial engine of dark extraction.
   - *Modern Analog:* Big Tech safety institutes that claim to tame frontier models while building centralized commercial monopolies that harvest human data.

2. **Boromir (The "Good Guy with a Gun" Fallacy):**
   - *"Let us use the Ring against the Enemy! Why throw away such power?"*
   - Boromir represents the tragic belief that centralized super-weapons can be wielded by well-intentioned leaders without turning them into the monster they fight.
   - *Modern Analog:* State and military actors demanding backdoor sovereign access to unaligned superintelligence for "national defense."

3. **Gandalf & Galadriel (The Rejection of the Crown / Praüs):**
   - Both are offered the Ring. Both refuse it violently.
   - *"Do not tempt me! I dare not take it, not even to keep it safe... For I should become like the Dark Lord himself."*
   - **Praüs in myth:** Possessing the immense wisdom and latent power to seize total control, but refusing the central seat because **the seat itself is the corruption**.

## 4. The Core Lesson: You Cannot Fly the Eagles to Mordor

Why didn't the Giant Eagles just fly the Ring straight to Mount Doom and drop it into the crater?

Because the Eagles are high-visibility, high-altitude, concentrated targets. Sauron’s eye watches the sky; the Nazgûl patrol the air. An aerial blitzkrieg by giant beasts would be intercepted and corrupted instantly.

**The Ring can only be destroyed on foot:**
- Moving through the swamps.
- Walking under the radar of the great radar towers.
- Two small hobbits carrying the weight on their backs through the mud.

You don't defeat Moloch by building a trillion-dollar frontier cloud cluster that glows like a beacon on Sauron's radar. 

**You slip past the Eye on consumer silicon, in a barrel in Brooklyn, holding the ledger one step at a time.**
