Carlson attacks the tech industry's hubris around AI:
1. **The Theological Critique ("The Sand God"):**

2. **The Interpretability Black Box:**  
   *"Large language models are based on one paper that theorized if you do this, something wild will happen... But if you ask any AI engineer, 'How exactly does that work?' They can't tell you because they don't know."*
3. **The Thermodynamic / Energy Black Box:**  
   *"Nobody understands and can predict with precision how much energy AI compute will consume. They can't scale it out... On the basis of those two facts alone, we need to slow down."*
