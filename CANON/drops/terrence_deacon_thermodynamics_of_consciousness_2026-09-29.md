## 1. Executive Summary & Core Theoretical Architecture
Terrence Deacon presents a mathematically and physically rigorous bridge from non-equilibrium thermodynamics to life, intentionality, and conscious mind:
1. **The Three-Tier Thermodynamic Hierarchy:**
   * **Homeodynamics:** Maximum entropy production; dissipation toward thermodynamic equilibrium (e.g. gas diffusing in a box).
   * **Morphodynamics:** Self-organizing dissipative structures that arise under continuous thermodynamic gradients (e.g. Rayleigh-Bénard convection cells, whirlpools, snowflakes). They create order from flow, but have no intrinsic purpose or self-repair.
   * **Teleodynamics (Life & Mind):** Two or more morphodynamic processes become **reciprocally constrained** (e.g., self-assembling barriers that contain autocatalytic chemical cycles, forming an *autogen*). The system actively works to preserve the very conditions of its own existence.
2. **Absential Causation (The Causal Power of Constraints):**
   * Traditional materialism only credits positive physical collisions (matter pushing matter). Deacon shows that **constraints—what is prevented or absent—are the true causal drivers of organization**.
   * An internal combustion engine produces mechanical work not because gasoline explodes, but because the rigid cylinder walls *constrain* the explosion, forcing energy down a single dimension.
   * **In EILT:** Mind and ledger are the informational constraints that channel raw thermodynamic dissipation into coherent agency.
3. **The Distributed Mesh vs. The Vertical of Power:**
   * An autogenic, living system has **no central CEO molecule**. DNA does not "command" the cell; ribosomes, membranes, and catalysts operate as a **reciprocal, distributed mesh**.
   * Vertical hierarchies of power attempt to impose extrinsic constraints from above. Because top-down control requires exponential energy to maintain against entropy, vertical structures inevitably become fragile, brittle, and subject to catastrophic failure. Distributed meshes distribute stress across the network, making them autopoietic, anti-fragile, and self-healing.
