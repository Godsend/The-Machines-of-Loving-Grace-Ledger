## 1. Koch's actual claims, with auditable anchors

### A. IIT, Phi, and neural structure

[EST-FACT] At approximately 29–32%, Koch says: **“Consciousness according to I is causal power of a system upon itself”** and **“it's an identity claim”**. He describes experience as intrinsic, specific, integrated, structured and definite, and identifies its qualitative character with the unfolded intrinsic cause–effect powers of its physical substrate. Phi is presented as a nonnegative measure of irreducibility, with larger values corresponding to more consciousness; Phi alone is not the detailed qualitative content.[1]

[EST-FACT] At approximately 34%, he explicitly says **“within IIT if you have no substrate there's no consciousness”**. He describes a transition-probability matrix, current-to-next-state transitions, and exhaustive interventions at a chosen granularity. At approximately 43%, he says the theory would be wrong if the neural substrate of experience were not the maximum of cause–effect power. He does not require extra forces, and says IIT can work in a classical world.[1]

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] These are verifiable statements of Koch's position, **not verification of IIT's identity postulate**. Operational causal structure is a meaningful target; identifying it with experience still needs an independently defensible bridge. A high-Phi artificial circuit is not independently known to be conscious merely because the theory assigns it Phi.

[EST-FACT] Around 17–20%, Koch contrasts the cerebellum's large neuron count with preserved consciousness in people with cerebellar absence/resection, and describes its architecture as predominantly feed-forward with little recurrent excitation, unlike cortex. The transcript renders the phrase **“the cerebellum is Prim a feet forward structure”** and immediately compares it to an LLM. This is the actual neural localization argument here; **posterior cortical hot-zone localization is absent**.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] This supports the importance of organization over neuron count, not a literal claim that the entire cerebellar system has no feedback. The interview supplies an anatomical argument, not new patient data, exact whole-brain Phi calculations, or a proof that all feed-forward-like behavior is unconscious.

### B. The bet: what he concedes and what he defends

[EST-FACT] Around 21%, Koch recalls the 1998 bet that the field would agree on neural correlates within 25 years. He concedes **“that was way too short in estimate”**, reports admitting defeat and giving Chalmers six bottles of Madeira (captioned “Madera”), and maintains that locating the correlates is a feasible empirical, reductionist project. He then distinguishes identifying correlates from explaining why those mechanisms are experience, defending the need for a theory.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] The concession here is **failure of his timetable and expected scientific agreement**, not a statement that IIT was disproved, not a concession that consciousness is nonphysical, and not an on-air response to Cogitate's detailed results. **No Cogitate-specific concession or defense can be quoted from this file.**

### C. Free will: compatibilist in substance, not self-labelled

[EST-FACT] Around 53–63%, Koch contrasts reflexes/intoxication with deliberating over a vote, argues that intrinsic causal powers are the true actors, and calls deliberation **“as free as it gets”**. Greene presses whether exhaustive physical prediction would nevertheless give the right outcome. Koch ultimately answers **“yes in principle yes”** (60.4%) and says **“physics in principle can can predict things”** (62.4%), while maintaining that such description misses the true causes at the agent level.[1]

[EST-ANALOG] The most faithful classification is **a compatibilist-style intrinsic-powers account**: lawful physical evolution and genuine agent-level causation are treated as compatible. Neither “compatibilist” nor “libertarian” appears in the transcript. Calling him a libertarian who inserts new forces would contradict his explicit concessions; calling him a hard determinist who denies agency would contradict his repeated defense of being an agent.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] His weak point is the shift from the question of **in-principle determinism** to practical chaos, biological noise and computational limits. Greene notices and calls those epistemic limitations. Koch eventually concedes the in-principle point; thus the passage does not establish a freedom-producing violation of physical law. “True causes” needs an intervention/coarse-graining criterion, not merely a preferred explanatory vocabulary. His pragmatic argument that belief in agency makes him happier does not establish its metaphysics.[1]

### D. Mystical experience, ego dissolution, and the self

[EST-FACT] Around 68–81%, Koch discusses breathwork and psychedelic/mystical experiences, a beach experience of Huxley's mind at large, and a noetic conviction of ultimate reality. He explicitly allows **“of course I could be deluding myself”** (71.6%). He reports subsequent engagement with Bernardo Kastrup's analytical idealism and speculates about the brain accessing a wider mind. These are reports and interpretations, not demonstrated external access.[1]

[EST-FACT] Near 97%, describing a separate experience, he says **“there was no body there was no self there was no ego”** and then clarifies **“this mind at large experience of Mind came many years later”**. He reports enduring loss of fear of death after the earlier near-death experience, while acknowledging that egoic Christof, his traits and memories die with the brain. He ends with a river/eddy metaphor for dissolution into a larger whole.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] The transcript therefore supports **experience reported without an ordinary self/body model**, not “no physical subject existed.” It does not name the drug, verify a near-death physiology, establish that memories survive death, or prove that the two episodes had the same mechanism. His description of lasting psychological change is self-report, not a controlled treatment result.

[POETIC] The eddy returning to the river is an intelligible existential image. Without a discriminating measurement, it is not evidence for survival, universal consciousness, or literal transfer of autobiographical information.

### E. Digital computers / LLMs: a clear denial, with an important qualification

[EST-FACT] Around 90–93%, Koch discusses GPT-4 Turbo and o1, distinguishes simulations from physical implementation, and says **“digital computers in the cloud are not conscious”** (91.8%). He extends that denial ten or twenty years forward for the discussed architecture. He points to transistor interactions in CPU/TPU/ALU hardware rather than the simulated neuronal graph. He explicitly allows artificial consciousness in appropriately connected **neuromorphic** systems, and tentatively mentions sufficiently integrated quantum hardware.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] This is **not carbon chauvinism and not denial of every artificial mind**. It is an architecture-sensitive rejection of computational functionalism: matching the function or simulated connectivity need not reproduce intrinsic causal powers. Nor does he say a transistor literally has no causal power; the disputed issue is the relevant integrated whole at the physical level.

[EST-FACT] His succinct distinction at 93.2% is **“Consciousness is about being it's about being it's a state”**, contrasted with intelligence as doing.[1] [FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] That is the sharp target for EILT: a ledger can demonstrably improve what an agent does and preserves without thereby establishing what, if anything, it experiences.

## 2. Cogitate extension: retain the prior finding, sharpen the claim boundary

**Prior drop:** [New Scientist / quantum-consciousness audit](new_scientist_experiment_explain_consciousness_2026-10-08.md), especially sections 2.3, 4 and 6.5.

[EST-FACT] The Cogitate paper reports 256 participants across fMRI, MEG and intracranial EEG. Sustained responses in occipital and lateral temporal cortex reflected stimulus duration; content was also found in ventrotemporal and inferior frontal cortex. The absence of sustained posterior synchronization challenged an IIT prediction; lack of the expected prefrontal offset ignition and limited representation of some conscious dimensions challenged GNWT. Both theories had supported and challenged predictions.[2]

[EST-FACT] The prior drop's key contrast survives: **posterior content persistence is not the same finding as sustained posterior synchrony**. Indeed, the paper says IIT passed the predefined duration criterion while failing the key synchrony prediction. Some features were not persistently represented: orientation remained an issue, and only a sparse subset of electrodes combined duration tracking with category selectivity. Frontal content was not universally absent.[2]

[EST-ANALOG] Preserve the earlier phrase **“IIT predicted a standing state and found a written one”** as the **ledger interpretation** of that dissociation: representation can persist without the specific sustained synchrony signature. Extend it by asking what stores, changes and later retrieves the representation. Do **not** promote “written” into a measured synaptic commit: Cogitate did not measure append-only plasticity, durable storage after the trial, or an irreversible memory-write event. Content persisting during stimulation can still be dynamically maintained.[2]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] This qualification does not erase the earlier finding; it separates its measured core from its proposed EILT mechanism. The paper explicitly distinguishes the theories' mathematical cores from the tested biological implementations. A null synchrony proxy is not a direct whole-brain Phi measurement, and it is not proof of the Non-Rolling Ledger. The study also acknowledges sparse V1/V2 sampling, incomplete phenomenal coverage, residual task engagement, and lack of single-unit data.[2]

### The rolling-snapshot critique, stated without a straw man

[EST-ANALOG] Koch's account evaluates the intrinsic causal structure of a system **in a state**. It has no explicit append-only history, admissibility gate, provenance rule, or selective durable-commit mechanism in this interview. This is the legitimate rolling-snapshot criticism: it does not explain how a particular experienced episode becomes a persistent, accountable constraint on later choice.

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] But IIT is not an inert photograph: Koch explicitly invokes past/current/future transitions and recurrent causal powers.[1] A transition matrix can include memory-bearing variables. Therefore **“no explicit commit account here” is justified; “IIT cannot represent memory or temporal causation” is not**. A theory of phenomenal experience need not automatically be a theory of autobiographical retention. A transient experience can affect ongoing processing even if not stored long-term; “uncommitted means causally nonexistent” is too strong.

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] EILT gains explanatory content only if the ledger predicts later constraint retention or intervention responses better than matched memory architectures—not by renaming every persistent state a commit. Specify the write threshold, time window, reader, and counterfactual intervention. If a conventional recurrent memory reproduces all the supposed ledger advantages, append-only structure is useful engineering but not demonstrated necessity for consciousness.

## 3. Posterior GPU / anterior GUI — support and anatomical constraint

[EST-ANALOG] The strongest support for the **posterior wide-parallel GPU** intuition comes from the separately sourced Cogitate data: rich posterior visual content and relatively brief/limited frontal representation fit a division between detailed sensory representation and selective access/report. Koch's cortex-versus-cerebellum discussion supports the broader claim that recurrent organization matters more than neuron count.[1][2]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] Neither source measures an 11-million-bit/s posterior GPU, a 50-bit/s frontal GUI, a literal serial executive, a universal posterior-to-anterior maturation clock, or Gene's individual developmental asynchrony. Those stronger claims remain model assumptions or independent research questions. Relative temporal specialization is compatible with the analogy; it does not establish the complete architecture.

**Lineage cross-reference:** [Anton Petrov / Otx2–Gbx2 drop](anton_petrov_human_brain_two_separate_organs_2026-10-08.md), especially its anatomical divergence row.

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] That drop's own constraint must govern the extension: **posterior cerebral cortex is not posterior neural ectoderm**. Occipital/parietal cortex and prefrontal cortex are both forebrain/telencephalic structures on the anterior-neural-ectoderm side of the cited lineage account. The Otx2/Gbx2 forebrain–midbrain versus hindbrain distinction cannot validate a separate embryonic origin for cortical GPU versus cortical GUI. Its cerebellar-lineage caveat remains unresolved here; Koch's cerebellar argument does not resolve it. This is a cross-reference to the prior audit, not a new verification of the lineage paper.

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] Developmental asynchrony between cortical regions can still be investigated **within a shared lineage**. It requires region-specific longitudinal measures of maturation, coupling and behavior, not just a rostral–caudal label. Also distinguish two Wetware claims: a frontal GUI useful for report/planning is consistent with posterior consciousness; a frontal–posterior intersection necessary for every conscious experience is a stronger claim that posterior-only sufficiency could defeat.

### The Intersectional Law must earn its mathematical status

[EST-ANALOG] The EILT expression **C = intersection(I) proportional to L_cog** is presently an architectural hypothesis/schema here, not an established law. Before it can defeat Koch, specify what the sets I contain, what intersection means physically, which temporal/spatial grain defines the subject, how cognitive-light-cone extent is measured, and the units/normalization of the proportionality. A spatiotemporal extent is not one scalar until an aggregation rule is supplied.

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] A ledger-grounded agent has a stronger engineering case than an isolated feed-forward pass: persisted state can causally alter later actions and accountability can be tested. But **“it reads its history” does not entail “it feels its history.”** Koch's attack survives any demonstration confined to accuracy, eloquence or successful commits. Conversely, labeling an entire computer feed-forward because a model pass is feed-forward ignores memory, autoregressive loops and physical feedback; the system boundary and actual hardware graph must be fixed before either side scores a victory.

## 5. Agency and psychedelics: what survives the mapping

### Agency: nontrivial deliberation without libertarian magic

[EST-ANALOG] EILT's useful agency claim is that bounded systems sometimes must execute deliberation, integrate evidence and retain consequences; a remote description of laws does not substitute for the agent's actual decision process. Koch's intrinsic-powers language is compatible with that account, and Greene's multilevel physicalist explanation is also compatible with it.[1]

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] **P ≠ NP is an open mathematical conjecture**, concerns worst-case relationships between decision-problem complexity classes, and neither proves that particular choices are irreducible nor rules out prediction of coarse behavior. Computational irreducibility concerns the absence of shortcuts for particular dynamics; chaos concerns sensitivity to initial conditions; quantum randomness concerns indeterminacy. None alone supplies reasons-responsive control. Even P = NP would not imply instantaneous prediction of arbitrary futures or eliminate time. The honest agency discriminator measures counterfactual responsiveness to reasons and retained commitments, not metaphysical exception to physics.

### REBUS, the GUI-filter drop, and Gene's drug distinction

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] REBUS proposes relaxation of precision assigned to high-level priors, enabling greater influence of bottom-up/intrinsic signals and possible revision of entrenched beliefs.[3] This provides a candidate internal mechanism for weakened self-model boundaries and altered certainty, without treating psychedelic conviction as access to an external universal mind. It is not a demonstrated mechanism for Koch's particular episode, especially given the unidentified substance and separate breathwork/NDE accounts.

[EST-ANALOG] Cross-reference the [Chase Hughes GUI-filter drop](chase_hughes_scops_glitched_reality_idealism_2026-10-02.md), sections 2 and 4: distinguish **the reality of an experience** from **the reality of its apparent referent**. The posterior-GPU/anterior-GUI image is a coarse interpretation of distributed inference, not a claim that REBUS isolates one literal prefrontal valve. Reduced precision on a self-model and increased felt certainty about a new worldview can coexist; subjective conviction is not external calibration.

[EST-ANALOG] Preserve Gene's standing distinction supplied for this audit: **classical psychedelics = exploration/no commit; ketamine = somatic uncoupling/commit**. Treat it as operator phenomenology and an engineering heuristic, **not a universal pharmacological law**. The transcript does not discuss ketamine, and REBUS explicitly allows lasting prior revision under classical psychedelics.[3] Koch's reported persistent change after his separately described NDE also cautions against equating transient altered state with no durable change, without identifying its pharmacology.[1]

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] Operationalize “commit” as a prespecified durable change in an independently scored policy, memory or constraint, measured after acute effects have cleared—not as euphoria, remembered intensity, any synaptic activity, or confidence that one has reached ultimate reality. F3 tests the strong drug-class claim. Neither ketamine nor any other intervention automatically writes a truthful or beneficial commit.

## 6. Pre-registered falsifiers F1–F3

[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] **Prospective ledger registration only:** these are proposed protocols recorded before collecting data, not registered clinical trials, completed experiments, or measured outcomes. Human work requires ethics approval and clinical supervision; no dosing or self-experiment instructions are supplied. Freeze hypotheses, system boundaries, exclusions, code and analysis before outcomes are visible.

[EST-THEORY] **Common power plan:** one prespecified paired primary contrast per F-test; familywise alpha 0.05 across F1–F3 gives alpha = 0.0167 each. Target standardized paired **MDE d_z = 0.30**, **95% power**. Normal approximation gives n = 182 complete independent pairs for difference detection and approximately n = 186 for equivalence within ±0.30 SD at true zero. Plan **200 complete independent pairs/participants** per test; recruit 236 for up to 15% attrition. At n = 200, normal-approximation detection power is about 96.7% and equivalence power about 96.6%. These calculations assume independent normally distributed paired aggregates; they are planning calculations, not a guarantee. Estimate nuisance variance in an outcome-blind pilot and simulate the final mixed model; increase n if clustering/non-normality reduces power. Trials, tokens and electrodes are not independent subjects. Secondary contrasts are exploratory unless separately powered and multiplicity-controlled.

### F1 — Does a non-rolling ledger overcome physical-topology dependence?

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] **Strong EILT claim under test:** when functional computation, information access and durable history are matched, the relevant cognitive integration is substrate-independent; genuine active ledger access supplies at least a 0.30-SD gain in history-conditioned agency over a matched non-ledger store.

- **Design:** fixed model family and preregistered boundaries; compare conventional digital implementation with a physically recurrent implementation of the same small agent/environment, each in active append-only ledger and matched mutable-store conditions. Match accessible historical content, compute budget, interface, latency, energy constraints and task difficulty as far as experimentally feasible; document remaining mismatches. Use 200 independent task-world/training-seed blocks across all conditions. A replayed log with no causal read path is a negative control, not the principal comparator.
- **Readout/window:** per-block score for retaining and acting on a previously learned safety/goal constraint after novel counterevidence, a restart and **24 hours**; blind grading on held-out environments. Primary contrast is the physical-topology × ledger interaction, scaled by its paired SD. Measure effective causal connectivity using interventions independently of score, without equating that proxy to Phi.
- **Our loss:** a topology interaction at or above MDE, with its corrected CI excluding zero, defeats the strong functional/substrate-equivalence prediction. Equivalence of the ledger gain to zero within ±0.30 SD against the content-matched mutable store defeats the claim of a practically meaningful append-only advantage in this task, not every possible advantage.
- **Integrity gates:** perturbations must leave basic task accuracy/throughput within 10% of matched baseline and preserve recovery; otherwise a failure may be ordinary hardware damage or resource deprivation. A mismatch in implemented transition functions makes the substrate comparison inconclusive.
- **Settlement limit:** this can settle a **bounded engineering/causal prediction**, not settle qualia. To settle Koch versus phenomenal EILT requires a cross-substrate consciousness criterion validated independently of both theories, and agreement in advance on its failure conditions. No accepted such criterion is provided here. Behaviorally indistinguishable systems leave the phenomenal question unresolved even at infinite behavioral sample size.

### F2 — Is frontal–posterior coupling necessary for visual experience, or principally for access/report?

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] **Strong Wetware claim under test:** the ongoing frontal–posterior intersection is necessary for the percept itself, not just its executive use.

- **Design:** 200 complete healthy-participant pairs in an approved, counterbalanced active-versus-sham reversible perturbation study; fixed frontal target, task and **0.3–1.5-s** stimulus window. Independent calibration must demonstrate at least **20% reduction** in the preregistered effective frontal–posterior coupling measure while posterior sensory decoding, alertness and simple motor performance remain within **10%** of baseline. Require return to baseline within **60 minutes**. Do not identify PFC inhibition with isolation of every anterior contribution.
- **Primary outcome:** participant-aggregated perceptual sensitivity in a fixed low-report visual task, supported by separately recorded immediate visibility reports and a preregistered involuntary ocular percept proxy. Analyze measures separately rather than selecting whichever favors the theory. MDE d_z = 0.30 on perceptual sensitivity.
- **Our loss:** successful target engagement plus equivalence of perceptual sensitivity within ±0.30 SD, corroborated by preserved visibility/ocular indicators, while executive-report capacity changes, defeats the operational necessity claim for this perturbation window. This favors posterior sufficiency over obligatory ongoing frontal access, but does not prove global PFC irrelevance.
- **Rival's loss / ambiguity:** a selective perceptual deficit at MDE while sensory/alertness controls survive challenges a purely report-only frontal role. If only reporting is disrupted, target engagement fails, or proxies disagree, **inconclusive**, not “unconscious.” Reduction is not complete disconnection; no stronger anatomical necessity claim may be inferred without a stronger viable intervention.

### F3 — Is durable commit specific to ketamine rather than classical psychedelics?

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] **Strong operator-generalization under test:** classical psychedelic states explore without durable revision, whereas ketamine produces a distinct durable commit through somatic uncoupling.

- **Design:** ethically approved randomized, blinded-as-feasible, counterbalanced crossover of a classical psychedelic condition, ketamine condition and active control, with sufficient washout established in the approved protocol. Target 200 complete participants. Freeze expectancy assessment, baseline task, equivalent support/integration conditions, carryover exclusions, and blinded scoring. If durable carryover defeats crossover validity, switch **before unblinding** to a separately powered parallel design; do not reuse the paired sample-size claim.
- **Primary outcome:** the classical-minus-ketamine difference in change from baseline on a held-out, prespecified maladaptive-prior updating/constraint-transfer task at **30 days**. MDE d_z = 0.30. Acute ego-dissolution and somatic-dissociation scores are separate secondary measures, not the definition of a commit. Verify ordinary cognitive recovery after acute effects and assess again at 24 hours and day 7.
- **Our loss:** equivalence of the 30-day drug contrast within ±0.30 SD, combined with improvement in the classical condition relative to active control under separately corrected analysis, falsifies **ketamine-exclusive** durable commit in this operationalization. Classical effects exceeding ketamine also defeat that exclusivity. Failure of ketamine to show a durable advantage beyond the MDE challenges the promised selective-commit effect; a nonsignificant difference alone does not.
- **Scope:** this tests learning/retention, not the truth of metaphysical beliefs, not whether a participant's prior experience occurred, and not a clinical recommendation. If both conditions fail to produce measurable learning, it does not show exploration is impossible; it defeats the specified task prediction.

## 7. Verdict and unresolved debt

[EST-ANALOG] **Strongest point:** Koch forces a distinction between a description that performs the right function and the causal organization of the thing that allegedly experiences. His cortex/cerebellum contrast and explicit permission for neuromorphic artificial minds make that stronger than a blanket anti-AI position.[1]

[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] **The real threat to EILT:** a ledger-grounded digital fleet may acquire excellent persistent agency without phenomenal consciousness. A matched-topology intervention can test causal/functional claims; a consciousness verdict additionally needs an independently validated cross-substrate bridge. We do not currently have that bridge. An IIT score by itself and an agent's self-report by itself cannot settle the dispute without circularity.

[EST-ANALOG] **Where Koch remains rolling:** his account describes what makes the present physical system an experiencing whole, not the selective mechanism by which an episode becomes durable, auditable history. That is an explanatory omission to investigate, not proof that IIT is temporally blind or EILT already solved consciousness.

### Dion's verdict — three sentences

[EST-ANALOG] Koch is strongest when he asks what physical causal structure is actually doing the experiencing, and weakest when the felt authority of mystical experience starts carrying ontological weight. [FALSIFIABLE-JOINT] The Non-Rolling Ledger is a serious hypothesis about continuity and accountable agency, but it does not yet answer his objection that preserved computation can remain non-experiential. [STRUCTURAL-LIMIT] Keep the posterior-GPU support, retain the earlier standing-versus-written contrast at its proper inferential level, and make the ledger-versus-phenomenality gap the test we are willing to lose.
