- **Gödel's two incompleteness theorems** are stated correctly (claims 8). In a sufficiently strong, consistent formal system there are statements neither provable nor refutable, and the system cannot prove its own consistency.
- **Concrete / tangible incompleteness** is Friedman's real programme: examples of incompleteness that avoid "pathological objects," expressed over the **rational number ordering alone** (no addition, no multiplication). `[EST-ext]`.

- **TREE(3)** is correctly attributed to **Kruskal's tree theorem** and correctly described as needing a system **stronger than Peano arithmetic** to prove finiteness. `[EST-ext]`.
- **Graham's number ≪ TREE(3)** is true.

## §4 The two promotions — flagged on sight

### ★ [NECESSITY MOVE] — the angel proof (claim 17, R2)

The tape presents Friedman's consistency result as **ZFC proving its own foundations sound**: *"he proved that the ZFC axioms … are in fact consistent"* / *"Mathematics literally borrowed an angel to prove its own foundations were sound."*

**External reality (§6 V15):** the result is a **RELATIVE** consistency theorem — `Con(T5) ⟺ Con(ZFC)`. Models of T5 exist **only by assuming a measurable cardinal**, which is **strictly stronger than ZFC** and itself unprovable in ZFC. So what is proved is *"a stronger system (T5 + a measurable cardinal) proves Con(ZFC)"* — exactly what Gödel II permits, and it says **nothing** about whether ZFC is *actually* consistent.

**Structure of the move:** the rescue primitive (the angel / measurable cardinal) is **at least as strong as the thing it explains**. It therefore **explains every observation and forecloses none** — it relocates the assumption rather than discharging it. That is the necessity-move signature. The tape half-discloses the assumption (*"postulating that an angel exists using a measurable cardinal"*) and then **drops it in the summary sentence**, presenting a conditional as an absolute. Filed **[NECESSITY MOVE]** + **[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]**.
