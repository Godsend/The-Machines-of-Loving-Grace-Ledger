## 1. Summary of Mishlove's Position
Mishlove synthesizes his 2021 Bigelow Institute (BICS) grand prize case for the survival of human consciousness after permanent bodily death:
1. **The "Bundle of Arrows" Methodology (Cumulative Case):**
   Mishlove concedes that no single empirical category offers an airtight proof of postmortem survival. Instead, he borrows William James's *radical empiricism* and Peter Sturrock's Bayesian cumulative framing: nine independent categories of anomalies ("white crows") converge:
   - Near-Death Experiences (NDEs) & Peak-in-Darien cases (seeing deceased persons not known to have died)
   - After-Death Communications (ADCs) & Mediumship (mental and physical)
   - Reincarnation cases (Ian Stevenson / Jim Tucker / DOPS children's past-life memories)
   - Instrumental Transcommunication (ITC) & Electronic Voice Phenomena
   - Xenoglossy & Possession cases
2. **The Metaphysical Ground (Idealism + William James Transmission):**
   Mishlove rejects materialism (cannot solve the Hard Problem) and substance dualism (cannot explain Cartesian interaction). He adopts analytical idealism (Kastrup / Planck): universal mentality is ontologically fundamental.
3. **The Transmission / Filter Theory of the Brain:**
   Citing William James (1898), Mishlove posits that the brain is not a generator of consciousness, but a biological *transceiver and reducing valve*. It restricts the vast mind-at-large to narrow sensory slices required for biological survival. When brain metabolism collapses (cardiac arrest, terminal lucidity, psychedelics), the reducing valve opens, releasing consciousness into a higher-dimensional space (Saul-Paul Sirag's hyperspace formalism).
4. **Rejection of Super-Psi / Living Agent Psi (LAP):**
   To defend survival, Mishlove must defeat the primary counter-hypothesis within parapsychology: that living psychics subconsciously retrieve information clairvoyantly. Mishlove dismisses unrestricted LAP as an unfalsifiable ad-hoc dodge.

## 2. Robert Lawrence Kuhn's Epistemic Pushback
Kuhn brings his trademark critical scrutiny to the table:
- **The "Leaky Bucket" Fallacy:** If each of the nine arrows is methodologically compromised—plagued by confabulation, cryptomnesia, cold reading, REM intrusion, hypoxic surges, and cultural conditioning—combining nine leaky buckets does not yield a vessel that holds water.
- **The Super-Psi Dilemma:** Kuhn highlights the internal contradiction: parapsychology cannot invoke omnipotent psychic faculties to explain mediumship and then arbitrarily forbid those same faculties from explaining away postmortem discarnate spirits.
- **The Physicalist Baseline:** As Kuhn notes in his LoC taxonomy, every known feature of conscious experience is tightly correlated with, and perturbable by, physical neurochemical substrate.
