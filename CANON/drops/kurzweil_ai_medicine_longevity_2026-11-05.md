## 4. Strongest point / weakest joint

**★ STRONGEST POINT — the medicine receipt layer is real and checkable, and it is the one domain where his exponential has tape receipts.** AlphaFold's mass release of protein structures (an **epiplexic public ledger**), AlphaProteo's de-novo binder design, Insilico's end-to-end AI-designed drug reaching Phase 2a, and the gene-edited pig-kidney xenotransplant are **the strongest items on the tape** — and they land precisely on the house's own axis (structure that is hard to derive but easy to verify). **★ WEAKEST JOINT (single) — the human-trials bottleneck, which he states and then waves away.** He shows the rate constant in his own evidence: the vaccine was *"discovered… in just two days"* but *"then it took us 10 months to test it in humans."* That is not a compute-limited quantity — it is an **institutional/adoption** quantity, and it is the one variable in the talk that has never tracked Moore's law. He then asserts, without mechanism, that *"by 2035 we'll be able to replace human testing with… simulating biology and computers."* **Every downstream forecast that depends on adoption speed inherits this un-accelerating constant** — BCI *"installed without surgery and available to everyone"* by the 2030s, *"digital trials… all done in a matter of days,"* and most consequentially **longevity escape velocity by ~2032**. So his headline "you'll get back an entire year per year" is **load-bearing on a rate he never shows compounds.** That is the single weakest joint: **the exponential is applied to the one variable (institutional adoption) whose own stated value — 10 months for a 2-day design — is the counterexample.**
