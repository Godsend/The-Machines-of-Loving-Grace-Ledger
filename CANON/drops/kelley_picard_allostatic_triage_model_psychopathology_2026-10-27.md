## 1. The paper's own model — [HYP] (authors' proposal; not yet validated)

- **[HYP] Bioenergetic definition of stress.** Stress = any increase in metabolic demand that overwhelms the capacity of the stress response to raise ATP output to meet it. They define *stress intensity* = Δ energy demand − Δ energy availability; stress occurs when intensity is positive, driving "allostatic triage" and functional impairment. *(Their framing; a proposed operationalization, not a measured result.)*
- **[HYP] Allostatic triage (CNS extension).** Under energy scarcity, the brain reallocates limited ATP **away from metabolically expensive, acutely dispensable networks** (executive function, long-term optimization) **toward cheaper networks that mediate short-term survival** (attentional vigilance, fear response). The authors state they *"extend [allostatic triage] for the first time to CNS functional connectivity"* — i.e. the CNS application is the novel move; the concept is imported from the energetic model of allostatic load (EMAL, Bobba-Alves/Justin/Picard 2022) and Ames's micronutrient "Triage Theory" (Ames 2006).
- **[HYP] Affect as the brain's internal model of its own metabolic efficiency.** Built explicitly on Lisa Feldman Barrett's **EPIC** model (embodied predictive interoception coding; Barrett et al. 2016): affect = *"the expected sensory consequences of allostatic changes within the body's internal milieu."* Positive affect ↔ perceived high metabolic efficiency / capacity to take on load; negative affect ↔ perceived low efficiency. *(The "affect represents metabolic efficiency" identity is the authors' proposal, not an established finding.)*
- **[HYP] Affect-driven prioritization (their named novel concept).** Affect sets the **setpoint/threshold above which allostatic triage occurs**. The authors are explicit that this is their one genuinely new integration: *"Each of these principles, other than our novel concept of affect-driven allostatic triage, is well-established."*
- **[HYP] The decoupling mechanism (the pathological pivot).** Chronic/traumatic stress dysregulates the MIPS and the allostatic-interoceptive network, reducing metabolic efficiency, and the resulting unreliable prediction errors **uncouple affective states from actual metabolic efficiency** — while affect *keeps driving* triage. So a signal that has stopped tracking the resource it is supposed to represent retains authority over resource allocation, exacerbating triage inappropriately.
- **[HYP] Canalization.** Persistent triage patterns become entrenched through activity-dependent (Hebbian) neuroplasticity into stable psychiatric phenotypes. They borrow Robin Carhart-Harris's use of "canalization" (Carhart-Harris et al. 2022), in Waddington's original sense (Waddington 1942). Brain-state *transitions* are themselves metabolically expensive (citing Gu et al. 2018), so an entrenched state is bioenergetically resistant to change.
- **[HYP] Transdiagnostic claim.** Psychiatric, cardiometabolic, and immune disorders are bidirectionally comorbid; the model proposes a **shared bioenergetic mechanism** (stress → energy scarcity → dysregulated MIPS/affect → pathological triage → canalized disease) as the common etiology/pathophysiology. Peripheral dysregulation is described as *one contributing pathway*, not necessary for affective dysregulation.

### Table 3 (verbatim hypotheses, [HYP])
- **H1.** Preexisting peripheral (AL) and/or CNS metabolic inefficiency (MAL), and increases in biological energy demand such as acute inflammation, will increase the experience of negative affect during stress.
- **H2.** Affect will closely represent biological metabolic efficiency in healthy organisms, likely within the biological systems and CNS networks implicated in expected future behaviors.
- **H3.** *Allostatic Triage:* when stress-induced energy demand exceeds CNS energy production capacity, energy will be triaged to the least expensive regions/networks expected to ensure short-term survival, and away from more expensive regions/networks that may produce better long-term outcomes.
- **H4.** *Affect-Driven Prioritization:* affective intensity predicts the degree of stress-induced triage of CNS network functional connectivity (more negatively valenced states → greater triage).
- **H5.** In psychopathology/chronic disease, **affect becomes less associated with biological metabolic efficiency** (reduced allostatic metabolic efficiency) **but continues to predict allostatic triage in the CNS.**
- **H6.** *Triage-Driven Canalization:* persistent allostatic triage from one network/circuit to another predicts the persistent CNS functional-connectivity changes that develop in psychopathogenesis (triage predicts which networks are canalized).

## 2. Established background the paper relies on — [EST]

*(Fair to tag strong because these are prior-work or textbook facts with checkable citations; NOT the authors' new model.)*

- **[EST] The MIPS concept itself** — the mitochondrial information processing system (Picard & Shirihai 2022): mitochondria as information-integration hubs that receive, integrate, and output "summary signals" regulating cellular and systemic allostasis (their Table 1: metabolites, DNA/RNA precursors, ROS, cf-mtDNA, ions, ATP, heat, peptides, steroid hormones; plus epigenetic regulation of nuclear gene expression). The house's Master Monograph cites this same concept as "MIPS; Picard 2018" — minor citation-year difference, same lineage, no tension.
- **[EST] Mitochondrial allostatic load (MAL)** — stress-induced reduction in ATP/ADP ratio relative to the combined cost of metabolism plus background costs of hypermetabolic states (Picard et al. 2014). **[EST] EMAL** (energetic model of allostatic load; Bobba-Alves, Juster, Picard 2022). **[EST] AL / AOL** (allostatic load / allostatic overload; McEwen, Seeman et al.).
- **[EST] EPIC model** (Barrett et al. 2016) and the **theory of constructed emotion** (Barrett 2017) — established published models; the paper *reviews* them, and the active-inference framing traces to **Friston 2010** ("The free-energy principle: a unified brain theory?", ref. line 1382 of cached text).
- **[EST] Canalization** as a named concept (Carhart-Harris et al. 2022; Waddington 1942).
- **[EST] Mitokines** — FGF-21 and GDF-15 released downstream of the integrated stress response (ISR) to signal metabolic stress to the CNS (refs: Flippo & Potthoff 2021; Wang et al. 2021). This is the same GDF15/energy-stress channel the house already tracks (see `picard_energy_flow_vs_pattern_freeze_test_2026-09-23.md`).
- **[EST] Brain energy budget (background physiology):** brain ≈ 2–3% of body mass, uses ~20% of O2 and ~25% of glucose (Erbsloh 1958; Magistretti); brain can raise cerebral metabolic rate of O₂ only **10–30%** (up to 40% in some grey matter) vs a ~10× increase in muscle (DiNuzzo et al. 2024; Buxton 2021/2024) — hence a "hard upper limit." **[EST] Cost hierarchy:** evolutionarily expanded regions (PFC; central executive and default-mode networks) have the most neuromodulator receptors, highest perfusion, use **up to 67% more glucose**, and show highest mitochondrial enzyme activity (Castrillon et al. 2023; Farahani et al. 2025; Mosharov et al. 2025).
- **[EST — VERIFIED at primary] Chronic stress and MDD.** The paper states chronic stress raises MDD odds **>3.5-fold**, citing **Kendler, Karkowski & Prescott, *Am J Psychiatry* 1999;156:837–841** (doi:10.1176/ajp.156.6.837). Confirmed real and accurate: that paper reports OR for MDD onset in the month of a stressful life event of **5.64** (all subjects), **4.52** (dizygotic twin pairs), **3.58** (monozygotic twin pairs). ">3.5-fold" is the conservative (within-MZ) figure. *Caveat: Kendler 1999 measures stressful **life events**, not "chronic stress" as such — the paper's phrasing is a slight compression.*
- **[EST — VERIFIED at primary] Chronic stress and hypertension.** The paper states chronic stress "more than doubles the odds of developing hypertension," citing **Liu, Li, Li & Khan, *Neurol Res* 2017;39(6):573–580** (doi:10.1080/01616412.2017.1317904). Confirmed real: meta-analysis of 11 studies (5,696 participants) reported **OR = 2.40 (95% CI 1.65–3.49)** for psychosocial stress → hypertension. "More than doubles" is accurate.
- **[EST] Rationality of the "triage not growth" premise:** energy is finite and the brain's energy production has a hard ceiling (above), so reallocation rather than arbitrary upscaling is the only option at the systemic level. *(Framing, but grounded in the cited physiology.)*

*Not [EST] / flagged:* the specific *direction* of CNS triage (CEN→SN) and the identity "affect = model of metabolic efficiency" are the authors' inference, retained at **[HYP]** — the paper itself says the effects of affect on CNS allostatic triage *"have not been directly investigated"* and that peripheral dysregulation is *"not necessary"* for the mechanism.

## 5. Friction Rule — where the mechanism does NOT map cleanly onto EILT [DIVERGENCE]

1. **[DIVERGENCE] Currency mismatch.** The ATP model's currency is a *real, conserved, measurable thermodynamic budget* — ATP, tracked as the ATP/ADP ratio, MAL, AL, AOL. EILT's ledger abstraction has **no conserved scalar currency**; "epiplexity" measures learnable *structure*, not joules, and the house's "~20 W" is a total-power limit, not an ATP/ADP term the model actually computes with. "Triage = commit prioritization" is therefore an **analogy [A], not an identity** — EILT cannot derive the ATP/ADP ratio from its own primitives.
2. **[DIVERGENCE] Ranking key mismatch.** The paper ranks networks for triage by **predicted survival value + metabolic cost**, and explicitly warns triage is *"not necessitate[d]"* to run strictly expensive→cheap — it follows predicted outcomes. EILT's commit priority would track structural informativeness (epiplexity), a *different* ranking function. A pattern can be information-dense (high epiplexity) and evolutionarily low survival value; the two orderings are not the same.
3. **[DIVERGENCE] Scope mismatch.** The ATP model is a **psychopathology / transdiagnostic-disease model** scoped to CNS functional connectivity and psychiatric-cardiometabolic-immune comorbidity. It makes **no claim** about consciousness, informational ontology, identity, or ledgers — it does not touch EILT's metaphysical layer at all. Mapping it onto the EILT ledger is a scope extension *the paper does not authorize*.
4. **[CONSISTENT, but noted] Direction of causation.** The ATP model puts *energy* (a metabolic deficit) causally upstream of the affective/informational state — which matches the house's *already-revised* two-tier position and would conflict only with the house's *retracted* "energy = never causal" line. So this row is a non-friction: it lands on the house's corrected side.
5. **[DIVERGENCE] No irreversibility/commit semantics.** Canalization is "entrenchment via activity-dependent plasticity" — a graded, potentially reversible, thermodynamic process (they even discuss psychedelic/plasticity-based therapies that reverse it). EILT's ledger is append-only / non-rolling. Canalization is *not* an append-only commit, and the paper proposes it can be *un*-canalized. Do not read "canalization" as "irreversible ledger commit."
