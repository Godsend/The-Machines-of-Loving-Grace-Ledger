# For The First Time Ever, Quantum Spins Shift a Centimeter-Scale Object

**Source:** ScienceAlert (David Nield) / OIST (Nayak et al., *Science Advances* 2026)
**Date:** 2026-10-10

## Core Scientific Claim
Researchers at the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) demonstrated that quantum forces (electron spins) can produce a classical mechanical response in a centimeter-scale, 128-milligram macroscopic object. By levitating a graphite plate holding a diamond with nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers, they used laser light to manipulate the electrons' quantum spins, generating enough force to physically shift the entire centimeter-scale assembly by roughly 100 nanometers.
