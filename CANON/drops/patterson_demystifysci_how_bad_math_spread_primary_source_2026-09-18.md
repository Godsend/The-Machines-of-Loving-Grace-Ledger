# Patterson (DemystifySci) — "How Bad Math Spread to Physics & Beyond": primary-source grounding

## Purpose
The Gemini share claimed Steve Patterson's critique gives EILT "the exact epistemological weapon" for the Abstraction Fallacy / discrete-ledger / death-of-continuum threads. I pulled the real transcript and checked every claim against what Patterson actually said. Verdict: the share's claims are **faithfully quoted** on every point — but the share's REGISTER TAGS overstate strength in one place that matters.

## What Patterson really says (all verified in full transcript)
- **Low math vs high math** [O → H]: "It's not that I think math is flawed. It's that high math is flawed. Low math, the things that computers are doing, the crude things that you know, actual computation, that's the real math." Also: "if that's the real math, we have to revise our high theories of math." Faithful.
- **Death of the continuum / discrete universe** [H → C]: Patterson explicitly proposes "a pixelated voxel universe," "location is a block of space," "the universe is like a sand-filled chamber." Zeno's runner "can't complete an infinite process" → "there's a smallest possible step." THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING CLAIM.
- **0.999… = 1 is "logically contradictory"** [O] — explicitly heterodox, self-flagged: hosts joke "now that we've declared we're cranks in the mathematical." Patterson: "this is precisely the heart of the issue."
- **Formalism traded truth for usefulness** (Cantor's infinite sets, foundational crisis, set theory as the "bedrock" error) [O → H].
- **Abstraction / zoom-in fallacy** [H]: "there are abstraction layers that aren't just ways of talking… real downward causality… zooming in is not the way to learn more… you have to zoom out." Faithful to the share's "Abstraction Fallacy."

## The register correction the share hid
The Gemini share tagged the continuum/death-of-the-container as **[T → H] ("Spacetime is Discrete [T → H]")** — implying a theorem-grade conclusion. That is WRONG on two counts:
1. **Patterson is a philosopher** (Natural Philosophy Institute / Patterson in Pursuit), not a physicist or mathematician. His discrete-universe proposal is a **metaphysical hypothesis** the episode itself treats as heterodox ("we're cranks in the mathematical"). It is [C]/[H] at best — Collins-list-adjacent, not theorem.
2. **The hosts openly push back** — "are you saying that's not actually a claim about space?" The smaller-step answer to Zeno is a live philosophical dispute, not an established result.
3. The "universe = discrete step-by-step computation" reading ([H]) is a faithful paraphrase but a **mapping choice**, not Patterson's formalism — he offers no math.

Only **Ravera/DFM** earns [T] on the death-of-the-container in canon (published, peer-reviewed, primary math). Patterson supplies the accessible-language [H]/[A] framing, not proof.
