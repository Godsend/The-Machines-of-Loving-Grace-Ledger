The filaments are the extended surface structure; the electron is *"the motion that surface fiber is doing… the circulation of that surface material,"* not a point charge `[QUOTE, L16-L17]`. - **How it would be detected:** *"My intuition is that the experiments have already been done… there is a level at which they filter data… we probably have a lot of information already encoded about the way that the filaments work but right now is being thrown away as just noise… especially for some of these single photon entanglement experiments."* `[QUOTE, L206-L207, 107:06-107:38]` — **a re-analysis proposal, not a specified measurement.**

### 3.3 The mechanism: "twist and tumble" — c from medium parameters

## 4. THE "NO NEW MATHEMATICS" PROBLEM — do they make one novel, checkable prediction?

**Their own explicit statement:** *"we're complementing the mathematics. We're not trying to quibble with the mathematics… we find most of them to be quite empirically defended. We're just trying to give physical form to what could be happening down there."* `[QUOTE, L223, 115:21-115:56]`. **This is an interpretation, on their own description.** An interpretation that changes no prediction cannot be distinguished from the standard account empirically — which is legitimate *as an interpretation*, and **cannot** be sold as a *mechanistic replacement*. The book's own framing ("no new mathematics required, instead vanquishes the paradoxes") is the correct description of an **interpretive re-description**, and the marketing register ("we have done it") is not.

**Do they make ANY novel checkable prediction? Partially — ONE, and it is a known-losing horse.**

**(i) Cosmological redshift = gradual dissipative loss of light's energy to the filament medium ("tired light").** `[QUOTE, L182-L183, 94:01-95:05]` and `[QUOTE, L210-L212, 109:10-110:13]`: *"you can probably model cosmological red shift as being caused by this kind of gradual dissipation of energy as light travels through the medium over very long distances"*; *"the difference between us and the [extant tired-light] models is that they're going to be trying to involve atomic means by which light gets re-energized. We're saying that no, over extraordinary distances you are actually losing energy to the actual fibrous interactions… You actually have a form of heat going into the atoms."*
- **This is a real, checkable divergence** from standard cosmology (expanding-space redshift, exactly non-dissipative in the sense needed). **But it is a prediction of a class that is already observationally constrained**, and the hosts *say so themselves* before offering theirs: *"there's like 20 different tired light models… They keep getting shot down every time somebody publishes one."* `[QUOTE, L211]`. The host-acknowledged kill list is the house's own kill list: **surface-brightness (Tolman) test**, **SN Ia light-curve time dilation**, and the **CMB blackbody spectrum** (a dissipative medium thermalises/degrades a perfect Planck spectrum). **They add no mechanism that evades any of these named constraints** — the only delta they claim is *where* the energy goes (filament heat vs atomic re-emission), which does not touch surface brightness or time dilation. **A tired-light claim without a surface-brightness answer is refuted-by-default.**
- **Second, softer, candidate:** *"we've defined mass in terms of the number of filament connections that a material body can make to the network"* `[QUOTE, L83, 42:44-43:14]` — if **inertial mass ∝ local filament connectivity**, a checkable consequence would be a mass/anisotropy dependence on local filament density. **Not developed, not quantified, no experiment proposed.** `[HYP]` at best.

**(ii) Stated detection proposal (not a prediction):** re-analyse *raw, unfiltered* single-photon / entanglement data for structure discarded as noise `[QUOTE, L206-L207]`. Direction of an experiment, with no specified signature and no specified filtering stage — not falsifiable as stated.

**(iii) Explanatory-not-predictive list:** asked what the filament model explains that the photon model does not, they answer **the paradoxes** — wave–particle duality, apparent FTL in entanglement, the three-polariser paradox, observer-dependent double-slit `[QUOTE, L220, 113:46-114:19]`. These are *interpretive* gains, not predictive. **Verdict: the "no new mathematics" claim is theirs and is accurate; the falsifiability footprint is one already-constrained tired-light claim plus one undeveloped mass hypothesis.**
