## 6. Falsifiers (pre-registered)

- **F-ROCK-1 (house-facing).** If the population of venture deal documents **fails** to converge on the 1968 convertible-note-with-vesting structure (e.g., SAFEs, revenue-based financing, and rolling funds come to dominate early-stage), then R1's "persisted constraining template" is a description of one era, not a structural ledger, and R1 collapses to "one influential document."

- **F-ROCK-3 (the anti-necessity check).** The tape claims contingency (*"none of this was inevitable"*). If a counterfactual world **without** the 1957/1968 deals still produces Silicon Valley's institutional shape at similar timing, the contingency thesis is wrong and the video's own engine weakens — but note this would **not** rescue a physics-necessity reading; it would only relocate the contingency.
- **F-ROCK-4 (§24-facing).** If the shape of **what got funded** (R4's "rewarded set") tracks the shape of **what was technologically possible** (a forbidden set) closely enough that the two are not separable, then "forbidden ≠ rewarded" is false and R4 should be downgraded — but the burden is on showing the funded set equals the possible set, which the tape's own *"problems that never got solved"* [43:47] denies.
