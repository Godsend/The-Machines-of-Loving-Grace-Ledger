## 3. Pushback (the joints that do not hold)

1. **The observer loop.** Observer assumes persistence → observer equivalences states → merging → single coherent thread → which is the persistence the observer assumed. Wolfram presents this as what "holds the ruliad together". It may be a consistent fixed point, but as stated it is circular, and no independent constraint is offered that would break it. Falsifier he owes: an observer property that *fails* to yield a self-consistent physics.
2. **"Derivable" is overclaimed in this register.** Nothing is derived in the video; derivability is reported. His written work derives Einstein-like equations *from hypergraph rewriting with assumptions about causal-graph statistics*, not from observer boundedness alone. The honest statement is "derivable within the model given the substrate", far weaker than "a different observer would believe different physics".
3. **Unfalsifiable by design, and he knows it.** The ruliad is arithmetic-grade necessity; no observation bears on it. All empirical content sits in the observer-derivation program, and the only near-testable item there is the 3-D-space guess, explicitly unfinished. Register the whole as **framework, not theory**.
4. **Tooley cuts straight through the theology move.** Filed this morning: proofs of a necessary being deliver no values. Ruliad-as-God is Tooley's false-advertising case in its purest form — omni-computational and value-free. Kuhn led Wolfram there; Wolfram accepted the analogy; the same objection lands.
