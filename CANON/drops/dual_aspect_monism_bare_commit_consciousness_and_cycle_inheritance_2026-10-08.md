## 2. Verified External Anchors

### Anchor A — Penrose's Erebons (CONFIRMED, WITH A CORRECTION)
`[EST-FACT]` Penrose, R. (2017), **"Correlated 'noise' in LIGO gravitational wave signals: an implication of Conformal Cyclic Cosmology,"** arXiv:1707.04169 [gr-qc], 12 Jul 2017. Claims, from the paper itself:
- Dark matter consists of **erebons**: scalar (spin-0), ~Planck-mass (~10⁻⁵ g) particles interacting only gravitationally, behaving effectively classically; "a reasonably robust implication of CCC."
- Each erebon decay converts the full Planck mass-energy into an impulsive classical gravitational wave (~10⁴³ Hz oscillation, registering in detectors as an instantaneous impulse easily mistaken for noise) — offered as the explanation for the Cresswell et al. (arXiv:1706.04191) correlated-noise puzzle in GW150914-era LIGO data.
- **The decay-timing point (checked directly, §4 of the paper):** erebons are born in *each* aeon when the Ω (conformal-factor) field acquires mass just after that aeon's big bang, and **"these particles have ultimately to decay, in CCC, since we do not want them to build up from aeon to aeon."** Erebons decay BEFORE the crossover; they do not carry through it. What crosses is conformally invariant information: the normal derivative of the Weyl tensor across the crossover surface X, carried in higher derivatives of the Ω-field (electric part) and the Cotton tensor of X (magnetic part). The previous aeon's erebon *decays* source the near-scale-invariant CMB temperature fluctuations of the next aeon (CCC's inflation replacement).

### Anchor B — Smolin's Cosmological Natural Selection (CONFIRMED)
`[EST-FACT]` Smolin, L., "Did the universe evolve?", *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 9 (1992) 173–191; popularized in *The Life of the Cosmos* (Oxford University Press, 1997); falsifiability treatment in **"Using neutron stars and primordial black holes to test cosmological natural selection"** (arXiv:hep-ph/9712189). Mechanism: (1) black-hole singularities bounce into new expanding spacetime regions, one per black hole; (2) at each bounce the dimensionless standard-model parameters undergo **small random changes** from the parent values — inheritance plus mutation. Prediction: our parameters sit near a local maximum of black-hole production (almost any small change should *decrease* it; by one count ~40 independent parameter-direction tests). Explicit falsifier stated by Smolin: observation of a neutron star with mass **> 2.5 M☉** "would cleanly refute the theory" (contingent on his kaon-condensate equation-of-state argument). `[EST-FACT]` Status flag: the heaviest well-measured pulsars (~2.1 M☉) sit below the bound, but GW190814's ~2.6 M☉ secondary (black hole or neutron star, unresolved) presses on exactly this joint.

### Anchor C — Chalmers' Double-Aspect Information (CONFIRMED)
`[EST-FACT]` Chalmers, D.J., **"Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness,"** *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 2(3):200–219 (1995), section "The double-aspect theory of information"; elaborated in *The Conscious Mind* (OUP, 1996). ## 4. The DAM Honest Limit

`[STRUCTURAL-LIMIT]` Dual-aspect monism (Chalmers' double-aspect principle included) **names** the two-aspect relationship; it does not **derive** why the inner aspect exists at all. Chalmers concedes this in the 1995 paper (speculative, underdetermined, "key questions unanswered" — e.g. whether all information or only some has a phenomenal aspect). That gap is precisely what the bare-commit account (§1.2) targets: the inner aspect as what commitment-from-inside *is*. But as of this drop, the bare-commit account is `[HYP]` — it relocates the gap to "why does committing have an inside?", which is progress in specificity, not yet a derivation.

## 5. Falsifiable Joint

`[FALSIFIABLE-JOINT]` **Penrose's erebon observables — with disputed replication reported honestly.** The 2017 paper makes concrete, in-principle-cheap predictions: directionally correlated impulsive "noise" in paired GW detectors pointing at any dark-matter-rich galaxy (Penrose: "settle on some prominent galaxy — say Andromeda — and look for correlations… it should not be hard to disprove it if it is wrong"), plus previous-aeon signatures (concentric low-variance circles, later Hawking points) in the CMB. Current status, all sides: (1) the Cresswell et al. noise-correlation claim that motivated the paper was itself disputed and largely deflated in subsequent LIGO-side analyses; (2) the CMB-circle claims (Gurzadyan–Penrose 2013; An–Meissner–Nurowski 2013) are disputed — DeAbreu, Contreras & Scott, *JCAP* 12 (2015) 031 find no significant anomaly, a dispute Penrose's own paper cites as ref [10]; (3) an independent Max-Planck-hosted calculation of the stochastic GW background from Planck-mass particle decay finds Ω_GW(40 Hz) < 7×10⁻¹⁴⁴ — ~136 orders of magnitude below LIGO sensitivity — undercutting the specific LIGO-noise mechanism. The joint stands as *formally falsifiable and currently unfavored*. Secondary joint: Smolin's 2.5 M☉ neutron-star bound remains live and is the cleanest selection-loop falsifier on the table.
