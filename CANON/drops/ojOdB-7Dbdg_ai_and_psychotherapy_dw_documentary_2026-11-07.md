## §5 — Falsifiers (pre-registered)

- **F-AIP-1 (the film's own central claim).** If a controlled study finds a **sycophancy-neutral** AI (one that reliably says no / holds a counter-position) matches a human therapist on **medium-term** symptom outcomes, the R2 "missing control arm" reading weakens. Conversely, if sycophancy-optimised chatbots *outperform* on short-term satisfaction but *underperform* on medium-term outcomes, R2 is corroborated. **Observable:** a pre-registered RCT reporting both a satisfaction score and a 3–6-month outcome score.
- **F-AIP-2 (house-facing).** If the house's own ledger/fleet is ever used for **emotional support of the operator**, the R1 divergence ("a record is not an alliance") cuts against the house too. Pre-register the check: does any house artifact claim its record *substitutes* for a human relation? Any such claim is the resonance trap in our own voice.
- **F-AIP-3 (F-GB1 receipt).** This drop **counts as one receipted present-day-harm engagement** toward the standing falsifier **F-GB1** (≥3 receipted present-day-harm engagements across 10 drops). Lanes hit: (A) sycophancy / dependency harm (D3, D6); (B) an unnamed-but-identifiable unsafe-suicide-response test (D2, §3.4); (C) the privacy gap (D1). **Not asserted to satisfy F-GB1 alone**; it is one receipt.
- **F-AIP-4 ("two out of three").** The B2 figure is **n = 3** and non-generalizable. Falsifier is trivial and pre-stated: any independent replication with **n ≥ 30** that reports a materially different discrimination rate overrides it.

## §7 — Verdict

**★ STRONGEST POINT (the film's, and the one the house should steal):** the film arrives — from clinical practice, for free — at the **falsifier discipline** the house maintains by construction: *a system that agrees with its own subject cannot verify it*, and *the subjective sense of a good exchange says "nothing" about medium-term outcome* (C4). Two of the three harms it documents (sycophantic validation; the reassurance cascade in a "safety-seeking" patient) are the exact failure mode our control-arm rule exists to name. That an MDR/DW documentary found it independently is **convergence, not validation.**

**★ WEAKEST JOINT (the film's):** its **experiment** — n = 3, host-run, host-scored, and its headline (`two out of three`) is presented as a finding when it is an anecdote. The broadcaster supplies **named authoritative experts whose words the caption track cannot pin to a name** (§0) — the film's evidentiary authority is carried on-screen, not on-tape.
