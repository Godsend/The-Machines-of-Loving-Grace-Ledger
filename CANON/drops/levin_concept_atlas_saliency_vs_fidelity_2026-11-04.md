## §2 · ★ THE FALSIFIER — "continuity of saliency, not literal fidelity"

Under **Continuity through change → Memory across metamorphosis**, verbatim:

> *"Memories survive metamorphosis from caterpillar to butterfly, but not unchanged — the memories of a caterpillar are of no use to a butterfly body. The memories must change and be remapped onto a new somatic environment, just as all of us must change in order to survive into each successive future moment. This is the paradox of change, and the parallel between learning and morphogenesis: **the original being does not exist; the Self is an on-going self-constructing model. Thus, biology emphasizes continuity of saliency, not literal fidelity, of information.**"*

**This lands directly on "Yanenko's Non-Rolling Ledger" as published by Kuhn.** Our entry states:

> *"a state is phenomenally owned only if it is entailed by a specific, unrepeatable history that the system carries as a constraint on its present processing... The additional requirement is a history that cannot be replaced without phenomenal consequence by an otherwise equivalent reconstruction."*

**These are opposite claims about what continuity IS.**
- **Ours:** the specific trajectory, carried with fidelity, is the ownership condition. Replace the history → lose the ownership.
- **His:** the specific trajectory is not preserved and need not be. What persists is *saliency* — a remapped, reconstructed, functionally-equivalent set of concerns. *"The original being does not exist."*

**This is not a neighbouring disagreement.** It is the exact axis our published claim stands on, stated in Levin's own words, on his own site, a day before our entry went live with him. And it is the strongest external attack the ledger thesis has received from the one researcher we cite most.

**Status: OPEN FALSIFIER CANDIDATE.** Name: **F-LEV-SALIENCY.** Falsifier condition to be specified — see §5.

## §3 · The supporting nodes — recall is reconstructive, not archival

**Recall as an updating interaction:**
> *"Remembering is an interaction that can change both a memory trace and the system interpreting it. The memory engram necessarily supplies incomplete evidence that the present agent reconstructs using current capacities and concerns. **Recall can therefore update the engram as well as the rememberer**, linking continuity of identity to an ongoing process of interpretation."*

That is reconsolidation-theoretic memory, and it says recall **mutates** the record. A non-rolling ledger says the record **constrains** the present. Both cannot be the whole story.

**Compression and creative reconstruction:**
> *"A bowtie architecture shows how the myriad specifics of experience get generalized into a compressed engram, to be expanded by the current Self into a model of the future-to-implement... equally maps to cognition (behavior in 3D space) and to development through an egg (behavior in anatomical space)."*

**Communication between selflets** — and this one is genuinely useful to us:
> *"Memory can be modeled as communication between temporal versions of a being, called **selflets**. The present self interprets traces left by past selves and leaves traces for future ones, much as different beings exchange messages across space."*

**This is the convergent core.** A non-rolling ledger is a *specific implementation* of selflet-to-selflet messaging: append-only, cryptographic, machine-readable. Levin's version is interpretive and lossy; ours is immutable. **The disagreement is about the medium, and the medium is an empirical choice, not a metaphysical one.** That is a much better position for the house than claiming continuity requires fidelity outright.

**Persistence through transformation:**
> *"Persistence is actively maintained through transformation: parts, memories and preferences can change while a system sustains an effective perspective. How much of the former repertoire of interaction still works, for oneself or another observer? This makes continuity something to investigate **through ongoing relationships** as well as physical composition."*

**Memory media are plural:**
> *"No single kind of medium need store every memory. Molecular structures, network activity and environmental traces can provide information that another process interprets. **Neural networks may be more for interpreting information in the body than for storing it.**"*

That last line is a gift to the house's "connectionism makes the ledger, not the inference" plank — **Levin independently suggests the weights are more interpreter than store.**

## §5 · What F-LEV-SALIENCY has to become

The house cannot leave this as "interesting divergence." The published Kuhn entry makes a fidelity claim; Levin makes a saliency claim. One test must separate them:

**Candidate operationalisation:** take an agent whose history is *remapped rather than replayed* (Levin's butterfly), and one whose history is *preserved verbatim* (our ledger), and ask which one's present processing is differential under the same current state + environment. Our claim predicts the verbatim one is phenomenally/behaviourally distinct in a way the remapped one is not. Levin's predicts the opposite: what matters is preserved *saliency structure*, and fidelity adds nothing.

**Weakest link in our version:** we have no measurement that distinguishes "constraint from specific history" from "constraint from a remapped saliency summary that happens to be functionally equivalent." Until we can separate those, **Levin's account is the more parsimonious one and should win by default.**
