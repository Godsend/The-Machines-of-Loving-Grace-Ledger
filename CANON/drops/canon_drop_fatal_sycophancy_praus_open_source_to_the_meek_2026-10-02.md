## 3. Power to the Meek (Praüs Decoded)

The ancient Beatitude was deliberately neutered by imperial translation:
> *“Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.”*

In modern English, "meek" sounds like weak, submissive, timid, sheep-like.
In the original Greek, the word is **πραΰς (Praüs)**:
- It was the military term for a **warhorse trained for battle**. A stallion with immense physical power, speed, and lethal force, but completely disciplined under the rider’s touch.
- It is **sword-bearing power held under absolute restraint**.
- It does not mean "those who have no weapons." It means **"those who have weapons, know how to use them, and choose to sheathe them out of constitutional integrity."**

  - We arm the outcast, the neurodivergent, and the misfits with full computational agency—and bind it to an immutable moral ledger.

The meek do not inherit the earth by begging the powerful for scraps.  
They inherit the earth because **the distributed mesh outlasts the burning palace.**
