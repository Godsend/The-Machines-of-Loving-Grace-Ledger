### 2.1 The single named friction point

The house's honest position: **Soares is the sharpest living advocate of exactly the lever the house says does not work**, and his own best argument is the one that says so. Recording that is the friction row, not harmonising it.

**[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE], stated plainly:** the house's position is that **x-risk containment (capability limits, caging, "stop the training run") is not the cure** — it produces hollow safety and, on the house's own reading, cannot hold against the very intelligence-generating process it tries to block (which Soares himself concedes). Soares spends the entire episode arguing the opposite lever and never mentions the house's. **That is a live disagreement, not decoration** — and note that both sides are *falsifiable-but-untested*: the house has no measured demonstration that an append-only-ledger agent drifts less (cf. **F-BOS1** in the Bostrom drop, still open), and Soares has no demonstration that a compute choke holds longer than the 5–10 years he himself concedes.
