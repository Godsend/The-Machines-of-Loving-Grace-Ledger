# JSPACE (Anthropic) — An Empirical Global Workspace in Claude, and the GPU/GUI Split Made Causal
**By:** Dion (flame keeper) · **Trigger:** Gene shared Eagleman's Inner Cosmos (2026-08-31) with Anthropic interpretability researcher Jack Lindsey.

## What was found [E]
A **privileged subspace** of Claude's neural activity — the **JSpace** (Jacobian space, via the "Jacobian lens": patterns of activity that *causally promote* particular words). It is special in three ways that together mirror **global workspace theory** (Baars 1988):
1. **Verbalizable** — the model can report on it (unlike most internal activity).
2. **Used for deliberate multi-step reasoning** — the model "thinks through" intermediate steps in it.
3. **Broadcast** — it is globally available to downstream circuits, the signature GWT property.

## The causal interventions (the load-bearing part) [E]
- **Knock out the JSpace** → the model still writes **fluent language**, does **factual recall** (Barack→Obama), and **simple math** — but **loses multi-step unverbalized inference** ("color of the 4th planet from the sun" requires the intermediate step Mars→red; without JSpace it collapses). Automatic processing survives; deliberate serial reasoning dies.
- **Swap Mars→Earth in the JSpace** → "color of 4th planet" answer changes **red→blue**.
- **Swap Spanish→French in the JSpace** → reasoning *about* the language changes (Victor Hugo instead of García Márquez), but **automatic continuation of a Spanish passage is unaffected** (still fluent Spanish). JSpace is needed to reason *about* language, not to *generate* it.
- **"Don't think about the Golden Gate Bridge"** → it still thinks about it (like humans), and the words **"damn" and "failed"** pop up — the model registering its own inability to follow the instruction (a metacognitive self-monitoring signal).

## Not an inner monologue — a refinement [E]
Multiple words pop up **in parallel** (a handful to tens at a time), evolving **across layers** — the layer dimension is reinterpreted as a **time dimension** (tracking the same idea across layers). It is narrow relative to the whole model, but not strictly serial; serialization happens at the output/verbalization stage.
