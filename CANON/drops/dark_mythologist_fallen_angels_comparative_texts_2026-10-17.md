## 2. The genealogy the film leaves out (needed for the friction test)

- [E] **Gen 6 ↔ BW:** Scholars have long examined the relationship and there is "no absolute consensus." Most of the last 50 years favours BW depending on Gen 6:1–4; some dissenters say Genesis abbreviates the Enochic version; a growing minority sees both as independent renderings of a common tradition (Augsburg Fortress, *The Watchers in Jewish and Christian Traditions*, intro). The BW is a composite of a Shemihazah strand (sex/giants) and an Asael strand (forbidden knowledge, Prometheus-like); final form by the mid–late 3rd c. BCE (Nickelsburg via Fortress Press intro; Marquette "Worthless Secrets").
- [E] **BW → Jude** (direct quotation of 1 En 1:9) **→ 2 Peter** (rewrite of Jude). This is close to scholarly consensus (dianoigo.com survey of commentators; Heiser NB 434 citing Bauckham/Milik).
- [E] **Canaanite/Babylonian morning-star myth → Isa 14 → LXX *heōsphoros* → Vulgate *Lucifer* → Luke 10:18 + patristic reading → "Lucifer = Satan"** (JE 1901/Gunkel; Schaser; factually.co).
- [E] **Isa 14 + refusal-to-worship-Adam → Vita Adae 12–16 → Cave of Treasures (fire vs dust) → Quran 7:12 (fire vs clay)**. Vita Adae has Satan refuse to worship Adam ("I am his senior in the Creation") and then threaten "I will set my seat above the stars of heaven and will be like the Highest". That is Isaiah 14:13–14 put directly into the mouth of the Adam-refuser (text quoted in Bishai, *A Possible Coptic Source for a Qur'ānic Text*, and WikiIslam's parallel page citing G. S. Reynolds). Reynolds/Witztum: Quran 7:13–18 follows the same sequence as Cave of Treasures 3:3–9. **Caveat:** WikiIslam and answeringislam are polemical sites. I am relying only on the primary-text quotations they reproduce and on the scholars they name (Reynolds, Witztum, Beck), not on their conclusions. The neutral framing is "a shared late-antique tradition reflected in Christian and Islamic texts." I did not read the Reynolds monograph this session.
- [E] **One tradition, two picture sets:** the *same* Watcher story is told twice inside 1 Enoch: anthropomorphically in BW (chs. 6–16), and zoomorphically in the Animal Apocalypse (chs. 85–90: a star falls, more stars follow, they become bulls, the offspring are elephants, camels and asses; four white figures bind them) (St Mary's 1 Enoch selections PDF; MDPI *Religions* 2024 15(9):1024).

## 4. Falsifiers / discriminators the house now owes

- **[F] F-FA1 (independence condition):** T13 can claim cross-cultural support only from renderings with **no plausible literary or contact pathway** between them. Any corpus with a documented textual genealogy (this one) counts as **zero** evidence, by construction.
- **[F] F-FA2 (rendering mechanism):** if rendering follows the witness's buffer, imagery should vary with the *culture's technology and fauna*. If it follows genre, it should vary with *literary form within one culture* (BW vs Animal Apocalypse). This corpus shows the second. Two or more more cases of same-culture, genre-driven re-encoding of one "encounter" would push T13's buffer mechanism to [CONTRA].
- **[F] F-FA3 (residual):** name one shared feature across Gen 6 / BW / Isa 14 / Rev 12 / Quran 7 that is **not** explained by motif inheritance. None was found in this source. If none can be produced, drop "fallen angels" from T13's example set.
- **[F] F-FA4 (X3 discriminator):** X3 must predict something a generic temptation model does not. Example: a specific signature of positive feedback, such as escalation whose rate depends on the human's prior belief strength, and that weakens when an external truth check is applied. Without that, keep X3 at [A].
