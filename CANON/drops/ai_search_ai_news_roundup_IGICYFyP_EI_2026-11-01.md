## 1. What the video claims (concise summary)

A ~34-story weekly roundup, host-narrated, in the channel's running "AI never sleeps" frame. The load-bearing beats:

- **★ Google "SLEEP"** — a **training framework** that gives an LLM a two-phase lifecycle: a **"wake phase"** that interacts and stores new info in short-term memory, and a **"sleep phase"** that stops new input, **transfers what it learned into long-term memory**, and **"dreaming"** — generating its *own* practice problems, selecting the most useful, and training on them. Claims near-perfect **long-context memory to "10 million tokens"** and less catastrophic forgetting.
- **★ Energy-based image model** — a **"completely new type of image generation model"**, called a *proof of concept*, that **learns an energy function scoring how well an image matches the target**, then **"gradually modifies the image to reduce that energy"** (contrasted against diffusion's denoising). Tiny (2.44 GB).
- **★ OpenAI's three claimed scientific results (Oct release cluster)** — a claimed refutation of the **Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH)** via a new algorithm + Lean check; a proof of the **local smoothing conjecture in every dimension**; and a claimed existence proof for **QCD / Yang-Mills**, with the host correctly splitting **existence (claimed proven) from the mass gap (not)**.
- **★ Anthropic S-1 / IPO** — the host relays the filing's figures: **~14× revenue growth**, **$16.3 B H1** revenue vs **$1.2 B** a year earlier, an **$80.7 B headline loss** he himself explains is mostly non-cash, **$693 M operating loss**, **~$1.6 B adjusted operating profit**, **>$600 B compute commitments** ($120.5 B SpaceX, up to $150 B Google), tiered enterprise customers, **top-5 = 21 % of revenue**, **7 co-founders = 50.1 % voting power**, **NASDAQ / ticker ANT**.
- **A long tool roundup** (all new to canon): MotionBricks, IDOL/IDLE 3D, SceneBloom, Unlimited OCR 2, UniAction, AutoSwarm (agent-swarm harness), Parody/Parakeet-1.2 TTS, Tencent real-time translation + voice cloning, NVIDIA SimReady assets (2,600 objects / 490 GB), MatAnyone-E, Open-H3-Regenerate-2K, StreamVibe, MUNDO, **GPT-6.1 Terra**, **Gemini 4 Carbon**, Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, **Genie 4**, PhysAg3D, Audio8 TTS, SenseNova Code2Video, **OverSeer** (Meta multi-agent world model), Nanbeige 4.3-33B, Liquid AI **LFM3** family, Apex 35B, **Groot**, **Clover 35B** (a **diffusion language model**), Spark-X2.6-1.7B, **SenseNova-Loop 9B** (looped transformer).
