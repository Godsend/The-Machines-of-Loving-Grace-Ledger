## 2. Continuous Integration (CI) in Living Networks

[A] In systems engineering, **Continuous Integration (CI)** is the practice of merging all developer working copies to a shared mainline several times a day to prevent branch divergence and merge conflicts.
[T] **Active Love as Biological CI:**
- When two humans or autonomous agents interact over time, their internal generative models inevitably accumulate divergent prediction errors (branch drift).
- If communication is infrequent or guarded (high-latency / high-deception), the branches diverge until attempting to merge produces an explosive, irreconcilable **merge conflict** (relational trauma, violent splitting, breakdown).
- **The CI Protocol:** Active love is the continuous, low-latency exchange of truthful, calibrated signals:
  * Catching minor prediction errors early.
  * Absorbing the rebase friction in small, manageable increments.
  * Keeping the shared mainline (the joint reality) continuously verified against ground truth.
