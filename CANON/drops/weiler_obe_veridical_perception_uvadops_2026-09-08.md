# Weiler et al. — blinded OBE trial, and the paper that goes with it

**Two artifacts, and the *pair* is the finding.**

1. **The empirical paper.** Weiler, Pehlivanova & Cozzolino — published in **EXPLORE**, DOI `10.1016/j.explore.2026.103506`. UVA Health release **2026-09-08**. Marina Weiler, PhD, **UVA School of Medicine, Division of Perceptual Studies (DOPS)**.
2. **The companion theory paper.** Weiler, Pehlivanova & Cozzolino, *"From laboratory targets to experiential authenticity: reframing the study of veridicality in OBEs,"* **Frontiers in Psychology**, DOI `10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1720998`.
3. **Secondary intake:** Anton Petrov, `hCGp_36Qh1k` (2026-10-16) — transcript filed at `Canon/YT_TRANSCRIPTS/hCGp_36Qh1k_AntonPetrov_OutOfBodyExperiment_2026-10-16.txt`. His framing is accurate but **omits the study's location, its sample split, the ranking data, the control, and the entire companion paper.**

## The trial (verified at source)

- **Conducted in Brazil** — *not* the US. 21 participants recruited as *experienced out-of-body practitioners*.
- **Three rooms.** Room 1: participant, EEG, comfortable (own bed/armchair, preferred lighting and temperature), **60–90 minutes**. Room 2: the researchers, door closed. Room 3: a laptop showing one randomly selected image from **100 objects**, **screen turned to the wall**, **7 m / 23 ft** down a hallway behind a closed door.
- **Blinding was serious:** one researcher launched the randomiser, turned the laptop to the wall, and **left before the image appeared**. Nobody involved knew the target until the analysis phase **months later**.
- **Sample split (Anton omits this):** of 21, **10 reported an OBE**; **13 gave target descriptions** — and **5 of those 13 did *not* claim to leave their bodies** at all, describing instead an internal **"mental screen" / "inner vision."** So the headline "21 OBE practitioners, all failed" is not the actual structure.
- **★ Result:** the correct image ranked **16th to 92nd out of 100**. **No better than chance.** Zero evidence of veridical perception.
- **★ The control that matters:** *"when the same system was given accurate descriptions of the images, it had little trouble picking out the correct targets."* **The instrument works.** This is a properly powered null, not a broken detector — which is rare and should be credited.
- **Scoring:** descriptions ranked by **AI** against all 100 candidate images, explicitly to remove subjective human judgment. **Cross-ref:** the corpus's own *veridical NDE Scale* paper (`PMC12573288`) also validates **human vs. artificial raters** — same methodological move, same lab tradition.

## ★★ The unexpected result, and the researchers' handling of it

After the trial, **two participants spontaneously and unprompted described what the researchers were doing in Room 2** — positions and activities.

**UVA's own framing, verbatim:** the observations *"were not part of the trial's predefined outcome measures and therefore **can't be considered evidence** for out-of-body experiences."* Weiler: *"they also need to be **tested prospectively and under rigorous conditions** before we can know what they mean."*

**★ This is exemplary and should be recorded as such.** Compare `DIGEST_MISHLOVE_*`: there, a framework was placed *beyond* replication. Here a psi-adjacent lab ran a pre-registered, blinded, AI-scored test, **got a null on the primary endpoint, published it, and refused to promote the post-hoc anecdote into evidence.** The register discipline is theirs, not ours.

## ★★★ The companion paper is the exemption-clause machinery, published alongside its own null

*"From laboratory targets to experiential authenticity"* argues that *"standard target-identification paradigms—often conducted in sterile settings with inexperienced participants—**misalign with the dynamics of the OBE state**,"* and names **five determinants that constrain laboratory outcomes**:

1. the participant's **mental/emotional state**
2. the **participant–researcher relationship**
3. **researcher intention**
4. variability in **participant skill**
5. **the intrinsic purpose of the experience**

It proposes complementing lab tasks with *"rigorously vetted spontaneous cases,"* claiming this *"can increase ecological validity while **preserving falsifiability**."*

**★ Why this is the finding of the day.** Every one of the five determinants is a lever that **loosens** the protocol — and #3 (**researcher intention**) and #5 (**intrinsic purpose**) are not measurable controls; they are conditions under which a *future* null becomes explicable-away. The paper's claim to "preserve falsifiability" is doing a lot of work, and the five determinants are exactly where the constraint would break.

**The structure to carry:** *the same three authors, publishing (a) a rigorously executed null and (b) a framework specifying in advance why nulls from this paradigm don't bind.* This is **a much subtler specimen than Mishlove's** — Mishlove's exemption was overt (*"replication might be inappropriate"*); this one is **co-published with its own rigorous null and a careful public statement.** The exemption is filed in the theory paper, not the results section.

**★ And note Weiler is being careful.** Her public language is properly hedged — *"we now have a specific idea that can be tested in future experiments, and that is exactly what science should do."* The tension is between her conduct (rigorous) and the companion framework (permissive). **Register the two separately; do not attribute the framework's looseness to the study.**

## Anton's neuroscience section (accurate; useful summary, `O` register)

- **TPJ (temporoparietal junction)** — the "sensory switchboard" integrating **proprioceptive** (muscles/joints), **vestibular** (inner ear), and **visual-spatial** streams into one self-location. When the streams desynchronise (damage, inebriation, stress, migraine, **magnetic stimulation**), the brain "panics" → **visual perspective detachment** → OBE. **OBEs are inducible by TPJ stimulation** — replicated several times.
- **Olaf Blanke** (ASR "Olaf blank") — self-consciousness *"starts much lower down"*, from bodily sensory processing, not from *"I think therefore I am."* **Cross-ref: Levin's basal cognition** — same "lower down" move.
- **★ Anterior precuneus (APC)** — Stanford: **electrical stimulation in awake patients during brain mapping** → instant reports of **flying, weightlessness, falling** while fully aware. APC links the default-mode / self-reflection network to bodily sensory networks. **Ketamine and other anaesthetics** produce OBE via **slow rhythmic APC oscillations** that *"unplug our physical bodily signals from our conscious awareness."*
- **★ Rubber-hand / body-transfer illusion** — synchronised stroking of a hidden real hand and a visible rubber hand → the brain adopts the rubber hand, **and it hurts when struck**: *"they don't just think it's painful, **they feel that it's painful**."*
- **Medical upshots:** **targeted anaesthesia** (switch off pain and body-awareness without depressing cardiac/respiratory function) and treating **depersonalisation disorder** by stimulating these networks.

## ★ Convergence: the self as a re-integrated output

Anton's conclusion: *"our feeling of existence and this person inside the physical body is **not some kind of a static fact** and is obviously **not some kind of a soul** or some kind of a floating consciousness but instead is a **very active moment-to-moment neurocomputational illusion**."* And: *"spatial self-location… is not some kind of an absolute truth. It is a very flexible output and is constantly being **reintegrated** inside your brain."*

**★ Third independent arrival at "the self is not intrinsic," in the same 24 hours:**

- **Blaise Agüera y Arcas** (same day): *"consciousness is not a thing that a piece of matter has intrinsically or doesn't have. It's not some essence or spark."*
- **Fields & Levin**, *From complementarity to non-duality* — objects and processes as pragmatic constructs.
- **This**: the self is a multisensory integration output, inducible, magnetically displaceable, and adoptable onto a rubber hand.

**And the rubber-hand result is the sharpest of the three** for our purposes: a *constructed* body model generates **genuine felt pain**. That bears directly on the zombie-multiplier argument and on the AI-suffering question — if felt pain attaches to a rubber hand via a model, then *"is it real pain?"* is answered by the **model**, not the tissue.

## Flags

- **Anton's title says "brand new study from 2026"** — accurate but the release is **2026-09-08**, ~5 weeks old, and the video is his coverage of it. He also says "**double** blind"; the blinding described is strong but the design is better described as *blinded target selection with AI scoring* — treat "double blind" as his characterisation.
- **Anton never cites the journal, DOI, or the companion paper.** He says "Dr. Wiler" (Marina **Weiler**). All specifics above come from source, not from the video.
- **The `Explore` journal** is a fringe-friendly venue (Elsevier, integrative medicine). Peer review there is not equivalent to *Nature*. The **companion paper in Frontiers in Psychology** is likewise mid-tier. Neither is a red flag on its own; both affect how much weight the null carries.
- **The two spontaneous hits are uninterrogated here.** Do not promote them. The researchers' own position is that they are not evidence, and no priors were registered for them.
- **Ketamine link is mechanistic, not clinical** — recorded because the corpus tracks the altered-state literature; not advice.
- **Watch for the follow-up.** Weiler's stated next study changes the target from a *static image* to a **dynamic change** (e.g. an unusual t-shirt) on the hypothesis that salient/emotional targets are more detectable. **That is falsifiable and prospective — register the prediction now so the next result is graded against a pre-existing standard rather than accepting a new paradigm post hoc.**
