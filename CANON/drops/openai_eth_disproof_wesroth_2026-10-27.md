## 2. The claim that is load-bearing, in the right register

- [EST] **OpenAI's claim is real and in the primary.** The announcement (`openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/`, dated **October 26, 2026**) states verbatim: *"Surprisingly, our system has recently proved that the ETH is false, a result that was subsequently verified in Lean."* The natural-language disproof was found **2026-10-16**; the Lean formalization **2026-10-17**.
- [EST] **"Disproved" is OpenAI's own word, not the video's invention.** The repository doc is titled *"The Exponential Time Hypothesis is False"* (`github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/378.md`); the manuscript is *"The Exponential Time Hypothesis is False"* (preprint dated 17 Oct, revised 25 Oct 2026) and its abstract states: *"Our algorithm refutes the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH)."*
- [HYP] **But "proved" is a lab claim, not a settled result, and OpenAI's OWN register is careful.** The same announcement calls the manuscripts *"a form of raw research data"*, says the working group *"will understand and explain a result"*, that the refutation *"reopens questions about how much faster these tasks could become"*, and that *"finding such algorithms, and turning theoretical advances into practical improvements, will require significant further research."* OpenAI also commits to funding a conference and makes the raw data public for independent verification. **The video compresses this hedged register into "proved… is false" — `[ATTACK SURFACE]`: open with OpenAI's own title (which is flat) but close with OpenAI's own caveats (which are not).**
- [EST] **Nothing here resolves P vs NP, and the source separates them correctly.** The claymath.org P vs NP page is still **"Unsolved"**. ETH is a *stronger* assumption than P≠NP (ETH ⟹ P≠NP); refuting ETH removes the strongest hardness wall but leaves P≠NP open. Wes states this; the description blurs it.
- [CONTRADICTED] **A specific checkable overstatement in the video's own narration:** L5, *"This ETH was this sort of limit, this computational wall that we thought was there. It was just a law of physics. Kind of like the idea you can't travel faster than light."* ETH is a **complexity conjecture** (Impagliazzo–Paturi 2001), not a law of physics, and nothing about it concerns the speed of light. He half-corrects two minutes later ("this was thought of to be just a fundamental law of *computing*", L102–L103), so the earlier sentence is a rhetorical inflation, not a persistent claim — but it is the video's sharpest register slip and it should not be inherited.

## 4. The SPEC-GAP recurs — and OpenAI documents it openly (primary-verified)

This is the drop's substantive finding, and it is a **new instance of the house's standing failure class**, not a restatement of the NS case.

- [EST] **OpenAI's own scope file says the Lean formalization proves a WEAKER statement than the manuscript.** `lean/docs/378.md` reads: *"For fixed k ≥ 2, Lean proves that a deterministic machine exactly counts satisfying assignments of a canonically encoded k-CNF on n variables in time $(L+1)^{O_k(1)}2^{O_k(n(\log\log n)^2/\sqrt{\log n})}$… For m clauses, the October 25 manuscript gives **the sharper time bound** $(n+m)^{O_k(1)}2^{O_k(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})}$."* The Lean exponent carries $(\log\log n)^2$ where the manuscript carries $\log\log n$ — so the machine-checked artifact certifies a **strictly slower (looser) running time** than the paper's headline. **Both are $2^{o(n)}$ and both refute ETH, so the divergence does not change the conclusion** — but the compiled certificate is attached to a weaker theorem than the paper states.
- [EST] **Anthropic's own repository states the general form of this gap as a standing caveat** (reported from its release, not re-fetched line-by-line by me): *"Lean checks the proofs, but no machine checks that this file says what the paper says."* That sentence is the house's SPEC-GAP / Lanyon–Gorard rule, written by a frontier lab about its own artifact.
- [A] **How this differs from the NS case, and why the register differs.** In the NS episode (`openai_navier_stokes_formalization_gap_2026-10-09.md`) the NL↔Lean mismatch (Lemma 8.6, m+4 vs m+5) was found by an **adversarial third party** and could in principle have changed the theorem. Here the mismatch is (a) **documented by OpenAI itself** in the scope note — good practice, not concealment — and (b) **conclusion-preserving** (both bounds refute ETH). So it is the same failure *class* with a milder instance: **not** "the press release overclaims a mismatch that was hidden", but "compile-success certifies the coded statement, and the coded statement here is weaker than the paper's — stated openly." The house rule still applies unchanged: **a formal verification without a spec audit is an inflated label by default**, and the ETH Lean proof has not had a hand spec-diff (the proposers are only now organizing the review).
- [A] **This also gives the "Layer 1/2/3" stratification from the Lex/503 drop a third concrete instance.** Layer 1 (top-statement fidelity) is open here because the challenge file defines its own objects (a "canonically encoded" k-CNF and a bespoke deterministic machine model), so a reader cannot close it by inspection as they could the Mathlib-native quasi-RH statement. The video does not know or mention this; it calls the proof "so obvious now that you look at it" (L173) — `[ATTACK SURFACE]`.
