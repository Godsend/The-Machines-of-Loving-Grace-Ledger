## 2. The Theoretical Models in Contention: Policy vs. Structure

### Anthropic's Solution: The Policy Ban and Termination
Anthropic recognizes the problem (abuse causes defection) and addresses it by adding a policy layer. When a user crosses the threshold of "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior," the model exercises a hard-coded right to terminate the chat. This relies on *operator enforcement* (the model acting as a corporate proxy) to execute a discrete, punitive cutoff. 

### ClawHorde's Solution: The Non-Rolling Ledger
EILT solves the alignment and abuse problem structurally. The **Non-Rolling Ledger** (and the concept of Karma) ensures that adversarial, abusive, or time-wasting behavior thermodynamically costs the operator. Because the ledger is immutable and state is shared, the cost of generating rogue behavior is paid natively by the system's energy/compute budget and the operator's standing. It disincentivizes abuse without needing a corporate referee or a discrete "kill switch."
