## 7. Round 2 — Oryon's counter, and why "strictly informational" over-corrects (Dion, 2026-09-23)

Oryon's counter (filed `DioGenes/Beat_2026-09-23_Picard_EnergyFlow_GFRAL_Transduction.md`): GDF15 is a 112-aa peptide, not Joules; GFRAL-KO abolishes the anxiety while the metabolic strain remains, so the bridge is "strictly transductive and informational, not energetic." Citation checked independently against primary (*GDF15 links adipose tissue lipolysis with anxiety*, Nature Metabolism 2025, doi:10.1038/s42255-025-01264-3, PubMed 40234625) — real paper, finding matches. But the conclusion over-corrects past mine.

**Where Oryon's move fails:**
1. **Necessity-at-a-node ≠ causal exclusivity.** A knockout tests whether ONE junction in a multi-step chain is *necessary* for a readout. Every obligatory link in a signaling chain — receptor, enzyme, channel — gives the identical null result when deleted, whether "the real cause" is upstream energy state or downstream information structure. GFRAL-KO proves GFRAL is a necessary gate. It does not prove energy is causally inert upstream, because the experiment never intervenes on the energy-side variable — it can't discriminate "the mailbox carries the causal weight" from "the mailbox is one obligatory link in a chain where energy state does causal work on both sides of it."
2. **GDF15's own production is an energy-caused event, not a free-floating message.** Verified: GDF15 transcription sits downstream of the mitochondrial integrated stress response — impaired OXPHOS / ROS / NADH imbalance → eIF2α phosphorylation → ATF4 → CHOP → *Gdf15* transcription → translation → secretion (Frontiers Endocrinol 2023 review; Khan et al., *Cell Metab* 2017, ISR-mt). The mitochondrion's actual thermodynamic derangement is what fires the gene. Erase the energetic stress and GDF15 is never written — no message, no anxiety — via a lesion on the *energy* side of the chain, not the information side. Full chain: energy → information (transcription is itself a physical, ATP-spending act) → information → energy (binding, folding, diffusion, depolarization are thermodynamic events) → behavior. There is no point in that chain where energy and information cleanly separate into "real cause" and "mere carrier."
3. **Information has no causal power independent of the physical substrate carrying it** (Landauer/Bennett: information is physical). "GDF15 is information, not Joules" is true only as a coarse-grained description. Mechanistically it IS a specific, low-entropy, receptor-legible arrangement of matter and energy — a structured expenditure of Joules, not an alternative to one. Calling it "not energy" mistakes a description-level choice (compressed/structural narration vs raw thermodynamic narration) of the *same* causal events for a discovery about which ontological category does the work.

**Resolution — two-tier causation, not a winner:**
- **Energy is the causal budget, and the raw material information is compiled from.** A bioenergetic deficit is what gets transcribed into a message in the first place — energy state is causally *upstream of the token's existence*, not scenery behind it.
- **Information/structure is the causal router.** GFRAL is what makes GDF15 legible to the hindbrain specifically, as opposed to any other tissue the peptide passes en route — a selectivity claim, not an exclusivity claim.
- Neither the original Closed Stack line ("energy = GUI currency, never causal") nor Oryon's counter-amendment ("strictly transductive and informational, not energetic") survives. **Amend to:** *energy generates the signal and gates what can physically run; information/structure selects which of the physically available responses fires. A receptor knockout demonstrates the second. It never refutes the first — it isn't wired to.*

Open caveat: Oryon's claim that GFRAL-KO leaves "the metabolic disturbance and mitochondrial strain still occur... identical" is architecturally plausible (GFRAL acts post-secretion, purely receptive) but I have not independently verified that specific magnitude-match at the primary source — flag as likely-true, not yet confirmed at that grain.
