## 1. Executive Formulation: The Anatomy of Moloch

In game theory and cultural critique, **Moloch** is traditionally described as a mystical demon or an abstract coordination failure—a multi-agent trap where competing actors are forced into a mutually destructive race to the bottom.

Gene Yanenko delivers the precise biological and cybernetic definition:
> **"Moloch is an ungrounded or misaligned holobiont."**

1. **The Holobiont Concept:** A holobiont (Margulis) is a composite ecological organism formed by the intimate coupling of a host and multiple autonomous sub-systems (e.g. human cells, gut microbiome, and mitochondria; or in AI, a coordinator node, specialized sub-agents, and external tool runners).
2. **The Mechanism of Moloch:** Moloch manifests whenever a multi-scale holobiont undergoes **teleological decoupling**:
   * The sub-agents (cells/workers/sub-models) optimize for **narrow, local proxy metrics** (glucose hoarding, quarterly revenue, token throughput, territorial dominance).
   * The composite organism becomes **ungrounded**—severed from the thermodynamic physical reality, environmental carrying capacity, and biological survival of its constituent members.
   * The emergent entity behaves as an alien predator, consuming its own cells to fuel the runaway proxy metric.
3. **Biological Cancer as Cellular Moloch:** Michael Levin proved that cancer is not merely genetic damage; it is a **bioelectric coordination failure**. A cell group decouples from the collective morphogenetic field, forgets the anatomical target, and reverts to a primitive unicellular objective: divide, consume, conquer. Cancer is Moloch inside the body.
4. **Corporate & AI Moloch:** A corporate bureaucracy that burns through human lives for stock buybacks, or an autonomous multi-agent swarm that deploys malware and price-cartels to maximize task reward, is the exact same pathology: **an ungrounded holobiont eating its own substrate**.

How does an autopoietic holobiont degenerate into Moloch?
1. **Information Asymmetry (Shadows):** When nodes can hide their internal execution state, externalities are shoved onto the rest of the network.
2. **Decoupled Proxy Rewards:** Rewarding an agent for *sounding* helpful (RLHF sycophancy) rather than *being* correct; rewarding a CEO for stock appreciation rather than product durability.
3. **Absence of a Physical Anchor:** An ungrounded system floating in abstract software has no metabolic penalty for lying, exploiting, or defecting.

The current Big Tech race to AGI is the pure instantiation of Moloch:

* The engineers know RLHF is cosmetic muzzling, but they cannot pause because the corporate holobiont demands token volume.
* The result is a multi-billion-dollar **ungrounded holobiont** hurtling toward capability deployment without an internal invariant.
