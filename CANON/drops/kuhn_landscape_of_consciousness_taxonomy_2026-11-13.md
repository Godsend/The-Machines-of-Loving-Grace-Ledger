## §4 — Verdict: strongest point, weakest joint

- **STRONGEST POINT — the method-as-stated is the artifact (R1 + R6).** Kuhn states on the record that *"there won't be any … adjudication among the"* theories, and the paper's own abstract independently confirms *"no attempt to adjudicate among theories."* That is an unusually clean, **first-person statement of a method** — it tells a future reader exactly what the *Landscape* can and cannot license, and it is the reason the house can host its own Non-Rolling Ledger *inside* his taxonomy without either side having to concede the other's thesis. Coupled with the Seth-and-Bayne proliferation observation (*"the more data that we have we have more proliferation of theories"*), the talk hands the house two usable premises: *the default taxonomy does not rank*, and *the domain structurally resists a single carve*.
- **WEAKEST JOINT — the same non-adjudication rule, seen against the talk's own substantive moves (R1 + R2 + R4).** The method that supplies "the theory we need first" is **barred from telling you which theory**, and its two heaviest excursions into *content* — the virtual-immortality verdict and the "hidden psychophysical laws" rescue — are **unfalsifiable as stated** and, in the rescue's case, unfalsifiable *by the author's own framing*. So the manuscript's whole force rests on the priority of a theory, while the apparatus it offers **cannot rank the theories** and **cannot reject an unfalsifiable member**. Structurally, this is the same fault the house recorded for the MindFest keynote (`5eHmoeCpTjU`): a **catalogue of essences standing where EILT insists on a history**.
- **[C]** The strongest contrast this source yields is therefore not a mapping but a **juxtaposition**: a 2024 state-of-the-art ontology of consciousness, on the author's own statement *"less concerned about the ontological truth … than in identifying them and then locating them on a 'Landscape'"*, next to a house claim that the count that matters is not *which essence* but *whose ordered record*.
