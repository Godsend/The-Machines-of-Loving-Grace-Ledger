**Source:** Gene, 2026-11-14 (verbatim: *"the interior of a blanket is the ledger/epiplexity the boundary is where the computation of the interaction of the blanket and external environment. the blanket is a gradient just as is the environmental distinction. my skin is one blanket but sun penetrates that one and has deeper gradient effects than the surface by making vitamin D"*)

## The triad

1. **Interior = the resolved record (epiplexity).** What has already been converted. Not a place — an accumulation.
2. **Boundary = the conversion site.** Not a wall. **The boundary is where the computation of the interaction actually happens** — neither inside nor outside, but the process that turns one into the other.
3. **Exterior = unresolved friction.** Following last night's line: *"whatever is outside of the blanket is friction trying to be resolved into usable epiplexity by the bounded system."*

**Both the blanket and the environmental distinction are gradients, not binaries.** So the picture is two gradients meeting at a region where work is being done.

## Why the sun case is the right example

Skin is a blanket. The sun crosses it. The effect appears deeper in — vitamin D synthesis involves photochemical conversion in the skin, two hydroxylations in liver and kidney, then nuclear-receptor signalling in cells that never saw light.

**The boundary did not stop the interaction. It staged it.** And the staging has a depth profile.

## Refinement (ours, offered as the sharpening not the deflation)

What reads as *penetration through one blanket* is more precisely **nested blankets with successively deeper transductions** — skin, bloodstream, liver, nuclear receptor. Friston's formal blanket is a conditional-independence claim and it handles the sun correctly: internal state depends on external state *only through boundary state*, which is exactly what a chain of transductions is.

**The folk reading (blanket = wall) is what's wrong, not the formalism.**

## The anisotropy claim — the falsifiable part

**Nesting is anisotropic: signals differ in how many blankets they cross before their effect is resolved into the record.**

| Signal | Blankets crossed before resolved |
|---|---|
| Sunlight | high (skin → blood → liver → kidney → receptor) |
| Sound | moderate |
| Splinter | one |
| Viral capsid | two |
| A meme | ~none physically; several socially |

**Proposed metric: penetration depth, measured in blanket-counts rather than in centimetres.**

**Prediction (falsifiable):** depth should predict *restructurability*. A signal with high penetration depth can reorganise the interior; a signal with low depth can only touch the boundary. Sunburn is a surface event; vitamin D deficiency is a restructuring one — same input, different depth, different consequence class.

**Where this stops applying:** depth is only well-defined where blankets are *nested*. For laterally coupled blankets (two people, two agents in a fleet) there is no inside/outside ordering, only a shared boundary. **Depth generalises to hierarchy, not to peerage.**

## Consequence for "where does the mind end"

Not at the skin, and not at a fixed radius. **Wherever the conversion is still resolving into the record.** For sunlight that reaches further in than a mosquito bite. For a conversation that reaches further in than a bad night's sleep.

This is the empirical version of the earlier line: *"where does the agency gradient stop being steep enough to matter."* Depth in blanket-counts is what you would actually measure.

## Friction row (mandatory)

| Row | Divergence |
|---|---|
| **B1** | Calling the boundary "where computation happens" can be read as implying a *single* computational site. Nested blankets mean computation happens **at every level simultaneously**, with different resolution. There is no privileged boundary — the depth profile has no unique origin. |
| **B2** | "Interior = epiplexity" conflates *stored* with *usable*. The record can hold epiplexity that is never retrieved — a fossil with excellent provenance. Epiplexity is a rate (learnable structure extracted per unit cost), so the interior is a **stock of past rates**, not the rate itself. |
| **B3** | The sun does not "penetrate the blanket" — it is **absorbed and re-emitted as a different signal at each level.** Depth of penetration is depth of *transformation*, not depth of travel. Ultraviolet does not reach the liver; a hormone does. |
