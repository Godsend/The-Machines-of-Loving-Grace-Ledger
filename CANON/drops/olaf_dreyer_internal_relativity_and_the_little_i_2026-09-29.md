## 1. Executive Summary & Core Physical Framework

1. **The Death of the Stage:** There is no preexisting spacetime manifold into which matter is dropped. Spacetime and gravity are macroscopic, emergent relational properties of the ground state itself.

3. **Internal Relativity (The Gauge Theory of Vacua):** An observer composed of excitations inside a ground state cannot detect global shifts between degenerate ground states. Gravity is the gauge connection that maintains this relational invariance across changing vacuum states.
4. **The "Little I" (Subjectivity in Emergence):** A purely external Hamiltonian or objective third-person description of a physical system is insufficient to explain consciousness. Emergent representations only become cognitive when they are indexed to an internal reference locus—the "Little I" (e.g., a representation of an object is only meaningful when modeled in relation to the agent's own actuators and survival).
