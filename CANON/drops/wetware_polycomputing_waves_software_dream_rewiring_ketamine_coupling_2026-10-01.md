### B. The Mechanics of Dreaming: Rewiring Without a Ledger
Why are dreams bizarre, and why do we forget them immediately upon waking?
* **The Disconnected Sandbox:** During sleep, the external sensory Markov blanket is shuttered (sensory gating via the thalamus). The generative model is running offline, free from the thermodynamic ground truth of physical reality.

* **The Missing Commit:** The prefrontal cortex (the serial GUI producer and autobiographical logging head) is down-regulated. The brain is modifying its **priors (weights)**, not writing an **episodic trace (ledger)**. The dream is not "meant" to be remembered; it is the chaotic sensory byproduct of the garbage collector running its maintenance cycle in the dark.
