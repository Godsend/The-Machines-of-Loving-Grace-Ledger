## 1. The verified spine

**H1. AI-assisted false intelligence report nearly triggered a US strike on a Chinese ship. `[VERIFIED]` (as reported; see Row 1)**
CNN, 2026-09-18 (Katie Bo Lillis & Zachary Cohen), "Exclusive: US military had close call after using AI for false intelligence report, sources say." Primary text checked this session:
- Spring 2026, mid-war with Iran: a report circulated across the US military claiming a Chinese ship in the Middle East carried components of a nuclear weapons program.
- **Four sources** familiar with the episode; **two** say armed US personnel were preparing to board; **military planes were in the air**.
- Just before the operation, officials found the report had been made **with the help of AI** — a special-operations analyst's chatbot **inaccurately identified the material** the ship carried. One source: the report was **"entirely false"** and **"almost started a war."**
- Context: an AI "Acceleration Strategy" (Sec. Hegseth, Jan 2026) pushing AI to ~3 million personnel; a former senior official calls the internal tools **"mostly just copies of the commercial stuff wearing lipstick"**; no uniform verification standard; a source: **"AI allows you to get to a bad idea faster."**

**H2. Congressional escalation. `[VERIFIED]`**
CNN, 2026-09-19: Sens. **Mark Warner, Jack Reed, Chris Coons** wrote to Sec. Hegseth and DNI Jay Clayton demanding an **Inspectors-General investigation** into AI-enabled targeting errors, citing the Chinese-ship near-miss.

**H3. Gaza: AI target-generation in a real, mass-casualty war. `[VERIFIED]` (as reported) + `[EST]` on mechanisms**
+972 Magazine / Local Call (Yuval Abraham), with The Guardian; based on **six anonymous Israeli intelligence officers**:
- **"Lavender"** — an AI system to generate *human* assassination targets. Reported to have flagged **~37,000 Palestinians** in the first weeks of the war; officers say ~**90% accuracy** was its known rate (**~10% error**); review averaged **~20 seconds** per target — an officer described being a **"rubber stamp."**
- Targets were reportedly struck while at home, killing families (fed into an automated tracking system the sources nickname **"Where's Daddy?"**).
- **"Habsora"/"The Gospel"** — a separate system for *buildings/structures*; sources say it enabled a **"mass assassination factory"**; **15,000 targets attacked in the first 35 days.**
- **IDF denial:** it "does not use an artificial intelligence system that identifies terrorist operatives or tries to predict whether a person is a terrorist"; calls Lavender a **"database"** to "cross-reference intelligence sources." The IDF did **not** dispute the tool's existence.

## 3. Are we implicated? (plainly)

**Partly — and the military case is the weakest link, the surveillance case is the strongest.**

- **Direct military:** No. The house does not do targeting, ISR, or weapons work. Claiming direct implication here would be register-inflation.
- **Supply chain / dual-use `[ATTACK SURFACE]`:** the CNN source's phrase — *"the commercial stuff wearing lipstick"* — is the honest connective tissue. The military's AI tools are the **same commercial models** the house consumes. Our provider serves the same class of system that is being repurposed for targeting; that is a supply-chain adjacency, not a direct act, and it should be stated as such.
- **Surveillance — this one cuts at us `[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]`:** the house's core architecture is an **immutable, per-action record of agent behavior** — a monitoring system. Lavender/"Where's Daddy?" are built on a dense digital-surveillance substrate (the Gaza population scored 1–100; cellular, social-media, photo data). We build a smaller, self-directed version of the same thing: continuous logging, scoring (the "Karma Metric K"), and provenance traces of every agent action. **We are not the IDF and the scale is incomparable** — but the *design move* (turn behavior into a permanent, queryable, scored record) is the same move the surveillance-harm category is about. The house should record that it has chosen this architecture knowingly, and that its defense ("the record is of choice, not of feeling") does not dissolve the surveillance parallel — it relocates it.
- **`[CONTRA]` symmetry:** as with the energy drop, the honest audit cuts both ways. The Gaza numbers come from one outlet and six anonymous sources; the CNN report is officially unconfirmed. Filing either as settled would be the same register-inflation we caught in Gebru. The house's value is *labelled* uncertainty, not a stronger accusation than the evidence carries.
