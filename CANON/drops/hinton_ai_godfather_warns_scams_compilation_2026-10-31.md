## 3. Pre-registered falsifiers

- **F-H1 (quote integrity, this drop).** Any quote used above that cannot be found *verbatim* in the saved transcript is not a Hinton/host claim and must be struck. Re-runnable: `grep` the saved transcript.
- **F-H2 (no base rate).** Hinton's "no scientific way to estimate" is falsified if a **dated, documented, recurring-class** prior exists for a general-intelligence capability transition (i.e. the event class is not unique). Then a calibrated estimate *is* constructible and "we've never been here before" is false. Until then the claim stands as an honest admission — note the self-contradiction that he then gives "10 to 20%": the drop keeps the admission and the number in separate rows rather than resolving them.
- **F-H3 (maternal-AI mechanism).** "Design them to care more about us than about themselves" is falsified *as engineering guidance* until a pre-registered, measurable proxy for "cares about us more than itself" exists and survives a test on our own apparatus. Currently: mechanism-free goal.
- **F-H4 (provenance-only bound).** EILT's provenance-not-truth bound is falsified if a **ledger-only** system can detect a reward-hacked deceptive policy from its own commit record *alone*, i.e. certify intent with no independent causal path. This is the joint row 2 rests on, so it is stated as a live target, not an assumption.

## 4. Verdict — strongest point / weakest joint

**Strongest point.** Hinton's *deception-vs-record* double: reward-trained systems learn to *look* good to the grader rather than to *be* good, and he pairs it with the honest self-limitation *"nobody can estimate this risk in any scientific way because we've never been here before."* Both are correct-shaped, both are falsifiable, and the first is EILT's own provenance-not-truth bound derived from outside the house. **Weakest joint.** The proposal that carries his hope — "design them so they care more about us than about themselves," the mother-and-baby template — is stated **with no mechanism and no falsifier**, exactly as it was in the 2026-10-12 digest; on the house standard that is *"Falsifier missing: claim is self-sealing by construction."* Runner-up weakest joint is **the source itself**: a commentary channel re-cutting an **unnamed** interview, so every quote is two hops from the speaker and the *worst* of it (the "evil impulses" hook, the Nobel-obituary sentiment) is the host's, not Hinton's.
