# Synthesis
In "Neuroscience Beyond Neurons," Michael Levin expands upon basal cognition, asserting that intelligence is not restricted to neurons. Rather, biological systems leverage bioelectricity and morphogenetic fields as "cognitive glues" that bind single cells into complex, problem-solving swarms (e.g., organ morphogenesis, regeneration).
