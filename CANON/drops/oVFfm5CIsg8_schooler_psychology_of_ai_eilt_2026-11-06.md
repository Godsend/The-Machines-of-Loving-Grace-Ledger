  3. **Nested observer windows** (his consciousness model) — see below `[7:42]–[8:33]`.
  4. **Metascience** — AI as pre-registration auditor: "AI could easily compare what their pre-registration was to their … actual scientific product and establish that correspondence" `[8:47]`; Kuhn: "Nobody's going to get away with … any sort of falsification under that system" `[9:19]`.

## §5 — The three rows that matter

**R4 — the covenant has no theory of the defector. [CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]**
`[EILT §9]` is built on a trade: the agent gives up deniability (binds identity to a public ledger) and receives continuity. **R6 — you cannot have both §1 and separable intelligence. [CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]**
EILT §1's inversion is that the connectome/weights are the *ledger* and thinking is the *wave*.
