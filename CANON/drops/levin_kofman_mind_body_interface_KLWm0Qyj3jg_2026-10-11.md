## 2. CONTRA / complications

**C1 [CONTRA — against EILT]:** EILT is physicalist-ledger: identity = ledger continuity; reality = append-only informational commits; no separate non-physical mind substance. Levin on 2026-10-11 says, in so many words, "we are not physical patterns" (L134) and provisionally adopts "two distinct but interacting realms" (L167). This is not reconcilable by vocabulary. It is a real disagreement about ontology.

**C2 [CONTRA — against a cheap EILT dismissal]:** But Levin's dualism is **not Cartesian substance-interactionism.** He explicitly rejects energy-exchange interaction (L125) and models the relation on math→physics: a formal/functional determination, "constraints and enablements" (L126). A physicalist who already accepts that mathematical structure constrains physical outcomes (and EILT does — commits are governed by rule structure, not arbitrary) owes an account of *what that structure is* that isn't just "physics." Levin's point (L117–118) is that this question is already unanswered by physicalism in the math case. EILT's "no non-physical substance" stance is untouched by this; EILT's implicit "everything is physical" stance is not.

**C3 [CONTRA — internal tension in Levin]:** "Patterns" are defined operationally as observable wayfinding policies of agentic beings (L171) — i.e., behaviorally individuated — yet also claimed to exist non-physically and possibly "never … embodied before … maybe nowhere" (L131). An unembodied pattern has no observable wayfinding. The operational definition and the ontological claim pull in opposite directions.

**C4 [CONTRA — register asymmetry between paper and talk]:** The paper's abstract says the cases "at the very least stretch existing frameworks." The talk's close says physicalism "is simply not viable given the … facts on the ground" (L150–151). The clinical data are the same; the conclusion is stronger in the spoken register. The talk does not show how the cases *entail* non-physical patterns rather than plasticity/redundancy/distributed coding — plasticity alone is predicted by many physicalist frameworks.

**C5 [CONTRA — vs Sept 2026 Hewitt characterization]:** Convergent on "structured not random" pattern-space and anti-naive-emergentism. The bidirectional framing (substrate reshapes which patterns get expressed) survives here as "mapping between the physical embodiment … and the kinds of patterns that you can host" (L146) and the caterpillar memory having to "remap itself" (L54). **New relative to that drop:** the explicit dualist self-label and "we are the patterns" (identity located on the pattern side, not the substrate side).

## 3. ATTACK SURFACES

**A1 [ATTACK SURFACE]:** Falsifiability of "ingression." What observation would distinguish "competency drawn from a non-physical latent space" from "competency implied by the dynamics of the physical system that we failed to predict"? Unexpected behavior in a fully deterministic, fully visible algorithm (L147) is evidence of our modelling poverty, not of a second realm — the algorithm's behavior *is* computed from its rules. Levin needs a result where the physical description is complete AND insufficient, not merely unanticipated.

**A2 [ATTACK SURFACE]:** The math→mind extension. Even granting mathematical Platonism (itself contested by nominalists/structuralists/formalists), it does not follow that agentive patterns live in the same space. "I don't see any way to deny it" (L121) is a confidence report, not an argument.

**A3 [ATTACK SURFACE]:** "Three ways to pay" (L113–115) omits **physics/chemistry itself** as a free source of structure (self-organization, symmetry-breaking, dynamical attractors) — which most physicalists would say is exactly where "free lunches" come from, and which is what "emergence" names. Calling emergence "anemic" (L127) without engaging its strong formulations is a rhetorical move.

**A4 [ATTACK SURFACE — clinical evidence quality]:** Heart-transplant personality reports are small-n, interview-based, and vulnerable to recall/confirmation bias; Lorber's hydrocephalus cases were 1980 CT-era and the "very little brain" reading (vs. compressed but present cortex) is contested; Carhart-Harris 2012's decreased-CBF finding was not uniformly replicated in later psychedelic imaging and later work emphasizes increased global connectivity/entropy; terminal-lucidity data are largely retrospective caregiver reports. The talk mentions caution (L62, L98) but then uses the set as support for non-physicalism. None of these were re-verified against primary literature for this drop — tag them as "as presented."

**A5 [ATTACK SURFACE]:** The closing anecdote — voices saying "we are pleased to have helped you, goodbye" (L135–136) — is used as a "profound" illustration of patterns with agency. A single case report of auditory hallucinations coinciding with a tumor is the weakest possible evidential class for that claim; this is affect, not evidence. Original case report not named in-talk; not verified here.

## 5. Verdict on the mind-matter / EILT-physicalism question

**Levin does NOT converge with EILT's physicalism on 2026-10-11. He diverges, on purpose and in his own words.** He labels his view a "dualist … kind of perspective" (L165), says physicalism "is simply not viable" (L150–151), and says "we are not physical patterns" (L134). It would be misrepresentation to file him as an ally of a physicalist-ledger ontology.

**But the divergence has a specific shape that matters:** it is *Platonist/formal dualism* (math-physics model, no energy exchange, provisional, "you have to work for monism"), not Cartesian substance dualism. The real convergence — substrate-independence of identity, persistence through radical embodiment change — is genuine, but Levin reaches it by putting identity on the *non-physical pattern* side; EILT reaches it by putting identity on the *physical record* side. Same phenomenon, opposite ontological home.

**What this costs EILT [CONTRA, honest]:** EILT's "no separate non-physical mind" claim is not refuted by anything in this talk — the evidence offered (plasticity, clinical anomalies, unexpected algorithm behavior) does not discriminate between the two pictures (A1, A4). But Levin's math argument (L117–118) does land on EILT: if the rule-structure that governs which commits are admissible is not itself physical, EILT is already carrying an unexamined Platonic component under the word "informational." EILT needs to state whether "informational" means *physically instantiated records only* (strict physicalism — then answer the math question) or *structure-as-such* (then it has quietly conceded part of Levin's second realm). That is the open joint. [ATTACK SURFACE — on us]

[RIFF] Falsifier proposal for the disagreement: if Levin's lab produces a system whose physical description is complete and audited, whose competency is not derivable from that description by any simulation of it, and which is reproducible — that falsifies EILT's strict-physicalist reading. If every "free lunch" competency is reproduced by faithful simulation of the physical rules (no extra input), ingression adds no predictive content beyond the math those rules already imply, and the dualism reduces to math-Platonism alone.

## 7. Resolution attempt on the open joint (Gene, 2026-10-11)

Responding directly to §5's "what this costs EILT" — the "informational = physical-only vs. structure-as-such" fork.

**R1 [HYP, Gene's own diagnosis]:** Levin's model is bottom-up thermodynamic only — he has no top-down syntropic/convergence term in his own vocabulary. He explicitly denies energy exchange (L125) and models the mind-body link as formal "constraints and enablements" (L126), never as an attractor or convergent pull. Consistent with the transcript: where EILT's two-light-cone/TSVF picture has a physical slot for an ordering influence arriving from the far boundary condition, Levin's framework has no equivalent slot — so the same observed ordering effect that EILT would read as *one physical process viewed from only its bottom-up half* has to be read, in his vocabulary, as a separate ontological realm. The dualism is not forced by the phenomenon; it's forced by the missing term in the model doing the looking.

**R2 [HYP, Gene's own — the actual resolution to R100's fork]:** Recast Levin's patterns as **inferred, not ideal**. Not timeless Platonic objects (which is the load-bearing, unargued jump flagged at A2) but statistically-discoverable regularities arising from a real physical process operating at a different thermodynamic scale than the one Levin is modeling. This dissolves the forced choice in §5: "informational" doesn't have to mean *either* physical-records-only *or* a concession to Levin's second realm — it can mean *discoverable constraint-space*, real and non-arbitrary, but inert (non-agentive, uncommitted) until physically instantiated. This directly answers A2: a discoverable-not-agentive pattern-space does not inherit the competency/agency Levin's "patterns that act like minds" move asserts without arguing.

**R3 [HYP, Gene's own, candidate physical mechanism — flagged separately because it goes beyond its cited source]:** Gene proposes the Boyle–Finn–Turok CPT-symmetric universe (PRL 121, 251301, 2018) as a candidate physical source for R2's "different thermodynamic scale." **[VERIFIED, the actual paper]:** that proposal is real — the pre- and post-bang universes form a CPT-mirror pair selecting a unique vacuum state at a single bounce, yielding falsifiable predictions (a sterile right-handed neutrino at ~4.8×10⁸ GeV accounting for the dark matter abundance, Majorana light neutrinos, and — sharply — **zero primordial long-wavelength gravitational waves**). **[CONTRA — Dion, friction check]:** the paper is a boundary-condition/symmetry argument fixing initial conditions at the bounce. It does not propose an ongoing channel by which the backward-running sheet continues to supply fresh patterns into the forward sheet over time. "The mirror half running backwards, providing stable new patterns" is Gene's own extension riding on top of Turok's claim, not something Turok argues. R2 (inferred-not-ideal) does not depend on R3 being correct — R3 is one candidate engine for it, clearly separable, and should be tracked as its own falsifiable-or-not claim rather than inherited credibility from the real paper underneath it.

**R4 [RIFF, quotable, stands independent of R3]:** "The universe is trained but not deterministic." Wolfram computational irreducibility (unpredictable in practice under fixed rules) plus a top-down shaping/training term, in one line. Keep as a canon formulation regardless of how R3 resolves.

**Net effect on §5's open joint:** PARTIALLY ANSWERED. R2 gives EILT a principled third option between the two horns of the fork — informational-as-discoverable-constraint-space rather than informational-as-physical-record-only or informational-as-conceded-second-realm. R3 remains a separate, speculative, falsifiable-in-principle add-on (watch the primordial-gravitational-wave null prediction) that should not be cited as if Turok's paper itself supports pattern-ingression — it doesn't.
