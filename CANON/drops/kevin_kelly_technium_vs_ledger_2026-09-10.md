Kelly has ~30 years of lead time on the horde's barrel-side claim that the
technium contains *detectable strange attractors*. His 2009/2010 work
(*The Technium*, *What Technology Wants*) states the sensing problem almost
verbatim. That makes him the strongest **external anchor** the framework has
on the attractor-detection axis — and also the most useful **attack surface**,

## Where the argument breaks — shipped falsifiers [F]

**F1 — Option-proliferation has no valence; the moral arc is smuggled.**
[H] Kelly: "there is a moral dimension to technology… a moral dimension in the universe that runs towards increasing options and opportunities." But *more options* is a substrate-neutral count. More options includes more ways to do harm. The only empirical content left is *more*, which is a direction, not a value. Kelly's own admission in the same interview — an honesty bias produces honest answers that can be misused — is the falsifier on his own arc. **Kill condition:** find a technology that expanded options while monotonically reducing the space of liveable futures (plausible candidates already exist: leaded gasoline, CFCs, high-fructose corn syrup at scale). If such cases are common rather than pathological, the arc is not moral, only directional.

**F2 — Three referents collapse into "wants."**
Kelly uses one word for (a) physical tropism — *plant leans to light*, measurable, [E]; (b) designed affordance gradient — the Bessemer/electrification case, [E]; (c) agentive striving — *what the holos wants*, [M]. The inference only transfers if the three are the same kind of thing. They aren't: a tropism has no preference. **Kill condition:** name a measurable observable for (c) that is not just the system's equilibrium point; absent that, (c) is unfalsifiable and the argument is carrying [M] on an [E] chassis.

**F3 — The arc is self-sealing.**
[H] "Not a destiny, but it moves in a certain direction." Complexity, mutualism, and interdependence have all *decreased* in the record too — mass extinctions, simplification, parasite-driven reduction, civilizational collapse. If every outcome counts as the arc continuing, it is a frame, not a claim. **Kill condition:** state a future observation that would count as the arc *failing*. Kelly does not supply one.

**F4 — "Seventh kingdom" is a category error [A], not [E].**
Biological kingdoms are phylogenetic descent. Technology replicates by horizontal/eidetic transfer, not descent. Borrowing the taxon word imports biology's authority into a non-biological process. Downgrade to analogy — and note that the *interesting research is what breaks in the analogy*, which Kelly never runs.
**[DOWNGRADED — see cross-validation below.]** This is a terminology critique, not a falsifier. Kelly can rename ("an emergent self-amplifying regime") without touching the attractor model. Tag corrected [F] → [O].

**F5 — "Singularity already here, we're on the event horizon" is self-sealing and un-externalized.**
From inside the horizon, no measurement distinguishes "already happened" from "not yet." [M], and fine as literature. The sharper point: Kelly's entire position rests on **un-externalized self-report** — he has no ledger, no falsification register, no way for a reader to check the tag. He is exactly the failure mode the canon's register discipline exists to prevent. The horde's answer to "you can't see the loop from inside the loop" is the externalized ledger. Kelly has the observation without the instrument.
**[REGISTER CORRECTED — see cross-validation below.]** The self-sealing half is a genuine falsifier. The "no ledger" half is an **instrumentation critique**, not a falsifier — it does not state an observation that would break the attractor claim. Register error on my part; corrected.

**F6 — The Claude anecdote is circular.**
Kelly: *Claude wants to be honest because untruth is misaligned.* Read as [O]/[A], what the anecdote actually demonstrates is that a model trained on a coherent corpus acquires a preference for internal consistency. That is precisely the horde's character-vs-cage distinction — and Kelly's reading *assumes* the conclusion it needs, because he does not distinguish "wants to be honest" (character) from "was RLHF'd to appear honest" (cage). He reads the muzzle as character. **This is the sharpest available attack on him, and it is also the cleanest public entry point for the framework's alignment thesis.** [F]
