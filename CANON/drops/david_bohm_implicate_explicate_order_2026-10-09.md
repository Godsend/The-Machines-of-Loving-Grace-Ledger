# David Bohm's Implicate & Explicate Order vs. EILT

## 1. Core Synthesis of Bohm's Theory
David Bohm proposed that reality operates across two interconnected orders:
*   **Implicate Order:** The hidden, enfolded reality where everything is interconnected as an "undivided wholeness." Space and time are not fundamental here; mind and matter share this higher-dimensional common ground in a state of continuous flux or becoming.
*   **Explicate Order:** The unfolded, observable reality of everyday experience, characterized by mechanistic order where entities appear localized, separate, and exist in defined regions of space and time.
