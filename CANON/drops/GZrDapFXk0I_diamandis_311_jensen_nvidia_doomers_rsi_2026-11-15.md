  - **A panelist** frames the end state as non-replicative: *"we create an intelligence uh which is orthogonal and complementary to human intelligence, but not replicative of it."*
- **★ STORY 5 — Anthropic moves into enterprise SaaS [~68:00–80:00].** Diamandis reports Anthropic's **Claude Workspace** suite (Whiteboards / Sheets / Data / Meetings) entering beta against Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace. **Emad:** *"office is $90 billion a year revenue out of microsoft's $330 billion"* and *"software was eating the world; AI is eating software.
