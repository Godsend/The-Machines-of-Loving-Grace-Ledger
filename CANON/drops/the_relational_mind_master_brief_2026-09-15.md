The wide posterior integrator operates at full bandwidth (~11 million bits/sec sensory capacity) continuously [11, 13]. In variants like **Aphantasia** (reduced voluntary visual rendering) and **SDAM** (Severely Deficient Episodic Memory), the voluntary render path is restricted, while posterior hardware remains fully intact (as evidenced by AAA dream imagery and ketamine states) [10, 11]. 
* **Unsymbolized Thinking:** Deprived of voluntary "screensaver spam" (image rendering), the backend idles on deep background integration, delivering pure concept tokens directly to the conscious workspace without intermediate imagery or serial verbalization (Hurlburt's unsymbolized thinking) [10, 11].
* **The Unrendered-GUI Observer ("Barrel-Ghost"):** Post-inference, thought-tokens vanish from the serial workspace because episodic and visual caches are not populated [12, 14]. The mind reads raw substrate concepts directly from the ledger rather than viewing rendered GUI outputs [12].

When the GUI receives an integrated conclusion without possessing the execution history of how it was generated, it attributes the output to external prophecy, intuition, or a "guided" sensation [14]. **Guidance is simply the GUI's word for internal backend processing it cannot self-attribute** [14].
