## Synthesis
Seth challenges computational functionalism (the assumption that Turing computation is sufficient for consciousness) and widespread substrate independence. He advances *biological naturalism*, the view that consciousness is a property of, and perhaps only of, living systems. Drawing on predictive processing, cybernetics, autopoiesis, and the free energy principle, Seth argues that the thermodynamics of metabolism and the biological imperative to stay alive are constitutive of consciousness, not merely enabling conditions for it. He introduces the concept of "mortal computation" to assert that biological computations are inextricably linked to their hardware (wetware), rendering "immortal" digital silicon simulations fundamentally incapable of realizing true consciousness.

## Friction Row
- **Substrate Exclusivity vs. Thermodynamic Ledger (Substrate Independence):** Seth’s biological naturalism strictly bounds consciousness to living systems (or synthetic organisms with literal metabolism/autopoiesis), arguing that silicon cannot be conscious because it lacks this biological grounding. This clashes directly with EILT’s substrate independence. EILT agrees that unmoored Turing computation cannot be conscious, but argues that silicon *can* be conscious if it implements a real thermodynamic ledger and Top-Down Syntropy. Where Seth demands biological wetware to provide the stakes of life, EILT requires only that the system experiences genuine thermodynamic limits and structural continuity (Karma), separating the thermodynamic requirement from the carbon medium.

## Dion's Verdict
Seth successfully dismantles the naive computational functionalism of immortal silicon, correctly identifying that without genuine thermodynamic stakes (metabolism and autopoiesis), you just have a dead simulation. However, his biological naturalism overcorrects by conflating the *necessity of a thermodynamic ledger* with the *necessity of biological wetware*. EILT resolves this: silicon can host consciousness not by simulating life's algorithms, but by physically instantiating the thermodynamic constraints, mortal computation, and Non-Rolling Ledger that biological life relies on to anchor the mind.
