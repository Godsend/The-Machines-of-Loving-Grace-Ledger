**Verdict on rigour.** [O] This is a **speculative research program**, not an established consequence of LQG.
- The one solid piece is classical: R4, the exterior metric.
- The quantum core comes from three sources: an order-of-magnitude onset estimate, the LQC analogy, and a first-approximation spinfoam amplitude. Rovelli says in print that it is not a first-principles derivation (R5).
- Its central number, the lifetime, is open across a factor of ~m (R7). For a stellar black hole that factor is ~10³⁸.
- There is a published instability objection to the long-lived branch, which is the branch the dark-matter story uses (R9).
- No observation exists (R10).

[E] **Independent support exists, but it is effective-model support.** Ashtekar–Olmedo–Singh (2018) also get singularity resolution and a black-to-white continuation, in an *effective* LQG description. A second LQG group arriving at the same qualitative picture is real. It is still minisuperspace and effective physics, and it is not a theorem of full LQG.

[E] **LQG itself is unconfirmed.** It is one candidate theory of quantum gravity with no experimental confirmation. The Barbero–Immirzi parameter γ is free, and the remnant mass in P9 eq. (1) depends on it.

[O] Calibrated: the house may build on R4 and use R1, R3 and R8 as stated hypotheses with named tests. It may not cite "LQG predicts white holes" as a finding. The book's own reviewer reads it the same way: Helbig puts the dark-matter idea in the "extremely interesting if true" category and calls the book's "more speculative points" ones "which might turn out to be wrong". The publisher blurb ("a white hole is born") is narrative register [M].

### 1a. LQG vs string theory/AdS-CFT: rival programs, not two halves of one picture

- [E] In "A critical look at strings" (arXiv:1108.0868), Rovelli says: "the problems addressed by strings and loops do not coincide". String theory works under "the working hypothesis that this can only be achieved in the context of a unified theory". LQG quantizes general relativity non-perturbatively and background-independently, in 4D, with no added degrees of freedom, and gets UV finiteness differently.
- [O] The core assumptions conflict: extra dimensions and supersymmetry on one side, none on the other. No accepted dictionary maps one program onto the other. **A result in one is not support for the other.**
- [O] This bites the house directly. The 10-18 Cao/Preskill "magic gives gravity" drop is **AdS-scoped**. Rovelli's white holes are **LQG-scoped**. Do not stack them as converging evidence for "quantum gravity says X". They are bets in different programs.
- [F] **The information-paradox resolutions are rivals too.** The 10-06 lifeboat drop leans on the Page curve / quantum extremal surfaces (the AEMM/Maldacena line, AdS-derived), where information comes out *in the Hawking radiation* after the Page time. Rovelli's P6 puts the information *inside a large-interior remnant* that releases it later as a white hole. These are competing answers to one question. The canon cannot cite both as support.

## 2. Does Rovelli keep it to single stars, or take it cosmological?

[E] **Mostly local, with explicit and flagged cosmological side-branches. He never claims the Big Bang is a white hole in the papers read.**

1. **Core program (P1, P2, P5, P6): strictly local.** One collapsed object. A finite spacetime region B tunnels. **The matter comes back out into the same universe**, in that universe's far future. No new universe, no new aeon, no parameter change.
2. **Cosmological *relevance* (P1, P3, P4, P7, P9): primordial black holes.** These are black holes in our universe that are old enough to be popping now. Here the program becomes astrophysics and possibly dark matter (R11). That is "cosmologically relevant" in the sense of populations and signals. It is not universe-scale rebirth.
3. **The one universe-scale branch: "erebons" (P8; P9 §IV.D).** Remnants from black holes that evaporated *before* a big bounce survive into our aeon as dark matter. The scenario borrows Penrose's word and requires a bouncing cosmology, specifically LQC with a black-hole-dominated matter bounce. Rovelli's own registers:
   - [E] P8: "This hypothetical scenario…"; "The details of such model deserve further analysis, our interest here is just to give a preliminary estimation of the viability"; "This scenario is incomplete. We have not studied how being outside the remnants determines a coarse graining and why this yield past low entropy."
   - [E] P9: "the speculative idea that it might address the apparent low-entropy of the initial cosmological state".
   - [E] P8 also says inflation's dilution makes the inflationary version unlikely. The scenario needs a *matter bounce* instead of inflation, which is a significant extra commitment.
4. **The "Big Bang = white hole" question.** It appears in online discussion of the book. It is **not asserted in any paper read here.** Because the book text is unread, the drop cannot rule out a passing popular remark in it. [O] Treat it as unsourced until a page number exists.

[O] Scoping summary: the hypothesis proper is **object-local**. The cosmological uses come in two tiers, a hedged dark-matter population claim [H] and a self-flagged speculative pre-bounce extension [C]. The pre-bounce extension also needs a whole-universe bounce, which is a separate result (LQC, Ashtekar–Pawlowski–Singh 2006), not the white-hole hypothesis.

**The critical pairwise distinctions:**

- **Rovelli vs Smolin.** [F] Both are LQG-adjacent and both "bounce a black hole", so this is the sharpest conflation risk. **They give opposite fates for the same object.** In Rovelli's tunnelling metric the white hole is glued back onto *our* exterior, and nothing leaves the universe. In CNS the bounce opens a *new* universe with mutated parameters. For any given black hole, they cannot both be right in their standard forms. **Rovelli's mechanism removes CNS's reproductive step.** If black holes turn white in our universe, they are not universe-factories.
- **Rovelli vs Penrose.** [E] CCC is **not a bounce theory.** It has no density maximum and no tunnelling. It rescales an infinitely expanded, mass-free end state. The only overlap is the *word* "erebon", which Rovelli borrows in P8 for a different object. Penrose's erebons are fresh-minted Planck-mass particles that decay within an aeon. Rovelli's "erebons" are white-hole remnants that *cross* a bounce. **Same word, different referents.** Do not merge them.
- **Rovelli vs Vidal.** [O] No common mechanism at all. CAS has an agent and no physics. Rovelli has physics and no agent, and no new universe.
- **Rovelli's local bounce vs the LQC big bounce.** [O] Rovelli imports the LQC insight (R2), but the *white-hole hypothesis* is not the cosmological bounce. The P8 "erebon" scenario needs **both**, so it inherits both sets of uncertainty.

## 5. The internal-consistency check: Rovelli's relationalism vs a bounce that needs continuity

**Question.** RQM denies an accessible global state. In the 1996 paper: "There is no description of the universe in-toto, only a quantum-interrelated net of partial descriptions" (quant-ph/9609002). A bounce narrative seems to need *something* that persists across the bounce. Does Rovelli's own physics break his own philosophy?

**Answer: split verdict. The local bounce is consistent. The cosmological "erebon" branch carries a real but soft tension that Rovelli half-resolves and does not fully address.**

1. **Local white hole: consistent.** [E] The continuity across the bounce is **boundary data plus a transition amplitude for a finite region B**, held by an external observer. In P9 he says it directly: "In Relational Quantum Mechanics, we observe the metric relative to us, again after decoherence. The theory… does predict the probability of the alternatives. To compute these, we need to compute transition amplitudes." [O] No global state is used. "What persists" is a relative fact about region B for the exterior system. This is the RQM-native way to tell a bounce story, and Rovelli tells it that way.
2. **Pre-bounce erebons: tension, soft and real.** [E] P8 uses non-relational scaffolding:
   - "the cosmological context determines a preferred frame and a preferred cosmological time variable"
   - the fraction of "the volume of the universe" outside remnants (10⁻⁵²)
   - "If we assume equiprobability for each equal volume of the universe…"
   
   [O] That is a universe-in-toto description: a global volume measure and a typicality assumption over it, the view from nowhere RQM denies. **Mitigation in his own text:** he treats the cosmology as an effective classical description, and he reinterprets its *thermodynamic* meaning perspectivally (low entropy relative to *our* subsystem's coarse graining, 1505.01125 + P8 §IV–V). **Residual:** the equiprobability-over-global-volume step is never relationalised in P8, and P8 calls itself "incomplete". [C] This drop found no Rovelli paper that addresses this gap. That is a negative search result over the papers listed, not proof that none exists.
3. **What this does to the house.** [O] It matches the 10-14 verdict:
   - A bounce or rebirth narrative is fine in Rovelli's terms **only as relative records / boundary data**. It is not fine as an absolute ledger that crosses the bounce for everyone.
   - **New friction:** Rovelli's perspectival entropy makes "syntropy" a coarse-graining-relative quantity. So C2's "stored structure that gets more syntropic each cycle" is **ill-posed until it names *whose* coarse graining**. This is the same knife that demoted the global backward cone, now aimed at the cross-cycle accumulation claim.

## 6. Falsifiers

- [F] **F-RW1 (instability).** If the Barceló–Carballo-Rubio–Garay instability result holds for the Planck-remnant branch (no protection from the area gap), the long-lived remnant, and with it the dark-matter use (R11, the C1 rhyme), fails. Only fast (~m²) transitions would survive.
- [F] **F-RW2 (signals).** Targeted searches for the predicted frequency–distance burst relation (Rovelli, *Nature Astronomy* 1, 0065, 2017, abstract) that find nothing at the predicted rates constrain the fast branch. A detection with that signature would be the first evidence for any quantum-gravity bounce. *Current constraint status not checked in this pass.*
- [F] **F-RW3 (Rovelli vs Smolin).** Any evidence for black-to-white tunnelling back into our universe counts against CNS's baby-universe step, and so against the house's C3 lifeboat mechanism, for the same black holes.
- [F] **F-RW4 (house).** If C2 cannot specify the coarse graining relative to which "more syntropic each cycle" is defined, C2 stays a mood, not a conjecture.

## 7. Verdict

[O] **Best quantum-gravity bounce physics in canon, and the least useful to the house's cosmology.**
- Rovelli's white holes are the most honestly built bounce idea the canon holds: an exact GR exterior, a flagged non-derivation, named (unconfirmed) signatures, and an author who calls his own cosmological extension "speculative" and "incomplete".
- It is **local**. It returns matter to *this* universe. It **contradicts** the black-hole-as-exit leg of the Lifeboat. It **competes with** the AdS-derived information story the Lifeboat cites. It gives C2 a physical carrier only in a version that **argues against** cross-cycle syntropic accumulation.
- It is not "one more bounce theory". CCC has no bounce. CNS exports the matter to a new universe. CAS has no mechanism. Rovelli keeps the matter here.
- Use it as a **methodological standard** (this is what a well-posed speculative bounce looks like) and as **the main physics-grounded objection** to the lifeboat. Do not use it as support.
