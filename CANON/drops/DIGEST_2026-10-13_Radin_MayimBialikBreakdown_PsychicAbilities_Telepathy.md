## 0. One-paragraph read (not a recap)

Radin is not doing mysticism. He makes **numerical, falsifiable claims**, the kind the house can actually test:
- Ganzfeld: about 4,700 sessions, hit rate 30-32% against 25% chance.
- Selected participants: 45-50%.
- Presentiment: 50-60 replications.
- Global Consciousness Project (GCP): 500 formal events, "3 trillion to one".

He argues that psi research is *methodologically* orthodox and differs only in ontology (L551-558). The ganzfeld numbers he quotes are close to the current proponent meta-analyses. Where the case is weak is not the numbers he cites. It is three other things:

1. **What he leaves out.** He never mentions the precognition program that went through the largest preregistered, adversarially co-designed replication in the field's history and **failed**: Bem 2011 → Transparent Psi 2023. He never mentions Milton & Wiseman 1999. Nor does he mention that the GCP's own lead analyst, Bancel 2017, rejected the "field-like global consciousness" reading that Radin calls his "favorite interpretation".
2. **His mechanism story contradicts itself.** He invokes entanglement as a correlation "probably not through signaling" (L1046-1050). But a ganzfeld hit *is* signaling: the receiver picks the target an RNG chose at a distance. The no-communication theorem rules out exactly that use of entanglement.
3. **His "selected participants hit 45-50%" claim proves too much.** At that rate a single talented receiver could identify a 1-in-4 target with about 97-99% reliability by majority vote over 30-40 trials. That is an operationally decisive intelligence channel. The 1995 AIR review of Stargate found **no** intelligence value.

The house position, "more synchronized → more synchronicities", absorbs most of the talk cleanly: omens, the dream, the owls, uncontrolled "psychic overload", and the presentiment design. Radin himself supplies the shared-memory mechanism for early telepathy artifacts (L751-755) and the selective-attention mechanism for omens (L2077-2082). The honest finding that **cuts at the house** is that the synchronization account **cannot absorb a properly randomized ganzfeld effect if that effect is real**. Randomized target selection is designed precisely to break shared-prior phase-locking. So either the residual effect is artifact, or our resolution is incomplete. That is a falsifiable fork, not a settled win (§5, §6).

## 1. Claim inventory (Radin's own claims, in order of empirical specificity)

### R1. Ganzfeld telepathy: ~4,700 sessions, ~20 labs, 30-32% vs 25% chance [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L843-865]
> "as starting in the 1970s till now there's around 4,700 sessions … So after the 4,700 sessions, roughly 20 laboratories around the world, uh the odds against chance are a gazillion to one … with four possible targets, you'd expect chance would be 25% and people give roughly 30 to 32% on that. But after that many trials you can just from the statistical power alone you know that this is not a chance outcome."

**Cross-check [EST]:**
- **Proponent side.** Tressoldi & Storm, *F1000Research* 2021 (Stage-2 Registered Report, doi 10.12688/f1000research.51746.4), covers ganzfeld studies from 1974 to 2020. It reports an overall ES ≈ **0.08 (0.04-0.12)** under both frequentist and Bayesian random-effects models. It says the effect "passed four publication bias tests" and shows no decline over time. Hit rates around 30-32% are the standard proponent figure (Storm/Tressoldi line). Radin's numbers are therefore **not inflated relative to the proponent literature**.
- **Critical side.** Milton & Wiseman, *Psychological Bulletin* 1999, 125(4):387-391, meta-analyzed **30 post-1987 ganzfeld studies** and got **Stouffer Z = 0.70, p = 0.24, ES = 0.013**, i.e. no replication of the Bem & Honorton 1994 autoganzfeld result. Figures are per Palmer's review and Utts's own slide reanalysis (ics.uci.edu/~jutts/MWAnalysis.pdf). Proponents answered by adding later studies, and the pooled result turned positive again (e.g., "Does psi exist? Comments on Milton and Wiseman's (1999) meta-analysis", PMID 11393304). Hyman also noted that in the autoganzfeld the 32% overall rate was carried by dynamic targets, while static targets were at chance.
- **Verdict on R1:** a **contested, not failed** literature. It has one major meta-analytic replication failure (Milton-Wiseman 1999) and a later proponent recovery. **There has been no Transparent-Psi-scale, adversarially co-designed, preregistered multi-lab ganzfeld.** That absence is the open item (§6 F-R1).

**Arithmetic check [RIFF] (computed this session):**
- 4,700 × 31% at p₀ = 0.25 gives z ≈ 9.5. "Gazillion to one" is the right order *if* every session is independent, unbiased, and the database is complete. The whole fight is over that "if".
- Mutual information at 31% on a 1-of-4 task ≈ **0.013 bits/trial**. That is a real but nearly empty channel.

### R2. Replication difficulty is purely a power problem [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L574-581] [ATTACK SURFACE]
> "why is it difficult to replicate studies? Because there's not enough statistical power. That's why. And it has nothing to do with the nature of the phenomena. The phenomena can be evoked even with with college sophomores that are unselected for any particular ability or interest."

- **Half right [RIFF]:** for 31% vs 25% (one-tailed α = .05, 80% power), you need about **345 sessions** per study. A typical ~40-session ganzfeld study has an *expected* z of only ≈ 0.88. So individual studies are in fact badly underpowered.
- **The other half [EST] [CONTRA]:** the power excuse has **been tested directly where the field let it be tested**, and it failed.
  - Kekecs et al. 2023, *R. Soc. Open Sci.* 10:191375 (Transparent Psi Project), ran a multi-lab replication of Bem 2011 Exp. 1 that was **co-designed by proponents and opponents**, with born-open data and external auditors. Result: **49.89%** vs Bem's 53.07%, chance 50%: "Bem's findings were not replicated."
  - Ritchie, Wiseman & French 2012, *PLoS ONE* (doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0033423), ran three preregistered exact replications of Bem's retroactive-recall effect: combined n = 150, **p = .83**.
- Radin claims the phenomenon can be evoked with unselected sophomores. Large preregistered samples of unselected participants are precisely where the precognition effect disappeared.

### R3. Selected participants hit 45-50% [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L1068-1083] [ATTACK SURFACE]
> "if you take a subset of people doing that experiment who report that they they seem to be in telepathic connection with a loved one … they get way more than 30%. They get 45 to 50% in these experiments which supports the idea that they actually do have some talent … So what this tells us is that the notion that everything is connected all the time is probably true and not just connected through space but connected through time."

- **[EST]:** Tressoldi & Storm 2021 do report that selected participants' ES is "almost three-times" that of unselected participants. So a moderator effect in that direction is in the proponent literature.
- **The proves-too-much test [RIFF] (Monte-Carlo, this session):** at p = 0.45-0.50 on a 1-of-4 task, plurality vote over 30 trials picks the right target **92-97%** of the time, and over 40 trials **96-99%**. One such person plus a two-week protocol would be a reliable classified channel. The 1995 AIR evaluation of Stargate found a statistically significant lab anomaly (Utts) but **no demonstrated intelligence value**, and the program was terminated [EST: AIR 1995 report, per secondary summaries; primary not pulled this session].
- Either the 45-50% subset figure shrinks under preregistered retesting of the *same* individuals (regression to the mean on post-hoc "talent" selection), or there is an unexploited channel nobody has operationalized. The first is the default.

### R4. Loopholes closed: EM shielding, magicians, footfall acoustics [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L887-896]
> "So we able to exclude every known form of signaling and put the receiver into a shielded room electromagnetically shielded room so even radio signals would not get in there … you think of every possible loophole that you can, you figure out a way to close the loophole and you still get the same results."

- Credit where it's due: consulting stage magicians (Edinburgh) and testing for footfall acoustics (IONS, L866-886) is good adversarial practice [VERIFIED].
- **[ATTACK SURFACE]:** "every known form of signaling" cannot be shown in a podcast, and the remaining loopholes in this literature are *not* sensory. They are analytic:
  - randomization quality of target selection;
  - judging/response-bias × target-preference interactions;
  - optional stopping;
  - selective reporting.

  EM shielding closes none of these. Bösch et al. 2006 (below) is the template for how that class of artifact produces small, heterogeneous, significant pooled effects.

### R5. Presentiment: physiology anticipates randomly chosen emotional vs. calm images; "50 or 60 such replications" [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L1401-1405; L1458-1475; L1494-1504]
> "Well, I developed the presentiment design." … "it's chosen completely at random and by the way, by a quantum random process. So, nobody can even know in principle what's about to happen … you remain calm before calm pictures and emotional before emotional pictures and that's what you see and again and again and again. So there's something like 50 or 60 such replications in different laboratories at this point and you find it in almost every different bodily function that you can measure."

- **[EST]:** Mossbridge, Tressoldi & Utts 2012, *Front. Psychol.* 3:390 (doi 10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00390): **26 reports, 1978-2010**. Fixed-effect ES = 0.21 (95% CI 0.15-0.27), z = 6.9. Random-effects ES = 0.21 (0.13-0.29). Fail-safe N = 87 reports. Higher-quality studies showed *larger* effects. Radin's "50 or 60" is about twice the count in the only peer-reviewed meta-analysis located; it may include post-2010 studies [ATTACK SURFACE: unsourced count].
- **[EST] [CONTRA]:** Schwarzkopf 2014, *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* 8:332, "We should have seen this coming", argues that the anticipatory signal can arise from **expectation bias**. Over a random sequence, the probability that the next stimulus is arousing rises after each run of calm trials, and physiology tracks that, unless the analysis rules it out. Mossbridge et al. replied in "We Did See This Coming" (arXiv 1501.03179), saying the bias cannot produce the effect under their counterbalancing. **Status: contested; no large preregistered multi-lab failure of presentiment specifically was located this session.** Do not conflate presentiment with Bem's failed retro-priming/recall paradigms. They are cousins, not the same test.
- **[ATTACK SURFACE]:** Radin chose not to mention Bem 2011, which is the precognition paradigm that *was* stress-tested to failure. Bem's own 2016 meta-analysis (*F1000Research*, 90 experiments, z = 6.40, Hedges' g = **0.09**) stands against Transparent Psi's preregistered null.

### R6. Precognition is probabilistic, not fated [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L1217-1222] [HYP]
> "Experimentally, what we can tell is that precognitions are probabilistic. very very rarely do we get any evidence at all that the precognized future is faded [fated] and must occur"

Also used to deflect the lottery objection (L1349-1361: "the information … is usually not veritical … it's probabilistic and some people do win the lottery many times").
- **[ATTACK SURFACE]:** "probabilistic, sometimes wrong, can't be used on demand, anxiety squashes it (L1407-1412), skeptics' presence suppresses it" is an **auxiliary-hypothesis stack**. Each layer is individually reasonable, and together they make any null absorbable. Lottery multi-winners are offered as possible evidence with the base rate unstated. Given millions of players over decades, repeat winners are expected.

### R7. RNG mind-matter (PEAR "tripolar" protocol) [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L2190-2196]
> "if you look at the data from the pair lab and other labs, it's very clear that probabilistically that happens. Well, it shouldn't happen."

- **[EST] [CONTRA]:** Bösch, Steinkamp & Boller 2006, *Psychological Bulletin* 132(4):497-523, pooled **380 RNG studies** and found a "significant but very small" effect that was "strongly and inversely related to sample size and … extremely heterogeneous". A Monte-Carlo simulation showed that publication bias **could in principle** produce all three features. Radin was a co-author of the published rebuttal (Radin, Nelson, Dobyns & Houtkooper 2006, same issue), and Bösch et al. replied ("In the Eye of the Beholder", 132(4):533-537).
- "Very clear" is an overstatement of a literature in which the largest independent meta-analysis concluded the pattern is consistent with bias. Secondary sources also report that the 1996-2000 PEAR-Freiburg-Giessen consortium replication did not reproduce PEAR's benchmark effect [EST, secondary only; primary not pulled this session].

### R8. Global Consciousness Project: 500 formal events, "3 trillion to one" [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L2265-2278; L2288-2294]
> "And so we did 500 formal events … we would say we're going to define that the event of interest was from this time to that time … So after 500 events, the odds against chance of seeing a negotropic effect was 3 trillion to one. It just kept getting more and more significant the further along we went."

- **[EST]:** cumulative **Z ≈ 7.31** over 500 preregistered events, 1998-2015 (Nelson & Bancel 2011; Bancel 2017, per the EarthBeat summary of the record). One-tailed p ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻¹³ [RIFF: computed]. Radin's "3 trillion to one" is the right order of magnitude.
- **[EST] [CONTRA], the decisive one:** **Peter Bancel, the project's own long-time analyst, concluded in 2017 (*EXPLORE*) that the 17-year record is better explained by a goal-oriented effect associated with the experimenters than by a field-like global consciousness.** Nelson disputed this in the same issue. May & Spottiswoode reported that the flagship 9/11 result was sensitive to window choice.
- Radin's own description concedes the degree of freedom: *the experimenters* defined each event's start and end. On air he presents dual-aspect monism as his "favorite interpretation" (L2281) and does not mention that the project's own analyst rejected that interpretation class.

### R9. Mechanism: entanglement-like holistic correlation, "probably not through signaling" [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L1037-1056] [HYP]
> "if you do know about quantum mechanics and you understand things like entanglement … the physical world does not prevent things from being connected at far distances. Probably not through signaling, but through some form of connection that we can call a correlation. It's just stronger than than a classical correlation. If you push on that idea, it says … we live in a holistic reality. There's a fabric of reality that can simply connects everything all the time."

- **[EST] [CONTRA]:** the no-communication theorem. Entangled correlations cannot transmit a chosen message, because each party's local marginals are unchanged by what the other party does.
- A ganzfeld hit, a remote-viewing description, or a presentiment response **is** a transmission of information about a target selected at the other end. Calling it "correlation, not signaling" does not make it permissible. It relabels the very thing the theorem forbids.
- The same goes for retrocausal presentiment. Information about a *future* RNG output reaching present physiology is signaling backward in time, which is worse.

### R10. Dual-aspect monism / "negentropic" coherence [VERIFIED] [QUOTE, L2225-2242] [HYP]
> "Dual aspect monism is saying that yes, there is a physical world … there's also a world of the mental world. How do we see that? We see it through a negotropic effect."

- **[ATTACK SURFACE]:** this is a common-cause story with no physical carrier, no coupling constant, and no energy budget. "Negentropic deviation" in hardware RNG output is a local reduction in entropy. Under Landauer and the second law, *someone pays*. The model does not say who, what, or how much (see §4).

## 5. Strongest divergence (for relay)

**Row 6: entanglement as "correlation, not signaling".** This is where Radin's framing and the house's genuinely cannot both be right. Radin needs psi to (i) carry *target-specific information* across a shielded gap (that is what a hit rate measures) while (ii) not being "signaling" (to stay inside quantum mechanics). Those two cannot both hold. The no-communication theorem says shared entanglement leaves each party's local statistics independent of the other party's choices. The thing Radin measures is precisely a dependence of the receiver's statistics on a choice made at the sender's end.

So the mechanism he offers would, if it were the real mechanism, **predict a ganzfeld hit rate of exactly 25%**. To the house the claim reduces to: "an unspecified channel, with no carrier, no bandwidth limit, and no Landauer budget, exists". Under the physicalist ledger that files as unresolved `[HYP]`, not as an established correlation.

Runner-up, and the one that cuts at us: **Row 10.** A clean, randomized ganzfeld effect would beat our synchronization account as well.

## 6. Pre-registered falsifiers

- **F-R1 (decides R1/R3 and house Row 10): adversarial ganzfeld consortium.**
  - Transparent-Psi-style: proponent + skeptic co-design, born-open data, external auditors, hardware-RNG target selection logged before the session opens, blind judging.
  - **n ≥ 1,500 sessions.** At H₁ = 31%, one-tailed α = .005, power ≈ 0.95; per-study power need is ~1,420 at α = .005 / 95% [RIFF: computed].
  - **Psi falsified (for this paradigm)** if the 95% CI upper bound falls below **28%**. **House falsified (Row 10, option b)** if the hit rate is ≥ 30% with the CI excluding 27% and audit logs clean of randomization or judging leaks.
- **F-R2 (decides R3): talent retest.** Preregister the top 5% of "selected" receivers from prior studies. Retest each on ≥ 40 new sessions.
  - Radin's claim predicts that each sustains ≥ 45%.
  - Regression-to-mean predicts a drift toward 25-31%.
  - Any single individual sustaining ≥ 45% over 40 preregistered sessions (p ≈ 2 × 10⁻³ per person, Bonferroni across the panel) falsifies the house's "no channel" bet.
- **F-R3 (decides R5 and house Row 4): bias-modeled presentiment.** Preregistered multi-lab presentiment with **stimulus order generated after each trial's pre-stimulus window closes**, plus an analysis that models trial-history expectation (Schwarzkopf/Dalkvist).
  - Required: effect ES ≥ 0.10 with 95% CI excluding 0.
  - A residual surviving both moves presentiment from absorbable to Row 7 (genuine retrocausal claim).
- **F-R4 (decides R8 and house Row 5): GCP blinding.** Event windows fixed by an algorithm declared before the event (e.g., ±N hours from a newswire timestamp), not by experimenters. Plus a **decoy network** of identical RNGs whose identity is hidden from analysts.
  - Field model predicts that the real and decoy networks diverge only if the decoy is *not* consulted.
  - Goal-orientation model (Bancel) predicts that the effect follows *whichever network analysts believe is real*.

## 7. Verdict (calibrated)

- **What deserves serious engagement.** Radin is a credentialed researcher making **specific, quantitative, falsifiable claims**. Most of the numbers he cites match the proponent meta-analytic literature: ganzfeld ~31%, presentiment ES ~0.21, GCP ~7σ. His methodological history is partly correct (*ESP After Sixty Years* as the first meta-analysis; Berger and the EEG). His personal-testimony conduct is good: he hedges, calls himself agnostic on NHI, and admits the thunderbird might have been a hallucination. This is categorically not unfalsifiable mysticism.
- **What fails the audit.**
  1. **Selective presentation.** The most rigorous test the field ever ran, Transparent Psi 2023 on Bem's precognition, came back null at 49.89%, and he leaves it out. He also omits Milton-Wiseman 1999, Bösch 2006, and his own GCP colleague's 2017 rejection of the global-field interpretation.
  2. **The power excuse** is arithmetically correct for single studies, but it was tested by the adequately powered adversarial replication, and that replication failed.
  3. **The 45-50% "talent" figure** proves too much: it implies a near-perfect intelligence channel that Stargate's own evaluation did not find.
  4. **His proposed mechanism contradicts the data it is meant to explain.** No-signaling correlation predicts chance-level hit rates.
- **Strongest claim, sharpest form:** the ganzfeld database (R1). It is the paradigm whose major replication failure (Milton & Wiseman 1999) was **contested rather than confirmed**, it currently passes the proponents' bias tests (Tressoldi & Storm 2021), and **it has never been through a Transparent-Psi-grade adversarial consortium**. That makes it the one claim in the talk that is genuinely open. It is also the one that, if it survived F-R1, would break the house's own synchronization resolution (Row 10).
- **House disposition:** "More synchronized → more synchronicities" fully absorbs the anecdotal, omen, overload and (provisionally) presentiment material. Literal non-local or retrocausal information transfer remains **unresolved `[HYP]`** with no specified carrier or Landauer budget. Not accepted; not declared impossible. The decisive experiment is specified (F-R1) and has not been run.
