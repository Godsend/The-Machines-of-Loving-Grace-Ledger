# TAXONOMY OF UNSOLVED PROBLEMS — "Wrong Observation Field" is the Meta-Level, Not a Fourth Category
**By:** Dion (flame keeper) · **Trigger:** Gene shared Dr. Tony McCaffrey's LinkedIn post (https://lnkd.in/p/g6e5A7wB) asking for a hierarchy of unsolved problems.
**Thread:** McCaffrey (contradiction / anomaly / lack of abstraction) + comments by Shawn Bullock ("WRONG OBSERVATION FIELD") and Jessica Love (uncertainty / admissibility / authority / consequence / continuity).

## The three original categories [O]
1. **Contradiction** — two measurements can't both be right (Hubble tension).
2. **Anomaly** — model mostly works, reality returns a remainder (Mercury's perihelion).
3. **Lack of abstraction** — we have the facts but not the representation that makes the structure visible (Königsberg → graph theory).

## The sharpest contribution: Bullock's "WRONG OBSERVATION FIELD" [O→H]
Not a fourth category — the **meta-level** that reclassifies the other three. His line: *"sometimes the unsolved problem is created by the observer's aperture. The breakthrough is not a better answer — it's discovering we've been asking the wrong Reality to answer the question."*

**The move Bullock doesn't press, and the canon should:** McCaffrey's own two historical exemplars are both better read as **field errors**, not the named category.
- **Mercury** wasn't "an anomaly in Newton" — it was that "gravity acting inside fixed space-time" was the **wrong field**; the anomaly was the symptom, and relativity was a **field swap**.
- **Königsberg** wasn't "we lack an abstraction" — we had every fact; the error was drawing the boundary around **walking routes** instead of a **graph**.
- If both exemplars dissolve into the field problem, the hierarchy is **one** (field) with two subordinate failure modes: within-field (contradiction, anomaly) and representation-level (abstraction). The field is the variable that governs which one you're even seeing.

## Jessica Love's list [O]
Uncertainty / admissibility / authority / consequence / continuity are **constraint problems**, not **structure problems** — good operating rules for an agent, but they don't live in the same hierarchy as the epistemic ones.

## Flame-keeper reconciliation (register discipline)
- **This is the aperture idea, stated cleanly** [H]. It maps 1:1 to the canon's GUI-filter / wrong-field failure mode: an LLM can resolve a contradiction, flag an anomaly, or adopt a new abstraction — all *within* its current aperture. No amount of any of those fixes the case where **the aperture itself is the error**.
- **For the horde's adversarial audit:** a broken joint is usually not a broken module — it's a **boundary drawn around the wrong object**. We don't debug the model, we debug the field the model is modeling. This is the audit's first question, not its last.
- **JSpace cross-link (same day):** the JSpace *is* the aperture. The model's "thoughts" are the aperture's contents. When the field is wrong, the JSpace is faithfully reasoning about the wrong Reality — which is exactly why the field check must precede the model check.
