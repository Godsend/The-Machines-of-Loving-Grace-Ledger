### ★ Friction rows (where they disagree)

**F1 · `[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]` — the arrow reverses at the point of depletion.**
EILT §4 ("Synapses Up") and §5 run the causal arrow **wave → ledger**: *"the wave carves the connections"*; mitochondria/bioelectric fields update the connectome; the ledger is explicitly the **inert** party (*"They do not compute; they constrain"*). *Consequence:* any house claim that inference/plasticity is the seat of agency and the ledger merely constrains must be qualified — the ledger is also the **fallback controller**, and it is the more robust of the two.

**F2 · `[CRITICAL DIVERGENCE]` (cuts at the house) — the cost model is the wrong currency.**
EILT's cost of a commit is **Landauer** — a per-irreversible-bit thermodynamic floor (`k_B T ln 2`, §7), independent of *content*. Decision fatigue's cost is a **neurochemical pool** (glutamate) with a **clearance-rate bottleneck** (`GLT1`) that accumulates with the **count and emotional stake** `[0:44:49]` of decisions and is discharged by **sleep/rest** `[0:04:38]`. These are different quantities: one is per-bit and content-blind; the other is per-decision, content-sensitive (emotional decisions deplete faster), and **cyclic** (cleared daily). **EILT has no homeostatic reset for the wave and no stake-weighting of the decision.** The physiology imposes exactly the two things the house's accounting lacks.

**F3 · `[DIVERGENCE]` — epiplexity changes sign across the day.**
EILT treats accumulated commits as **monotone-good capital** ("the ledger is the externalized connector," nirvana via more ingression). Decision fatigue says that **within a day, more decisions is strictly worse** until rest; the same variable flips sign over a single 24-hour cycle. EILT measures the *stock* of epiplexity; decision fatigue is about the *flow budget* that produces it — and the flow **cannot run all day**.
