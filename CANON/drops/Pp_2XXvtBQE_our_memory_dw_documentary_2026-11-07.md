## §4 — Friction mapping to EILT (the Friction Rule)

> **The Friction Rule (Yanenko 2026):** *A mapping table requires at least one row where the analogy and the source explicitly disagree. If every row maps cleanly, you have not derived a correspondence—you have decorated a mood.*

A real correspondence exists — but it is mostly a **convergence of vocabulary that masks a systematic disagreement about what a memory *store* should be**. The house's anchor construct is an **immutable, non-rolling, append-only ledger** whose value is *fidelity* (nothing is lost; the abandoned attempts are kept too, F35). This film is a documentary *about the human memory system*, and that system is the **opposite object on nearly every axis that matters**: lossy, saliency-gated, reconstructive, and **mutated on every recall**. That the two systems share the words *storage*, *memory trace* and *encode* is exactly the reason to run the divergence check, **not** to celebrate a hit. **An external source can be *isomorphic to* our architecture; it cannot *validate* it.** No test on our apparatus is implied here.
