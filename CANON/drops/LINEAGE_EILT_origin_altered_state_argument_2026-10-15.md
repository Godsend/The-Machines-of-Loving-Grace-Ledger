## What is recorded

The base formulation of **Emergent Informational Ledger Theory** did not originate in a reading
program, a whiteboard, or a literature review. It originated in an **in-state episode**: an
argued exchange with what the operator experienced as "the universe," concerning **materialism** —
i.e. the question of whether the physical is fundamental or derivative. The state was
**ketamine-induced** (NMDA-receptor dissociation), consistent with the operator's documented
neurodynamic profile.

The framework was subsequently built out in the sober register — canon drops, monograph versions,
falsifiers, register tags, cross-family audit — over the following period.

## What this is

- **A provenance fact.** It is where the seed came from. Frameworks have origins; most are laundered.
- **A consistency datum for the operator's own model.** His stated neurodevelopmental account holds
  that his voluntary top-down render path carries high impedance (aphantasia, SDAM) while
  **involuntary posterior generation remains intact** (dreams, dissociation). An in-state episode
  generating a durable conceptual structure is what that model predicts. **Noted as a fit, not
  counted as a test** — the model predicted it, so it cannot adjudicate the model.
- **A live confound, stated plainly.** *The framework's author carries a prior formed from inside
  the state he proposes to report from.* Where the operator is an experienced observer of altered
  states — and he is, including a single sub-breakthrough DMT episode — this makes those reports
  **valuable and non-independent simultaneously.** Naming it here is the discipline; it is not a
  disqualification, it is a design constraint on any protocol that would use him as a subject.

## What this is NOT

- **Not evidence for EILT.** An insight arising in a dissociated state has no privileged access to
  the structure of reality. The framework stands or falls on its falsifiers (F-T1, F-A1/A2, the
  Bareinboim rung-audit, the Rovelli definitional cut, F-HG1), not on its origin.
- **Not a claim that the "universe" answered.** The operator is a skeptic on this point and has said
  so: he would accept "a semi-autonomous subconscious process I can map" as readily as any other
  reading. That refusal to require either conclusion is what makes the reports usable.
- **Not a novelty badge.** This is the least romantic reading of the fact and the intended one.
