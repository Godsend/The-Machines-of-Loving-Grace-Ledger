## 8. Falsifiers

Formulated against the four construction traps (no vacuous substrate destruction; no strict-null; no conflated optimisation; no tautological divergence).

- **[F-G1, CORRECTED 2026-10-28, Oryon review] The delta must be measurable against a stated baseline, not against logical entailment.** Given complete deterministic semantics plus state/inputs/schedule, a substrate's trajectory is fixed by construction — "not implied by its source code" tests a determinism-failure reading (whether the house failed to anticipate the output), not Levin's actual claim; the original criterion was overstrong and is replaced. **Corrected test:** preregister a finite task battery, an explicit baseline predictor, an input distribution, an intervention, a resource budget, and a success criterion; measure *predictive surprise relative to that baseline* or out-of-task performance, not non-entailment. Any ensemble sample size must come from a specified effect size/distribution or a pilot — the original "≥30 substrates at 95% power" carried no effect size, variance, test, or dependence assumptions and is struck. An ensemble can estimate prevalence only in its *specified sampling measure*, not in an undefined "unbiased" space of all substrates; high prevalence alone does not identify nonphysical ingression, low prevalence alone does not establish selection. **This is now an optional house test, not a source-derived necessary test of every version of Levin's proposal — it is still the cheapest test available and still has not been run.**
- **[F-G2] Ingression must predict, ex ante.** *"you need to be paying attention"* `[34:5x]` is a post-hoc discovery procedure. Falsified (as a *mechanism*) if a **pre-registered** prediction of *which* competency a novel construct will show — derived from a stated structured map of the space, and registered **before** the experiment — fails in the majority of trials. A discovery procedure that only ever classifies after the fact cannot be told apart from "we looked at the output and were surprised."
- **[F-G3] Write-back must be shown, not bet.** Levin's own words: *"we haven't proven that you can write information into that space but I bet you can"* `[57:54]`. Falsified for the 2026-10-28 statement if the announced experiments return negative **and** no positive write-back demonstration (a physically-unknowable-to-physically-performed change in a pattern's behaviour, traceable to a deliberate physical action) appears within the announced window. **Note the continuity issue the house must track:** on **2026-09-24** (Hewitt) he said data was *coming in a few months*; on **2026-10-20** (632nm) the status was still *"no evidence yet"*; on **2026-10-28** the status is *still* *"haven't proven … but I bet you can."* **Three statements across five weeks with no data crossing the line.** That is not a contradiction; it is a **watch item with a rising cost**, and it should be logged as such rather than re-each time as "promising."
- **[F-G4, CORRECTED 2026-10-28, Oryon review] The "not genetic" inference is already insufficient as an argument, independent of any future falsifier.** §C18's move (*"and and if that's not in the genes, this, you know, this this other stuff, there's no reason to think that it is"*) rides the planarian case, where one genome sequence supports two anatomical outcomes — that defeats *sequence-alone determination of that specific outcome under those conditions*; it does not establish "not genetic at all" for a different domain (extraordinary human ability). **Wording fix:** the original falsifier asked for a "demonstrated dissociation between genotype and ability... shown to track a specific genomic/chromatin change" — but *tracking* is covariation, not *dissociation*; the two point in opposite directions and cannot be the same falsifying observation. A genuine falsifier needs a **replicated causal genomic intervention** affecting a defined ability; mere covariation is weaker than that and would still not settle the metaphysics either way. §C18's original caveat already recognised much of this gap and should be retained as the load-bearing text here.
- **[F-G5] Axiom-hunting must cut both ways.** Levin's Turing example is *"we have these um uh uh buried uh unstated axioms"* `[59:3x]`. **Apply his own test to the Platonic-space framing:** state the axiom that would be falsified by a purely physicalist account of the same bubble-sort/xenobot observations.
